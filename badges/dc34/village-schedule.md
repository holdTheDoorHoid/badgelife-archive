# DEF CON 34 Badgelife Village schedule

<script src="{{ '/assets/js/schedule-fit.js' | relative_url }}" defer></script>

Preserved from the badge.life home page as it stood in August 2026: talks, badge drops (with the makers' storefront links) and volunteer shifts for the Badgelife Village at DEF CON 34. Every badge and SAO dropped here should have an entry under [DC34]({{ site.baseurl }}/badges/dc34/).

All events listed here take place in the Badgelife Village. Most will be at our "silent disco" type stage setup. Please be sure to thank our 2026 Village Sponsors below! They really came through last year and are helping us fund this year as well!
