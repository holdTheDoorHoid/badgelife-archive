The EFF Certbot SAO was listed on the DEF CON 31 (2023) community badge sheet as a $30 solder-on add-on themed around Certbot, the Electronic Frontier Foundation's free-certificate tool for HTTPS websites. The sheet's only link was a t.co shortlink pointing to an Eventbrite listing under the `privacybadger.eventbrite.com` subdomain, which suggests it may have been tied to an EFF Privacy Badger-related sale or event rather than sold on its own — but that listing is gone, and no other source (search engines, EFF's own shop and site search, social media, or the Wayback Machine) turned up a cached copy or independent confirmation of who made it or what it looked like.

This entry is likely distinct from the separately catalogued `dc31-certbot-sao` (maker "H4rl0d"), which appeared on the same community sheet under a different row and link; a prior research pass on that entry confirmed the two are not duplicates of each other, though neither has enough surviving detail to say definitively how the two Certbot-themed items differed.

Because the original source is unrecoverable, no maker, chip, LED, or image details can be confirmed, and this entry remains largely as it was imported from the sheet.
