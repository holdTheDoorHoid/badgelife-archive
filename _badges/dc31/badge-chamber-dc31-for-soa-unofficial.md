This is not a badge or SAO but an unofficial 3D-printable test-fit tool: a rough STL export of the internal mesh geometry of the official DEF CON 31 badge's SAO ("Shitty Add-On") chamber, uploaded to Thingiverse by user skippymcneal. It let other badge makers print a stand-in for the DC31 add-on socket and test-fit their own SAO designs against it before the official badge was in attendees' hands.

The maker describes it as "a rough export and done quickly," warns that the mesh needs to be scaled by 10,000% in a slicer to come out at the right size, and jokingly cautions not to make "$9001 SOAs for your friends" from it — framing this as a quick community convenience file rather than a polished or authoritative reference. It carries no price, chip, LEDs, or display, since it is a free CAD file rather than a physical product; there is nothing in this record to correct toward a purchasable badge or SAO entry.
