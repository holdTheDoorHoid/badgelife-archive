The DEF CON 31 (2023) community badge sheet lists "TechGirl House of Blinky" as a maker present that year, with no further detail on what they brought. TechGirl House of Blinky is the Tindie storefront run by TechGirlMN (Rochester, MN), who is documented elsewhere in this archive making the 2022 "L0nely Hackers Triple Threat" badge for DEF CON 30 and, later, Diana Initiative-affiliated SAOs for DEF CON 32 and 33.

No DEF CON 31-specific product could be confirmed. The maker's Tindie store renders its product listings via client-side JavaScript, which defeated both a live fetch and the nearest Wayback Machine snapshot (December 2023); no Hackaday.io project, press coverage, or social post naming a 2023 item turned up either. It is possible the maker simply attended or vended existing products (such as the Triple Threat or the older Sparkle Invader) rather than releasing something new for DC31, but that is speculation, not a confirmed fact, so it is not recorded in the structured fields.
