A pin-style Joker badge made by Abhinav Panda, who designs hardware under the
Hackerware.io studio name and is credited elsewhere in this archive for several
DEF CON 32 and DEF CON 34 badges (AI Village, Arcanum, Bug Bounty Village, and
others). The maker announced it on Twitter/X in the run-up to DEF CON 31 (2023),
saying it would be available at the HackerWarehouse booth during Hacker Summer
Camp, tagged with the community `#badgelife` hashtag.

The announcement's wording, "Joker meets DEF CON Jack again," reads as a callback
to an earlier appearance of the character, but no source confirming a prior-year
version was found. No dedicated project page, storefront listing, schematic, or
technical write-up turned up for this specific pin, so its construction (chip,
LEDs, display, if any) and how many were made remain unconfirmed.
