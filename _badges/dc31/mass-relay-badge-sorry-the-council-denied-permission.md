DCEnterprise listed a Mass Effect-themed "Mass Relay" badge for DEF CON 31, planned as a limited run of 100 units at a $100 price point. Before the con, the maker announced on the community sheet that manufacturing permission had been denied - phrased as a joke in Mass Effect's own lore terms ("the Council denied permission") - and that the badge would not ship for DC31, with a note that another attempt might be made the following year.

No maker page, storefront listing, project repository, or press coverage for this badge could be located. The single link included with the sheet entry is a shortened t.co URL that redirects to an unrelated YouTube video referencing the same "hanging up on the Council" joke, rather than any documentation of the physical badge, so no technical, visual, or distribution details beyond the sheet's own text could be confirmed.
