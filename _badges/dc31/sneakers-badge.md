The Sneaky Badge is Hacker Warehouse's tribute to the 1992 hacker heist film
"Sneakers," built around a Raspberry Pi Pico with an OLED screen. Rather than
just displaying a static homage, the badge is a puzzle device: it has 14 slots
that hold custom, plug-in scrabble-style letter tiles (56 tiles ship with the
badge), and its firmware runs word-game puzzle challenges that use those
letters. It was sold through Hacker Warehouse's online store and at their
booth at DEF CON 31 for $120, alongside their other badgelife offerings that
year.

The community badge sheet that seeded this entry listed the item as "Sneakers
Badge" by maker "Gigs" at a quoted $140, which differs slightly from the
storefront's name and price. The Hacker Warehouse SKU for the product, "GS-SB,"
lines up with a maker-initials pattern this archive has seen elsewhere in
their catalog (their Tipsy Badge, made by "seeess," carries SKU "SS-TB"),
which is consistent with "Gigs" as the designer even though the product page
itself does not credit a maker by name.

As of the most recent check, the badge is listed as out of stock. No separate
maker page, GitHub repo, or press coverage was found, so details like LED
count, connectivity, and battery/power are not recorded here.
