The community sheet for DEF CON 31 (2023) has only a single line for this maker: "Untitled Electronics (creator of 0xCafebabe Badge)," with a note that a badge had been announced but no further details, title, image, or link. The parenthetical "creator of 0xCafebabe Badge" appears to be how the sheet's contributor identified this maker (presumably by an earlier or separate product called the "0xCafebabe Badge"), rather than the name of the 2023 item itself — but no record of a "0xCafebabe Badge" could be found on the maker's own channels or elsewhere.

Untitled Electronics is a real, ongoing badge/SAO maker (untitledelec.com), and the same name (with the maker's Discord handle @wrickert) later produced the NeoSword, a sword-shaped SAO totem sold at DEF CON 33 in 2025 (see the separate `dc33-neosword` entry). What, if anything, they actually released at DEF CON 31 in 2023 is not documented anywhere found in this pass; the storefront's current catalog starts with the 2025 items, and archive/search tools needed to check for a 2023 snapshot were unavailable or blocked during research.
