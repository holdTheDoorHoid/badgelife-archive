At DEF CON 31 in 2023, a set of small acrylic "sign" style badge addons was sold for $40, according to the community badge-tracking sheet. The sheet lists no maker name directly, only a note that buyers would need to track down someone known as "True" in person to get one — a common arrangement for informal badgelife sellers who do not run a storefront.

The badgelife maker who goes by "true" (trueControl) runs a documentation site, basic.truecontrol.org, cataloguing their DEF CON and Supercon projects going back to DC27. That site confirms trueControl made a "Mini Acrylic Sign Addon" for DC30 (2022) and had at least two other projects at DC31 (an "Addon Addon" shard for the official badge, and a "Pirate Puzzle Badge"), which makes them a strong candidate for the "True" named on the sheet. However, the site's own DC31 listing does not include an acrylic sign item, and no page, photo, or further specification for this particular DC31 listing could be located, so the identification is offered here as a likely lead rather than a confirmed match.
