The Cannabadge is a cannabis-themed wearable PCB badge made by Rex's Lab for DEF CON 31 (2023), sold through the maker's Tindie store under the username rex212. It packs 100 RGB LEDs for decorative lighting effects, an ESP32 for control and mesh networking between badges, a flowmeter that roughly estimates how much smoke the wearer has exhaled, and an air quality sensor. The maker built it "for fun at Hacker Summercamp" and describes it as being made "in remembrance of Cannacon," the now-defunct cannabis industry conference.

The badge was sold for $134.20 and was explicitly hand-delivery-only at DEF CON, with the listing noting orders placed afterward would ship separately; it went out of stock on July 29, 2023 and has not been restocked since. The community sheet that seeded this entry credited the maker as "Hotboxed," while the Tindie listing itself credits "Rex's Lab" — both are kept here since they may refer to the same person under different handles. A similarly named "CannaBadge" from the same maker later appears at DEF CON 32, suggesting this was the first entry in a small recurring line rather than a one-off.
