Peppy is a small cat-shaped simple add-on (SAO) made by Th0m4s (@___th0m4s___, also th0m4s@defcon.social) for DEF CON 31 in 2023. The board is a green PCB cut into a sitting-cat silhouette, with red LEDs positioned so they shine through cutout eyes in a black overlay — the maker's own photo shows two units side by side, one lit and one dark, to show off the effect.

The community badge sheet lists it at $5 with no image; the maker's tweet, captioned simply "Peppy SAO #badgelife" and posted July 24, 2023, is the only public record of the piece. Distribution appears to have been informal and in person: the maker's sheet listing notes that people needed to track down "th0m4s" or "@im_eningeer" at the con to get one, with no separate storefront or preorder found. No chip, exact LED part, firmware, or hardware files for Peppy turned up in the maker's linked blog or GitHub accounts, so those fields are left blank rather than guessed.
