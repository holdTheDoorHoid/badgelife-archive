DC801 has a history of yearly party badges (DC24 through DC27 each have a dedicated hardware/firmware repo on GitHub), and by 2023 they were also maintaining an ongoing hardware platform, BM-Badge, described in its README as "the DC801 Badge Platform for DC28+" (Nordic nRF52840, 2.4" touch TFT, KiCad design files). Given the sheet's original description — "listed, no details" — and the lack of any DC31-specific project found, this entry is left with an unknown status rather than guessing whether DC801 handed out a new badge, reused the BM-Badge platform, or brought nothing at all that year.
