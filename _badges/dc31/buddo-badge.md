The Buddobot Badge is Hackerware.io's first badge combining acrylic, UV printing, and PCB fabrication, designed by Abhinav Pandagale for BuddoBot, a security research and red-teaming outfit associated with Jason Haddix. Hackerware and BuddoBot promoted it on social media around DEF CON 31 (2023) under the hashtag #buddobelieveit, and Hackster.io hosts the maker's own project writeup and photos.

Little of the badge's electronics or distribution have surfaced in public sources: no page found states its chip, LEDs, display, price, or how many were made, and it is unclear whether it was physically handed out on the DEF CON 31 show floor or distributed by BuddoBot through its own channels around the same time. It should not be confused with its 2024 successor, the Buddobot OLED Badge, an ATmega32A-based capture-the-flag badge Hackerware built for the BSidesSF Hardware Challenge Village.
