The Diana Initiative, a diversity-focused infosec conference held during DEF CON week in Las Vegas, sold a "2023 DIY Electronic Badge Kit" as a $55.20 add-on when registering for its 2023 event (August 7, 2023, at the Westin Las Vegas) via Eventbrite. Attendees who bought the kit could build it at the event's dedicated badge soldering and maker village. An archived snapshot of the registration page from June 2023 shows 46 kits still available, which had dropped to 29 by the time the community badge sheet recorded this listing in mid-July 2023 — consistent with the kit selling steadily in the run-up to the con.

This appears to be an annual tradition for the organization: the same Eventbrite page also listed a "2022 DIY Electronic Badge Kit - arcade cabinet" from the prior year, suggesting each year's kit carries its own theme. The community sheet names this year's badge "Pair-o-Dice," which was not found verbatim on any archived Diana Initiative or Eventbrite page checked for this entry, though the pun fits a Las Vegas/casino theme (the Strip sits in the unincorporated township of Paradise, NV). No photos, schematics, or component details (MCU, LEDs, display) for the badge were located, and the live Eventbrite page today shows only the expired event listing with none of the original ticket-tier detail.
