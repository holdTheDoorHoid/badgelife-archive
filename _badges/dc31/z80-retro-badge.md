Uberfoo Heavy Industries — the Shopify storefront and GitHub handle of hardware hacker uberFoo (Keith Star) — sold a Z80 Retro Badge for $130 through their own online shop (shop.uberfoo.net) around DEF CON 31 (2023). The badge's shortlink from the community sheet (t.co/mCz5WVFIDH) redirects to that store's product page, confirming the maker and the price, though the live product listing has since gone offline (the store itself currently returns "This store is unavailable").

An archived snapshot of the storefront from March 2024 shows the badge marked "Sold out" at $130.00, alongside a product photo (filed as flyer-1080.png) that unfortunately was never captured by the Wayback Machine, so no image could be recovered for this entry. No further technical details — microcontroller, LEDs, display, or design files — were found in any surviving source, so those fields are left blank.

The same maker also listed a "Z80 Retro Badge" for DEF CON 30 (2022) at $140, sold at the Hacker Warehouse booth rather than online. Whether the DC31 badge is a straightforward rerelease of that design or a distinct revision is not established by available sources.
