The Embedded Systems Village (ESV) sold this badge at its village during DEF CON 31 (2023, the Linq). It is shaped like an old-fashioned skeleton key: the bow of the key carries a stylized skull with LED eyes, ringed by a circuit-trace border and the words "EMBEDDED SYSTEMS VILLAGE," while the shaft is silkscreened "ESV" and lit by a row of red LEDs. Beyond being a wearable, the badge doubles as a functional tool: ESV's announcement described it as a USB-to-serial debugger, useful for the hardware-hacking work the village runs.

The badge was tied into ESV's on-site CTF, gating three challenges for people working through the village's Embedded CTF. According to the community sheet it sold for $60, and scoring well enough in the CTF also earned a badge for free — a "play to win one" distribution alongside the straight sale.

No hardware or firmware files, chip details, or a "how many were made" figure turned up in what's still available online; ESV's current Shopify storefront no longer lists it and no snapshot from 2023 survives in the Wayback Machine, so those fields are left blank rather than guessed.
