The DEF CON 31 (2023) community badge sheet recorded only a maker name, "Jolly
Roger," for this row, with no badge title, price, or other detail. No further
research could pin down what was actually sold or given away that year.

"Jolly Roger" appears to be an earlier handle for the same maker/operation
that later branded itself JollyBadge, run under the Twitter/X handle
@GetJollyBadge and the site getjollybadge.com. The archive already holds a
DC30 badge credited to "Jolly Roger" (dc30-jollybadge), a DC32 row credited to
"JollyBadge" with an unreleased name (dc32-name-not-released-as-of-yet), and a
DC33 release documented in detail as "JollyBadge [V2] - The Final Batch"
(maker Steve Jabs of Jolly Media Group LLC). None of the sources checked for
this entry -- the maker's current site, their GitHub repository, and the
Wayback Machine -- name a specific DC31 product or confirm that a badge was
actually released that year rather than just listed on the community sheet.
