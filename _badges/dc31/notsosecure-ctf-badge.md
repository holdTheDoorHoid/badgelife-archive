This entry was listed on the DC31 (2023) community badge sheet as a CTF badge tied to
NotSoSecure, the penetration-testing and training arm of Claranet Cyber Security, with a
link pointing to Claranet's Black Hat page and a note that the badge was earned rather
than bought. The maker credit on the sheet, Abhinav SP / Hackerware.io, points to a real
and prolific badge-design studio: Hackerware.io has made custom conference badges for
RSA Conference, HITB, F5, and several DEF CON villages, and the same maker went on to
build a long run of later DEF CON badges (see the DC32-DC34 entries in this archive),
so the attribution is plausible on its face.

Beyond that, no independent record of this specific badge could be found. The sheet's
own link now 301-redirects to Claranet's current Black Hat landing page, which carries
no trace of a 2023 DEF CON 31 CTF or badge program, and no maker page, Hackaday.io
project, press coverage, or storefront listing turned up describing its chip, LEDs,
look, or how many were made. Rather than guess, those fields are left blank; a check of
the Wayback Machine for the old claranetcybersecurity.com/blackhat page, or a direct
outreach to Hackerware.io, would be the next step if this needs firming up.
