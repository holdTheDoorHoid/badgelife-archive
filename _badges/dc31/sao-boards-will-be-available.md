The Black Badge Raffle is a recurring, informal DEF CON tradition: an unofficial raffle offering a chance to win a black badge from a past year, run off a Twitter/X account (@DefconRaffle) rather than a company storefront. For DEF CON 31 (2023), the organizers listed a $5 SAO board as part of that year's offering, continuing a pattern seen the year before at DC30, where a similarly priced SAO accompanied the raffle. Alongside the new SAO, the same listing mentions a small leftover stock of DC30 Black Badge Raffle badges being resold for $100 while supplies lasted.

No maker website, GitHub, or Hackaday.io project could be found for this line, and the Twitter/X account itself is not fetchable without a login and has no archived snapshot from 2023, so technical details of the DC31 SAO (chip, LEDs, board art, exact quantity) are not confirmed. What's recorded here reflects only what the community sheet captured at the time: the price, the sales channel, and the note about the DC30 badge resale.
