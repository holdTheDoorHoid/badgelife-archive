The Birds Aren't Real broSAO is a small blue, bird-shaped SAO (Simple Add-On) made by the Anti Hacker Hacker Club, a San Antonio-based badgelife maker, and sold on Tindie starting in mid-2023. It plays on the "birds aren't real" internet conspiracy meme: a bird-shaped board with a camera-styled head and a single red LED that blinks, nodding to the joke that birds are secretly government surveillance drones.

The board uses the standard 2x3 SAO connector so it can draw power from a host badge, or run on its own from an onboard CR1220 coin cell with an on/off switch. It was sold both as a kit (bird board, resistor, battery holder, CR1220 cell, SAO connector, and switch for the buyer to assemble) and as a pre-built unit, at $15. As of this check the Tindie listing shows the seller "taking a break," so the item is not currently orderable.

No hardware files, firmware, GitHub repo, or independent press coverage turned up for this SAO; everything here is drawn from the maker's own Tindie listing.
