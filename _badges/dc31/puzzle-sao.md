The Puzzle SAO was one of three badge items BlackGirlsHack brought to DEF CON 31 in 2023, priced at $25. According to the community sheet entry, it was meant to go on sale early in con week alongside a companion "Blinky Badge," while a third item, a "Mystery Badge," was to be announced only once the con was underway. Buyers were pointed to BlackGirlsHack's and organizer @R_Rich22's Twitter/X accounts for drop-location announcements.

No maker page, storefront listing, or photo of the Puzzle SAO itself could be located during research, so specifics such as its chip, LED count, artwork, and how many were made remain unknown. It is treated here as a distinct SAO rather than an SAO bundled with the Blinky Badge, since the sheet lists a single combined price without breaking out per-item cost or confirming whether they shipped as one unit.
