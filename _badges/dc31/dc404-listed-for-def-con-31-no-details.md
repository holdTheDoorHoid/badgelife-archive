DC404 is the Atlanta-based DEF CON group (District Caucus 404), and this entry comes from a
community badge-tracking sheet that recorded them as present at DEF CON 31 (2023) without
capturing what, if anything, they brought as a badge or SAO that year.

DC404 has a history of badge-adjacent projects — a "Shitty Add-on for DefCon 27" (2019) and a
recurring "Learn to Solder" training badge (a 2021/2022-dated version is covered separately as
`dc30-dc404-training-badge`) — but no source found during this research pass ties a specific
badge or SAO to DEF CON 31 itself. The group's own site and GitHub organization show no post or
repository from that year. Absent further evidence, this entry is left as unknown rather than
assuming it was a repeat of the training badge.
