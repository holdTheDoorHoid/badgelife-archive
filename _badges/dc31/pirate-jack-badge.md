The Pirate Jack Badge was technick's DEF CON 31 (2023) entry in an annual series of 3D-printed "Jack" wearable badges, following King Jack Badge the year before and preceding AI Jack Badge at DEF CON 33. Unlike many badgelife pieces, it is a passive wearable rather than an electronic badge: no listing, photo, or description mentions a microcontroller, LEDs, battery, or SAO header. It shipped as a 3D-printed pirate-themed body with a customizable DEF CON 31 nameplate (up to 8-10 characters), a lanyard loop, stickers, and "random loot while supplies lasted," and was offered in six color, hat, and patch combinations.

It sold on Tindie for $25 each ($22 at 10+), starting around late July 2023, and sold out by August 22, 2023. The community badge sheet recorded the maker's forum handle as "NiCk"; technick's own Tindie storefront and later-year listings for the same "Jack" series use the handle "technick," which is used here as the maker name.
