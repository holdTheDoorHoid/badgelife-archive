DC540 is a DEF CON group (originally formed in Fredericksburg, Virginia in 2018, now based near Washington Dulles) that runs regular meetups and has since become known for the RP2040-based "Chakra Badge" it produced for DEF CON 32 in 2024. The community badge sheet for DEF CON 31 (2023) lists DC540 as a row with no descriptive details, dated to a sheet update in February 2023 -- well before that August's convention.

No corroborating record of a DC540 badge or SAO at DEF CON 31 could be found: the group's own blog has no posts at all in the June-through-September 2023 window (no con recap, announcement, or after-action post), and general web searches for the group and "DEF CON 31" turned up nothing usable. DC540's earliest documented electronic badge is the following year's Chakra Badge, which is a separate archive entry. It is possible the 2023 sheet entry reflects an early intention to bring something that never became a real, documented item, or refers to something outside DC540's own web presence; either way, there is not enough to say what (if anything) it was.
