Hackerware's Astronaut badge is a full-colour, UV-printed capture-the-flag badge built around a serial-port puzzle game: connect it over micro-USB, open the Arduino IDE serial monitor at 9600 baud, and solve five progressive puzzles by sending the right flag text. Each correct answer permanently lights up another part of the badge's space-mission artwork, and a slide switch gives a three-second sneak preview of the fully-lit design. It was Hackerware's first badge to use their full-colour UV-printing process, and the maker published a companion PDF ("Astronaut Badge CTF Interfacing") along with a Hackster.io write-up of the build.

The maker's own site frames this as the badge built for Badge Village at BSides SF 2023, not DEF CON — nothing on hackerwares.in mentions DEF CON 31, an SAO, or an "alien abduction" theme. The community badge sheet that seeded this entry lists it under DC31 at $70, sold at the Hackerware booth at DEFCON, which suggests the same badge (or a variant of it) was also offered for sale at DEF CON 31 that year, but no DEF CON-specific or alien-abduction-SAO source could be found to confirm the details beyond what the sheet recorded.

## Notes merged from the duplicate entry "The Astronaut CTF Badge"

Hackerware's Astronaut badge is a full-colour, UV-printed capture-the-flag badge that Abhinav Pandagale (Abhinav SP) built for the Badge Village at BSides SF 2023, themed to that year's con theme, "Putting The Cyber In Space." Players connect the badge over micro-USB, open the Arduino IDE serial monitor at 9600 baud, and solve five progressive puzzles by sending the correct flag text; each correct answer permanently lights up another part of the badge's space-mission artwork, and a slide switch gives a three-second sneak preview of the fully-lit design. It was Hackerware's first badge to use their full-colour UV-printing process, and the maker published a companion "Astronaut Badge CTF Interfacing" write-up.

It sold for $50, fully assembled, with a bonus "Alien Abduction" SAO included, pickup-only at the BSidesSF 2023 Badge Village; the storefront listing is now marked out of stock. No MCU, LED count, or production quantity is stated anywhere the maker has published.

This same badge (matched by its distinctive bundled "Alien Abduction" SAO) also appears in this archive under DC31, sourced from a community sheet that lists it selling again at $70 from the Hackerware booth at DEF CON 31 — likely the same design resold at a second con that year rather than a separate item.
