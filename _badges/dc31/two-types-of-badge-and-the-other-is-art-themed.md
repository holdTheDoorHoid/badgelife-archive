This is the second half of a pair of DEF CON 31 (2023) badges made by
GothCon, the crew behind the con's long-running, DEF-CON-unaffiliated
goth-themed dance party. The community badge sheet lists it immediately
after a sibling row ("Two types of badge. One is blinky...", maker
GothCon, $20) with identical acquisition instructions, so the two rows
together describe one GothCon offering with two variants that year: one
blinky (LED), and this one, "art themed" — implying a non-illuminated,
purely visual/artistic design.

Distribution was a scavenger hunt rather than a sale: GothCon would tweet a
`#badgedrop` from their account (@dcgothcon), and attendees had to track
down and "spot the Goth" in person to receive one — "You must find them in
order to acquire. Gotta spot the Goth!!!" Unlike the sibling row's listed
$20 price, this row's price is recorded as $0.00 on the sheet, which may
indicate this variant was a free giveaway rather than sold, though that is
not independently confirmed.

could be reached during this research pass — GothCon's X/Twitter profile
returned an HTTP 402 error, general web search was unavailable, and no
usable search-engine results turned up for the sheet's distinctive phrasing.
As a result, the badge's appearance, materials, and exact quantity remain
