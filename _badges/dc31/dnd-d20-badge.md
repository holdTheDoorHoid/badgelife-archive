The DnD D20 Badge is a Dungeons & Dragons-themed electronic dice roller built by KernelKrash and shown on Twitter/X ahead of DEF CON 31 (2023). The wearer selects which die to simulate — d20, d12, d10, d8, d6, or d4 — and then physically "rolls" the badge; a built-in tilt switch detects the motion and the badge reports a result, mimicking an actual dice roll. The maker's post tags it with #badgelife and #DEFCON31 and references the D&D at DEF CON community (@dungeonsatdefc1), suggesting it was made for or around that group's activities at the con.

Beyond the single tweet and its photo, no maker page, GitHub repository, storefront listing, or press coverage was found, so hardware details (MCU, LED type, power source), pricing, quantity made, and distribution method remain unconfirmed and are left blank.
