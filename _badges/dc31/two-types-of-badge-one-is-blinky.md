GothCon is the crew behind the long-running, DEF-CON-unaffiliated goth-themed dance party held during the convention; in other years they are confirmed to fund the party by selling badges through their own Shopify storefront and handing some out in person. This DEF CON 31 (2023) entry, titled "Two types of badge. One is blinky...", comes from the community badge sheet and implies GothCon offered two variants that year, only one of which lit up — a pattern that recurs the following year at DEF CON 32, where GothCon separately sold an LED "Blinky Badge" alongside a non-illuminated "Standard Art Badge."

The sheet lists a $20 price and describes acquisition as a scavenger hunt: rather than a storefront sale, GothCon would tweet out a `#badgedrop` from their account (@dcgothcon) and attendees had to track down a Goth in person to get one — "You must find them in order to acquire. Gotta spot the Goth!!!"
