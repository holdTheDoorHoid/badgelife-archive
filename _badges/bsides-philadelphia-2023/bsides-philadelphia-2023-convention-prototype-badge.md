This is a proof-of-concept PCB badge Garrett Fails designed and had fabricated ahead of BSides Philadelphia 2023, posted to PCBWay's project-share gallery in October 2023. Fails describes it as validating "a unique conference badge idea" that was "highly unusual but worked," using the prototype run to catch small feature-placement issues before committing to a production version for the event.

The PCBWay listing is a fabrication share rather than a product page: it confirms the board is a 2-layer, 1.6mm PCB and praises the silkscreen quality and board-to-board consistency, but it gives no microcontroller, LED, display, power, pricing, or quantity information, and no design files are linked. No independent coverage (Hackaday, storefronts, social posts) of this specific badge could be found, so it remains documented mainly through the maker's own share page.
