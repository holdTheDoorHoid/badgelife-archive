The LPV minibadge was one of nine official SAINTCON 2017 conference minibadges, made to represent that year's Lock Pick Village (LPV). It is documented only by name on SAINTCON's own 2017 badge documentation site, which lists it fourth among the nine ("X - Jupiter," "A - Luke," "HHV - Protoboard," "LPV - Lock pick," "Vault - Key hole," "Speaker," "TFHT - Alien Head," "Staff - Block S," "Hackers Challenge").

No maker credit, description, technical specifications, price, quantity, distribution details, or photos could be found for this specific minibadge, either on SAINTCON's site or elsewhere online. SAINTCON's minibadge tradition predates the community-run minibadge.wiki catalog, which only covers 2021 onward, so a photographic or maker record of the 2017 lineup may simply not exist online.
