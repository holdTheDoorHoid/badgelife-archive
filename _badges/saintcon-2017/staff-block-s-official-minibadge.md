The "Staff - Block S" minibadge was one of nine official minibadges issued as part of the SAINTCON 2017 conference badge program, documented alongside the main Raspberry Pi-based conference badge on the event's official GitLab Pages badge site. Unlike the "unofficial" minibadges on the same site, which were designed and produced by individual community members (DC801, Bashninja, and others), the official minibadges were issued by SAINTCON itself. Its "Staff" name suggests it marked conference staff or volunteers, though no source describes what the "Block S" design actually depicts.

Beyond its listing as an official minibadge, the source site gives no further detail: no photo, color scheme, dimensions, technical features, price, or production quantity are documented for it specifically, and no distribution channel could be confirmed beyond what the name itself implies.
