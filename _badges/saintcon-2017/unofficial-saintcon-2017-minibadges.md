This page is not a badge or SAO. It is the "Unofficial Mini-badges" index page from the official SAINTCON 2017 badge documentation site (a companion to the con's real, official minibadge page), listing eight unofficial minibadges that community members brought to the con that year: Weber State Shield-A, HITB - Various (compukidmike), DC801 Sheep - Hampster (DC801), 8x8 Matrix (Compukidmike), UVU Cyber Security Club (Bashninja), LDS Hacker (Bashninja), 801Labs (Bashninja), and Psychoholics. The page gives no technical details, pricing, or photos beyond a bare list with a few outbound links.

Two of the eight - the DC801 Sheep-Hampster minibadge and the HITB minibadge - already have their own entries in this archive. The remaining six (Weber State Shield-A, 8x8 Matrix, UVU Cyber Security Club, LDS Hacker, 801Labs, and Psychoholics) do not appear to be documented yet and are reported below as candidates for their own entries.
