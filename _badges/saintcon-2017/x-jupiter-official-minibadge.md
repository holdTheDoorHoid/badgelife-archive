"X - Jupiter" was one of nine official minibadges issued as part of the SAINTCON 2017 conference badge program, alongside minibadges named A - Luke, HHV - Protoboard, LPV - Lock pick, Vault - Key hole, Speaker, TFHT - Alien Head, Staff - Block S, and Hackers Challenge. It is documented only by name on the event's official badge-assembly site, which lists it first among the nine but gives no description, maker credit, or specifications for any of them.

The 2017 SAINTCON badge (and by extension its minibadges) implemented the community MiniBadge hardware specification maintained by lukejenkins, which standardized how minibadges plug into a host badge. Beyond that mechanical context, no source located during this research describes what "X - Jupiter" looked like, who designed it, or what (if anything) it did electronically - it may have been a simple passive board, consistent with several other minibadges of that era.

No maker could be confirmed. A community figure using the handle "Jup1t3r" is documented working on SAINTCON minibadges from 2022 onward (a Healthcare Community minibadge in 2022, co-running the minibadge trading booth in 2024), but nothing ties that person to this 2017 item, so the maker field is left blank rather than assumed.
