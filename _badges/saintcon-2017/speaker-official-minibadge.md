The Speaker minibadge was one of nine official minibadges produced by SAINTCON for its 2017 conference, alongside others themed around Jupiter, Luke, a protoboard, lock picking, a vault, an alien head, staff, and the Hackers Challenge. SAINTCON's own documentation names it only in that list, with no detail on how it was distributed; the name suggests a connection to conference speakers, but no source confirms that or says it was free versus sold.

Like the other official minibadges of that era, it almost certainly followed the community MiniBadge hardware/connector specification (maintained in the `lukejenkins/minibadge` repository) that the main 2017 SAINTCON badge supported, but no design files, photos, or component specs specific to the Speaker badge itself turned up in research — SAINTCON's own documentation names it only in a bulleted list with no further detail.
