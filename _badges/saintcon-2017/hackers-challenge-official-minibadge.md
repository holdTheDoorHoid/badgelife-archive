One of nine official minibadges issued at SAINTCON 2017, named for the con's "Hackers Challenge" — the event's long-running Jeopardy-style capture-the-flag contest. It is listed alongside eight other official 2017 minibadges (Jupiter, Luke, Protoboard, Lock pick, Key hole, Speaker, Alien Head, and Block S) in the badge team's own documentation, but that listing gives only the name, with no photo, maker credit, or technical specification.

SAINTCON's minibadges generally follow a small pin-header add-on format documented in the community `minibadge` hardware spec (maintained by Luke Jenkins and contributors), which the 2017 badge site links to for assembly purposes. Whether this particular design uses that connector, and what its chip, LEDs, or artwork look like, could not be confirmed from any source found. No indication was found of price, production quantity, or exactly how attendees obtained it (e.g., for participating in vs. completing the Hackers Challenge).
