The HHV - Protoboard is one of nine official minibadges issued for SAINTCON 2017, the annual security conference in Utah. It is listed by name on the conference's own badge documentation site alongside other 2017 minibadges such as "X - Jupiter," "LPV - Lock pick," and "Staff - Block S," but that listing gives no further detail: no maker credit, photo, technical specs, price, or production count survive online for it.

The "HHV" prefix most likely refers to SAINTCON's Hardware Hacking Village, and the protoboard theme fits a village that centers on hands-on soldering and hardware assembly, but no source explicitly credits a designer or confirms that attribution, so it is not stated as fact here. A search of the SAINTCON Hardware Hacking Village's GitHub org, a community-run repository of shared SAINTCON minibadge design files, and a modern community minibadge wiki (which only archives badges from 2021 onward) turned up no further record of this specific badge.
