The 10x10 Display Case is a display stand and wall-mount holder made for SAINTCON's "10x10" minibadge grid boards — the community boards that hold a 10-by-10 array of minibadges. According to the maker Cyberm3n's SAINTCON 2025 submission, the case works with both generations of 10x10 board (the older USB-micro powered version and the current USB-C version), as long as the board follows the SAINTCON spec. It can be mounted on a wall or set up free-standing on a shelf, desk, or table.

The listing rates it "Beginner" difficulty and says instructions are included with the case itself. It was categorized as a "Badge Accessory" and marked "Rare" on the community wiki, with acquisition listed simply as "Find me" — implying it was obtained in person at the con rather than sold online. No price, production quantity, board house, or design files were found; the maker does not appear to have a separate storefront, repository, or social profile linked from the community record.
