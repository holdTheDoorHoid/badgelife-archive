The Factorio Rocket is a SAINTCON 2025 minibadge made by slever, styled after the rocket silo from the video game Factorio, with the tagline "Ran out of planet? No problem. The Factory MUST grow." It is a passive (no microcontroller) LED board lit with orange and red LEDs, listed as "Advanced" soldering difficulty on the community sheet because of its mix of precision resistors and capacitors and an unusual step where the red LEDs are soldered upside-down and solder-bridged to their pads.

The badge was not sold; the maker's sheet lists it as available only by trading in person at the con, and marks it "Super Rare" under the "Personal" category. It shares a soldering-instruction style and the same "Factory Must Grow" theme with a companion minibadge by the same maker, the Factorio Transport Belt, also from SAINTCON 2025.
