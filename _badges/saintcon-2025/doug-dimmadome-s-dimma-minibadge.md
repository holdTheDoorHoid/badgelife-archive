Doug Dimmadome's Dimma-Minibadge is a SAINTCON 2025 minibadge by designer Pips, part of the SAINTCON badgelife tradition of small trade-only PCB pins documented on the community-run minibadge.wiki. It depicts Doug Dimmadome, the wealthy department-store mogul from Nickelodeon's animated series "The Fairly OddParents," in his signature white suit, cowboy hat, and sheriff-style star badge.

The badge is a beginner-level build: the maker's assembly notes call for soldering a single LED, a resistor, and pin headers, giving it a basic light-up element rather than any microcontroller-driven functionality. Pips lists it as a personal-category badge with "Super Rare" rarity, obtainable only by trading directly with the maker rather than through a storefront or free giveaway. No print quantity, price, PCB manufacturer, or open-source design files were published alongside the listing.
