The Minibadge Community at SAINTCON 2025 — run by SHIFTY and distinctm1nd — gave out its own official minibadge at its community booth, alongside its usual activities: a minibadge trading table, a "museum" display of attendee-designed boards, a KiCad build guide, and a bingo-card game where finding and collecting other attendees' minibadges around the con earned prizes.

The community's own event page did not describe the badge itself beyond confirming it would exist, saying only that "details are still being worked out" and directing attendees to the booth to learn how to get one. No design specs (chip, LEDs, shape, colors), price, production quantity, or photo of this particular badge turned up in a search pass across the SAINTCON sites, the community's GitHub org, and the community-run minibadge.wiki archive, so most technical and imagery fields are left empty here rather than guessed.
