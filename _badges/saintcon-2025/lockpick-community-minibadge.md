The Lockpick Community MiniBadge is a SAINTCON 2025 community badge made by SHIFTY for the con's Lock Picking Community. Rather than being a one-off collectible, it doubles as a progress tracker: the community describes lock picking as a security "rite of passage," and the badge has replaceable belt inserts that follow a lockpick belt system, so attendees who take part in the community's instruction, challenges, and practice time can visibly level up their belt as their skills improve over the course of the con.

Assembly is pitched at beginners: the submission notes that the surface-mount LEDs and resistors are soldered using the single-pad hand-soldering method. No board house, quantity made, price, or open-source design files are listed in the source record, and no separate maker page or repository for SHIFTY was found, so those fields are left blank.
