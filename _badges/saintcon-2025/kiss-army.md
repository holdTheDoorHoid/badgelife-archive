Kiss Army is a personal-category minibadge bitl!sa brought to SAINTCON 2025, built as a tribute to the band KISS: the maker's own note says they grew up listening to Kiss (up through the *Unmasked* era) and figured other attendees did too. It's a simple build for hand-soldering beginners -- a single LED and resistor mounted single-pad style on the back, plus four 2-position headers -- with no microcontroller or programmable behavior.

The maker didn't sell it; the community listing says the way to get one was to trade with bitl!sa in person or simply ask, on the condition that you tell them your favorite Kiss song. No price, print run size, or design files are recorded anywhere the archive could find, and the badge is marked "Uncommon" rarity on the community sheet.
