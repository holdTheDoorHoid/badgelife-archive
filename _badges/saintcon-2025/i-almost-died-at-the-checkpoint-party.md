"I almost DIED at the CheckPoint party" is a SAINTCON 2025 minibadge by a maker going by Pips, distributed at (or via) the CheckPoint booth. It is a joke/personal-category piece: the description explains that "some brave soul partied so hard at the 2024 checkpoint party that they died," and the badge is made "in his honor. RIP." The board is a black rectangular PCB with the title lettering in a rough, hand-brushed yellow font and the phrase "DIED" set in bold cutout letters, alongside a stylized "CHECK POINT" wordmark and logo.

It carries no active electronics (MCU: none) — it is a beginner-level, learn-to-solder minibadge whose only assembly step is soldering the through-hole pin headers, per the maker's own instructions. It was marked Uncommon rarity and Personal category on the community sheet.

No storefront, repository, or additional maker page beyond the minibadge.wiki community submission was located, so pricing, exact quantity, and open-source status are left blank.
