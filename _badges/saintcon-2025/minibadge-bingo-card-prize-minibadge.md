Given out by the SAINTCON 2025 MiniBadge Community, this minibadge was the reward for completing a Bingo Card — a card of squares filled in by collecting other minibadges around the conference. Anyone who filled the whole card got one "while supplies last"; the joke lands on the back of the board, silkscreened "I GOT BINGO AND ONLY GOT THIS LOUSY MINIBADGE" over a design credited to DistinctM1nd, a recurring SAINTCON minibadge designer.

Mechanically it's a simple solder-your-own minibadge: the maker's build instructions call for hand-soldering LED(s) and resistor(s) using the single-pad method, followed by a 2-position header, matching the pattern used across other SAINTCON minibadges that chain power through their headers. The board is shaped like a pair of overlapping street signs, one reading "MINIBADGE COMMUNITY / BINGO" on the front.

A related but separate minibadge, the "MiniBadge Community Bingo BLACKOUT MiniBadge," was reserved for the first three attendees to fill every square ("blackout"); it shares the same theme and header hardware but is a distinct catalog entry and not covered here.
