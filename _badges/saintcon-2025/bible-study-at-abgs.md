"Bible Study at ABGs" is a personal joke minibadge made by a SAINTCON attendee going by Pips for SAINTCON 2025. Rather than being sold or distributed at the convention itself, it was handed out informally by the maker at ABG's, a bar in Provo, Utah, to people recognized as fellow con-goers — the maker's own description calls it a "sacrilegious minibadge" tied to that off-site tradition.

The badge is a simple pin-header minibadge: the only assembly step listed is soldering the header pins, and no chip, LEDs, or display are documented. It is cataloged on minibadge.wiki (a community-run SAINTCON minibadge database) as a "Personal" category item with "Common" rarity and a soldering difficulty of "Beginner."
