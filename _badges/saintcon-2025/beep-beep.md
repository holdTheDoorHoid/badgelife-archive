BEEP BEEP is a SAINTCON 2025 minibadge by Pips: a small PCB carrying a large, deliberately oversized button on the front that triggers a piezo buzzer mounted on the back. The joke is in the proportions — the maker's own description calls it "an obnoxiously-loud buzzer with an obnoxiously bright and large button," daring the wearer not to press it.

Assembly is rated Intermediate: LEDs and resistors go on first, then the buzzer on the back (with its leads trimmed flush), then the button on front. An optional potentiometer (R1) sets buzzer volume; builders who skip that part bridge a pad marked "NO R1" with solder instead. Because of the buzzer's height, the badge needs extra-long pin headers to seat properly in a host badge's socket.

The maker's community-sheet listing gives a quantity made of 0 and marks the badge "Super Rare," with the only stated way to get one being to trade directly with Pips. No independent storefront, repository, or press coverage was found; everything above comes from the single self-reported minibadge.wiki record.
