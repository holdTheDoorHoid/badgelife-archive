The Factorio Transport Belt is a SAINTCON 2025 minibadge made by slever, one of a small personal series themed on the video game Factorio ("The Factory Must Grow."). It is a beginner-level solder kit: the assembly instructions call for soldering an LED and a resistor using the single-pad hand-soldering method, then four 2-position headers — the standard link-in-chain headers SAINTCON minibadges use to connect to other minibadges. The maker listed it as "Rare" and distributed it informally, telling people to "just ask for one" at the con rather than selling it.

The badge is part of the same small-batch, personal-category minibadge culture documented on the community-run minibadge.wiki site; details beyond the maker's own submission there — PCB color, exact LED count, and whether any design files were shared — were not found.
