"Birds Aren't Real" is a SAINTCON 2025 minibadge by maker slever, playing on the internet meme/conspiracy-theory bit of the same name. The community minibadge.wiki listing describes it only in the joke's own deadpan register ("Have you ever actually seen a bird? No you haven't...") rather than with a real functional writeup.

Electronically it is a simple beginner-level soldering badge: a red LED and a blue LED, each behind its own resistor (0 ohm and 68 ohm), plus four 2-position headers. It was given away for free at the con -- the listing says people could get one by simply asking -- and the community sheet rates it "Rare" despite that, with quantity made left unrecorded.

No maker page, repository, or storefront for this badge was found; the only located source is the minibadge.wiki community database entry and its underlying data feed.
