This SAINTCON minibadge takes the shape of Jules Winnfield's wallet from *Pulp Fiction*, made by community member alairock for personal distribution rather than as an official con badge. It's a simple build: one LED and one resistor, with the maker's own soldering note warning that the green wire should point toward the resistor side of the trace.

Alairock had 250 made through JLCPCB and offered them informally, telling people to find him on Discord to get one. The badge appears only in the community-run minibadge.wiki database; no separate storefront, repo, or maker page was found. The wiki's own record lists the conference year as 2025, which is why this entry has been moved from SAINTCON 2026 (where the discovery sweep first filed it) to SAINTCON 2025.
