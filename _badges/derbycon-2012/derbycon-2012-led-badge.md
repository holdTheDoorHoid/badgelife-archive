Mr & Mrs Make It Urz made this unofficial badge for attendees of DerbyCon 2012 (DerbyCon 2.0, "Reunion"). It is a laser-cut, two-layer acrylic badge — one clear layer and one black layer — with a sheet of laser-cut paper sandwiched between them. A small battery and a single LED are set into the black back layer, hidden from view by the black paper insert so the electronics don't show through; engraving is cut into the back side of the clear front layer for a clean, illuminated look.

No storefront, pricing, quantity, or design files for this badge were found; the only source located is the maker's own Flickr posting of a photo of the finished badges. It is not known whether the badge was sold, given away, or made only for the maker's own use, or how many were produced.
