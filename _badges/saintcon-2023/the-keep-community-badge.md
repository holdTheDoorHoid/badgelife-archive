The Keep Community Badge is the official 2023 SAINTCON minibadge for The Keep, a hands-on community at the conference where attendees practice hacking skills in a built environment ranging from beginner to expert difficulty. Rated "intermediate" difficulty and "common" rarity in SAINTCON's own minibadge guide, it was given out to people who took part in The Keep's activities rather than sold.

The badge is a simple PCB piece styled after a brick, paired with a separate plug-in "torch" accessory: a small board carrying a 3mm candle-flicker LED and a resistor, soldered to two short header pins on the main board and two long header pins on the torch, so the torch stands up from the brick and flickers like a lit torch. The back of the main PCB carries The Keep's pixel-art logo. It has no microcontroller; the flicker effect comes from the LED's own internal driver chip, not a program running on the badge.

Designed by Jup1t3r, a prolific SAINTCON minibadge designer with dozens of other badges in the archive from 2017 through 2023, this piece fits the same minibadge format used across SAINTCON's various themed and community boards that year.
