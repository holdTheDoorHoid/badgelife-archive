The Hack-In-The-Box Badge is a SAINTCON 2023 event minibadge made by SHIFTY for Hack-In-The-Box (HITB), the after-hours hangout track at SAINTCON. It's a small red PCB silkscreened with a blocky "HACK IN THE BOX" wordmark on the front; the back carries two 0805 LEDs (D1 and D3) and three 0805 resistors (R1-R3), soldered by hand along with a pair of 2-pin headers that let it chain into the wearer's other minibadges. The official 2023 SAINTCON Minibadge Guide rates it "Intermediate" to assemble and "Uncommon" in rarity.

Like SAINTCON's other event minibadges, it wasn't sold — attendees got one by showing up to the Hack-In-The-Box after-hours event and talking with the SHIFTY team, per the guide's "how do I get one" blurb. No separate storefront, repository, or press coverage for this specific badge was found; everything here comes from the minibadge guide itself.
