No physical "Iceman RFID Badge" could be confirmed to exist. The name traces to "Iceman," a well-known RFID/NFC security researcher (associated with the Proxmark3 and Chameleon tools) who gave a talk titled "SAINTCON 2023 - Iceman - RFID Hacking" at the 2023 conference. A research sweep appears to have picked up this talk listing and mistaken it for a minibadge entry.

The official SAINTCON 2023 Minibadge Assembly Guide, which documents every sanctioned minibadge for that year, does not credit any badge to Iceman or list an RFID-themed badge under that name. A community-run catalog of the SAINTCON 2023 minibadge ecosystem likewise has no matching entry. This is distinct from the "RFID/NFC 2024 minibadge" (co-credited to ICEMAN and GOBO42), which is a confirmed, separate item made for SAINTCON 2024.

This entry is left as `rumored` pending any source that actually documents a physical Iceman-branded badge from SAINTCON 2023. If none surfaces, it is a candidate for removal.
