The SAINTCON Golf Club minibadge was one of the event minibadges handed out at SAINTCON 2023, tied to a mini-golf course the conference set up right in the conference area that year. Designed by SHIFTY, one of the organizers of SAINTCON's long-running minibadge community, the badge carries shield artwork reading "SAINTCON GOLF CLUB" over a golf ball, and is built as a simple single-LED PCB: one 1206 LED and a matching 1206 resistor, hand-soldered using the single-pad method, with a 2-pin header.

Per the official 2023 minibadge build guide, the badge was rated Beginner difficulty and Uncommon rarity, and attendees got one by playing a round on the on-site putting green rather than by purchase — it functioned as a free drop tied to that on-site activity. No separate storefront, repository, or social post about the badge turned up in searches; everything confirmed here comes from the guide itself.
