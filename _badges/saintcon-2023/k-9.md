K-9 is a SAINTCON 2023 minibadge by Ray-Man depicting the robot dog from Looney Tunes' Marvin the Martian cartoons. The maker made it as a companion to a Marvin the Martian minibadge that a fellow attendee, "Berly," was bringing to the con, giving K-9 "his trusty counterpart to keep him out of trouble."

The badge is a green PCB silkscreened with K-9's likeness, wearing his signature space helmet. It carries two hand-soldered green LEDs and two resistors, using a single-pad soldering technique aimed at beginners; the maker's instructions note the LEDs must be oriented so they read correctly right-side up rather than "standing on his head."

It was not sold: the maker gave copies to attendees who showed up with Berly's Marvin the Martian badge, and would otherwise trade for one. The minibadge.wiki community sheet lists it as "Super Rare," though no specific print quantity is recorded.
