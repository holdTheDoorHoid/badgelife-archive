The VENT EVENT BADGE is a SAINTCON 2023 minibadge made by SHIFTY and given to attendees of "VENT," a SAINTCON session where security professionals speak and swap stories off the record and work through problems facing the industry together. It is a simple, beginner-level hand-solder kit: LEDs and a resistor attached with the single-pad technique, plus pin headers, with no listed microcontroller.

The badge was earned rather than bought — attendees had to attend and participate in the VENT event to receive one. Minibadge.wiki, the community-run SAINTCON minibadge database, lists it in the "Event" category with a rarity of "Uncommon."

No maker page, GitHub repo, or storefront listing was found beyond the minibadge.wiki entry, so design files, exact LED count/type, and quantity made remain unconfirmed.
