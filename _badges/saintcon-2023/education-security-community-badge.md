The Education Security Community Badge is a SAINTCON 2023 minibadge representing the conference's Education Security community, organized by people from Utah Education Network (UEN). The community's discussions, walk-ups and hands-on workshops focused on cybersecurity in K-12, higher-education and library settings, and this beginner-level minibadge — a blue PCB with whale artwork on the front and a single hand-soldered LED and resistor on the back — was the collectible marker for that group, rated "uncommon" in the guide's rarity scale.

Like most SAINTCON community minibadges, it wasn't sold: attendees got one by visiting the Education Security community's discussions or booth. It is a simple build (a single 1206 LED and 1206 resistor using the single-pad hand-soldering method, plus pin headers), consistent with the "learn to solder" minibadges SAINTCON hands out across its community booths.

The same team (credited as SHIFTY, though the 2023 guide's body text misspells it "SHFITY") produced a follow-up Education Security badge for SAINTCON 2024, cataloged separately.
