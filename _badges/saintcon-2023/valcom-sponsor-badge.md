The Valcom Sponsor Badge is a SAINTCON 2023 sponsor minibadge made for cybersecurity company Valcom ("VLCM"), designed by Jup1t3r, one of SAINTCON's regular minibadge designers. It depicts Valcom's mascot, a "rabid ferret," drawn wearing a wide-brimmed hat and a shaggy beard, silkscreened on a small PCB with a single 0805 LED and matching 0805 resistor. Assembly is rated intermediate: the LED and resistor are both hand-soldered single-pad style, followed by 2-pin headers, with the guide warning not to exceed 300°C when soldering the LED.

Attendees got one by visiting the Valcom booth in the SAINTCON 2023 expo center; the official guide lists its rarity as "common." No quantity-made figure, price, or open-source design files were found for this badge.
