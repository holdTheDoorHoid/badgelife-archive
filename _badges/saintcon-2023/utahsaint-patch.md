The UtahSAINT Patch is a $4 velcro-backed embroidered cloth patch (4"x3", rounded top) sold through the SAINTCON 2023 online store. It commemorates UtahSAINT, the year-round Utah security community associated with SAINTCON, rather than being an electronic minibadge or SAO.

It was listed in the SAINTCON store's general merchandise section alongside other similar cloth patches (the SAINTCON BadgeLife Patch, LockPick Community Patch, and Scavenger Hunt Challenge Patch), an iron-on velcro attachment bundle, and mini-badge display boards. No hardware, electronics, or design files are associated with it — it is fabric, not a PCB.
