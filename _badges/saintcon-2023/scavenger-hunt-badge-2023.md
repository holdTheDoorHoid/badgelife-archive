The Scavenger Hunt Badge is the 2023 edition of Jup1t3r's yearly contest minibadge for SAINTCON's Scavenger Hunt game, following 2021's SCAVENGER HUNT and 2022's SCAVENGER HUNT BADGE (2022). It is a small PCB cut into the shape of the game's angry raccoon mascot, yellow soldermask on the front with the raccoon artwork, and black soldermask on the back carrying a "SCAVENGER HUNT" wordmark, the maker's handle, and the badge's only active component: a single 1206 LED lit through a 1206 resistor, both hand-solderable with the single-pad method along with a 2-pin header.

Per the official SAINTCON 2023 Minibadge Guide, the badge was rated beginner difficulty to assemble and uncommon rarity, and was not sold — attendees earned one by visiting the Scavenger Hunt contest booth, working through a posted list of items or challenges to present to the game's "Perpetrators," and turning them in for points. No price, print quantity, or open-source design files are published for it.
