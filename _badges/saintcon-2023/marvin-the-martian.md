Marvin the Martian is a SAINTCON 2023 minibadge made by a community member known as Berly, depicting the Looney Tunes character. It falls under the Minibadge Wiki's "Personal" category, distinct from village or vendor badges, and was rated "Intermediate" soldering difficulty with standard SMD assembly.

The badge was not sold through a storefront; instead, it was given away by Berly directly, in person, to attendees who found her around the SAINTCON conference center. The Minibadge Wiki listing marks it as "Rare," though no specific production quantity is documented.

No further detail on materials, PCB colors, or electronics is available from the sources found; this appears to be a simple personal minibadge without stated LEDs, MCU, or SAO connectivity.
