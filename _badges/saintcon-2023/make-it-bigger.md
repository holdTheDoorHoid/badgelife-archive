"Make it Bigger" is a SAINTCON 2023 minibadge by Honki and Xanadu2600, made for the volunteers staffing the conference's registration/check-in tables rather than sold or distributed to the general badge crowd. The name plays on the phrase repeated constantly at check-in as attendees hold up QR codes too small for the scanner to read. The badge answers the joke visually: three LEDs (red, yellow, green) wired through an 8-pin IC and a pair of resistors (200k and 100k ohm) light in sequence to depict a hand opening from a closed fist to fully spread fingers.

The build is rated "Advanced" difficulty and the badge itself "Rare" — the minibadge guide notes it wasn't distributed through the normal contest/trade channels but had to be talked out of a check-in volunteer's hands. No price, print run, chip part number, or open-source design files were found; the only two sources located, SAINTCON's own 2023 Minibadge Guide and the community-run MiniBadge Wiki, describe it identically and neither lists a maker storefront or repository.
