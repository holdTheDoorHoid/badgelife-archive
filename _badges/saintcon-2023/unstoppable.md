Unstoppable is a SAINTCON 2023 minibadge by Honki and Mike's Wife: a green PCB shaped around a roaring T-rex holding a pair of grabber-claw tools, under the badge's title in a starburst banner. It plays on the classic joke about T-rex arms being too short to reach anything, solved here by giving the dinosaur a pair of mechanical grabbers.

Assembly is rated beginner: builders solder a resistor and two red LEDs onto the back of the board. The maker's listing describes it as a personal, low-production piece — rarity "Rare" — obtained by asking for one at SAINTCON or trading for it, rather than through any storefront or preorder.

No hardware files, firmware, or independent press coverage turned up; everything here traces back to the maker's own entry on the minibadge.wiki community sheet for 2023, which is also the source for both photos.
