The "ICS Escape Challenge - I Failed Badge" is a SAINTCON 2023 contest minibadge made by SHIFTY for their ICS (industrial control systems) escape-room challenge, in which players tried to identify and contain simulated cyber threats to restore a fictional company's industrial operations. The badge itself carries no MCU or interactive electronics beyond two through-hole LEDs (a white D1 and a red D2) soldered by the recipient using the single-pad hand-soldering method common to SAINTCON minibadges; its red PCB is silkscreened with a mushroom-cloud/bomb graphic and "ICS... I FAILED" lettering, playing on the idea of the contest room ending in a simulated meltdown.

The badge was not sold; SAINTCON's contest room handed it out to participants who came to play the challenge, alongside a second, presumably "I succeeded" counterpart badge referenced in the maker's own listing text. Community-submitted records give a soldering difficulty of "Beginner" and a rarity of "Uncommon," but do not state a price, a firmware/hardware repository, or a quantity actually produced.
