The ID/10T COMMUNITY BADGE is a SAINTCON 2023 community minibadge made by a maker credited as SHIFTY, representing the con's Industrial Devices and IoT (ICS/IoT security) community. Per the maker's own description on minibadge.wiki, it was meant for attendees interested in "control systems and how they work" and the security implications of those systems, and was given out at that community's area during the convention.

Assembly is rated beginner difficulty: the maker's instructions call for soldering the LEDs first using the single-pad method, then a resistor the same way, then the pin headers. The wiki listing does not record an LED count, chip, color scheme, quantity made, or price, and no separate storefront, repository, or social page for the maker was found, so those fields are left empty rather than guessed.
