The Briefs Community Badge is SAINTCON 2023's minibadge for the "Briefs" community, a track built around short, low-pressure talks (5-10 minutes, hard stop at 10) on any security topic. Designed by SHIFTY, who made the bulk of SAINTCON's 2023 minibadge lineup, the badge is a small beginner-level PCB build: a single reverse-mount 1206 LED (D1) wired through a 1206 resistor (R1), assembled by hand-soldering the LED and resistor first and then the 2-pin headers. The front carries a cartoon mad-scientist mascot over a "BRIEFS COMMUNITY" wordmark; the back is a simple perimeter-mount board silkscreened "SAINTCON 2023 / SHIFTY."

It was not sold — the official guide lists it as "Uncommon" rarity and free, obtained by attending a Briefs session and presenting. A separate, rarer "Briefs Speaker Minibadge" existed for the same community, distinguishing attendees who spoke from those who simply showed up. No print run size, storefront, or open-source design files were found for this badge specifically.
