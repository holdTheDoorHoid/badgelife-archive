The HOMELABS COMMUNITY BADGE is a SAINTCON 2023 minibadge made for the Home Labs Community, a group the minibadge.wiki listing traces back to a SAINTCON talk in 2019 that grew into "a large following of hardware and software enthusiasts, hackers, recyclers, coders, network people, and pretty much everything in-between." The badge itself is a simple beginner soldering project: a white PCB printed in black and yellow with a hooded, gas-masked mascot above a pixel-font "HOME LABS COMMUNITY" wordmark, populated with a single through-hole LED, a resistor, and header pins.

It was handed out freely at the Home Labs Community's area at the con rather than sold — the listing's only stated requirement to get one was "that you bring yourself and your curiosity." No price, production quantity, board house, or design files are given in the source, and no independent coverage of the group or the badge turned up in a limited search.
