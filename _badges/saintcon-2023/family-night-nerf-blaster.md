The Family Night - Nerf Blaster minibadge was made by TGalvez for SAINTCON 2023's Family Night, a track meant to let attendees' families take part in the con alongside them. It was handed out specifically to people who showed up to the Nerf Blasters community activity and shot a Nerf blaster with the group; TGalvez also kept a small stock on hand afterward for anyone who missed the event itself but still wanted one.

Electronically it's simple: a single through-hole LED and resistor, with the LED deliberately bent flat against the back of the board rather than standing upright, plus four 2-position headers along the bottom edge (likely for daisy-chaining with the other Family Night minibadges from the same year, Lock Pick and Rocket Building). The front art is a green PCB silhouette of a Nerf blaster with "NERF" lettering; the back carries two cartoon kids in safety goggles under "Family Night Event" text and TGalvez's sunflower maker mark.

## Make your own

No hardware files, Gerbers, or BOM were published for this badge. The official SAINTCON minibadge guide gives basic assembly steps (solder the resistor, then the LED with a half-inch standoff bent to lie flat, then the four headers), and a companion guide with clearer photography for the full set of 2023 Family Night badges was published at minibadges.zevlag.com, but neither is a design-file release.
