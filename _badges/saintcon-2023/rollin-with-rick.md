Rollin With Rick is a beginner-friendly SAINTCON 2023 minibadge by maker Z0nifer, part of the personal-category swap culture around SAINTCON's minibadge tradition. It carries a single LED that can be wired to either blink or stay solid, set by which of two solder pads gets bridged, plus a piece of badge art built around a scannable QR code (the "Rick" of the title suggesting a Rickroll-style joke, though the maker's page does not spell that out).

The badge was not sold; the maker's own note on how to acquire it was simply "Come trade with me!", making it a swap-only item at the con rather than a storefront release. It is logged on minibadge.wiki with beginner soldering difficulty, a "Super Rare" rarity tag, and a quantity-made value of 0, which likely reflects an untracked run rather than a literal count.

No independent maker page, repository, or fabrication share was found beyond the maker's own submission to the minibadge.wiki community database, so hardware details like PCB color, board shape, and MCU/architecture (this appears to be a passive LED board with no microcontroller) are not confirmed beyond what the soldering instructions imply.
