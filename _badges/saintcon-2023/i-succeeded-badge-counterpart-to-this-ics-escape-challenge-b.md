No badge titled "I Succeeded" turns up in SHIFTY's own SAINTCON 2023 records. The maker's ICS Escape Challenge -- an industrial-control-systems-themed escape room where players had to identify and contain simulated cyber threats to restore a fictional company's ("Acme") operations -- handed out one of exactly two companion minibadges to participants: "ICS Escape Challenge - I Survived Badge" for those who finished, and "ICS Escape Challenge - I Failed Badge" for those who didn't. Both are already catalogued as their own entries in this archive, each sourced from the minibadge.wiki 2023 community data feed.

This entry's title most likely traces back to a guess in the "I Failed" badge's write-up, which speculated its counterpart was "presumably an 'I succeeded' badge" without having confirmed the actual title. No source -- the minibadge.wiki feed, a search, or any maker page -- describes a badge called "I Succeeded" as a distinct object from "I Survived." Treat this record as a probable duplicate of saintcon-2023-ics-escape-challenge-i-survived-badge rather than a third, separate badge.
