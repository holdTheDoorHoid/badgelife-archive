The AI Community Badge is a SAINTCON 2023 minibadge for the con's AI Community space, designed by SHIFTY (who leads SAINTCON's MiniBadge community). It is themed around the generative-AI tools popular at the time — ChatGPT-4, Bard v2, Dall-E, and Midjourney — and was meant to draw attendees into the AI Community area to try out the tech, get project ideas, and discuss AI ethics and "which way the world will end."

It is a simple, beginner-friendly, solder-it-yourself minibadge: an FR4 PCB carrying 1206 LEDs and a 1206 resistor, assembled onto a 2-pin header so it plugs into a host badge for power. Per the official 2023 SAINTCON Minibadge Guide, its one distinguishing behavior is that the D2 LED blinks in time with the (host badge's) clock. The guide rates it BEGINNER difficulty and COMMON rarity, and says it was given out to attendees who came and participated in the AI Community space rather than sold.

No maker storefront, source files, or photos of the assembled badge turned up outside the guide itself, so price, quantity, exact LED count, and visual details (color, shape) are left blank pending a source that shows the physical badge.
