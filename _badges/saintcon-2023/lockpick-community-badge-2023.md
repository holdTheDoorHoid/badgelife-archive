The Lockpick Community Badge is a SAINTCON 2023 minibadge for the con's Lock Pick Community (LPC), designed by Jup1t3r. It is a simple hand-solderable board — a single 1206 LED and 1206 resistor on FR4, soldered single-pad style — built around a progression gimmick: alongside the four corner header pins used for mounting, two extra center pins are left open for "belts," small add-on pieces that attendees earn as they level up through the community's lockpick belt system over the course of the con. The badge follows SAINTCON's shared "clock standard" wiring, letting its LED blink in sync with other minibadges on the same lanyard chain.

The Lock Pick Community itself, run by community leads odie and k33n, taught lock mechanics and picking technique at a booth with hands-on stations for beginners and a "Progressive Picking Challenge" for more experienced attendees; distribution details for the badge were handled at the booth rather than published in advance, consistent with how most SAINTCON community minibadges are given out rather than sold. No maker storefront, open-source files, price, or production count were found for this badge.
