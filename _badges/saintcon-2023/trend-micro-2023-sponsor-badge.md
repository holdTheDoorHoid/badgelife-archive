Trend Micro sponsored SAINTCON 2023 and gave out this minibadge at their sponsorship booth to anyone who visited and talked with their staff. It is a simple square PCB carrying the company's circular "t" logo mark and the word "TREND" on the front, with "MICRO" printed along the edge; the back carries a stylized leaf-like logo variant and the tagline "We secure your connected world."

Electrically it is about as simple as a minibadge gets: two 1206 red LEDs (D1, D2) and a single 1206 100-ohm resistor (R1), all sourced from AliExpress per the official guide's parts list. The two LEDs alternate on what the guide calls the "clock standard" pattern rather than lighting together. The official SAINTCON 2023 Minibadge Guide rates it beginner difficulty and rare rarity, and includes a short assembly video (bit.ly/48t5MS5) covering LED orientation (green-mark side toward the "garage" pad shape) and resistor placement.

No price is listed because it was not sold; it was a free giveaway tied to booth engagement, and no total quantity produced is stated in the available source.
