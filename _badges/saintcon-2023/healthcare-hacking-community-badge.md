The Healthcare Hacking Community Badge is a SAINTCON 2023 community minibadge designed by Brucifer for the Health Care Hacking Community, a gathering space at the conference for attendees to learn and share information on health care cybersecurity and how it intersects with personal health. The PCB is cut into a heart shape, with a heartbeat (EKG) trace silkscreened across the front alongside a line of binary digits, and two 1206 LEDs (with a matching 1206 resistor) that the builder hand-solders onto the back.

Like other SAINTCON community minibadges, it was not sold: attendees got one by visiting the Health Care Hacking Community table and participating, per the official 2023 Minibadge Assembly Guide, which rates it "Beginner" difficulty and "Common" rarity. No hardware or firmware files, storefront listing, or maker's own post about this specific badge were found beyond that guide.
