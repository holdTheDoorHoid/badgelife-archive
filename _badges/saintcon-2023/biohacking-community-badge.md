The Biohacking Community Badge is a 2023 SAINTCON minibadge made by Jup1t3r for the Biohacking and Healthcare Hacking Community, one of the con's many community-run collectible boards. Its face crosses a wrench with a DNA double helix on a dark purple PCB, tying together the "hacking" and "biology" halves of the community's name. The official minibadge guide rates it ADVANCED to assemble and RARE to find, and warns that the single LED's polarity is not marked on the silkscreen, so builders need to get its orientation right from the schematic or reference photos rather than the board itself.

The badge was not sold; attendees got one by tracking down a Biohacking representative at the Healthcare Hacking Community booth during the conference. Beyond the minibadge guide entry and the community's own (largely placeholder) SAINTCON page, no maker storefront, repository, or social post specific to this badge could be found, so its price, print run, and any electrical details beyond "has an LED" remain unknown.
