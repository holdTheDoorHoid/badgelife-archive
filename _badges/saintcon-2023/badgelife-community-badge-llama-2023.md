The BadgeLife Community Badge is a SAINTCON 2023 minibadge designed by Jup1t3r (with SHIFTY co-credited on the board's back silkscreen), built around a simple two-LED circuit: a single 1206 resistor and two 1206 LEDs (D1, D2) wired through a 2-pin header that draws power from the host badge. The front artwork is a llama wearing sunglasses, flanked by "BADGELIFE" lettering on each side; the back reads "BADGELIFE COMMUNITY" over the two LED footprints.

Rated Beginner difficulty and Common rarity in the official 2023 SAINTCON Minibadge Guide, it was one of the "Community Minibadge" category badges tied to the con's BadgeLife community/minibadge display. Attendees could claim one for free by bringing a completed minibadge to contribute to the display, or simply by sharing their favorite thing about minibadges with the people running it — a low-barrier design meant to draw newcomers into the minibadge-building hobby rather than to be sold or rationed.

It is a separate item from the similarly-named "Minibadge Community Badge," a larger 7-LED board that SHIFTY alone designed and gave away that same year as a one-off prize for whoever was seen wearing the most lit-up minibadges; that badge has its own entry in this archive.
