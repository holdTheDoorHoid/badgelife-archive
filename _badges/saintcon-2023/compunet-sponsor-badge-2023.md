CompuNet, Inc. is a longtime SAINTCON sponsor, and this minibadge was its giveaway for the 2023 conference, marking a return appearance ("Compunet is back again as one of this years amazing Sponsors," per the official build guide). It was designed by Jup1t3r, who also designed the archive's earlier 2021 CompuNet minibadge, and was distributed for free to attendees who visited the CompuNet sponsor booth and talked with their staff.

The badge is a simple, beginner-difficulty build: a round FR4 PCB in blue and brown carrying CompuNet's swirl logo on the front, with a single 0805 LED, a 1206 resistor, and 2-pin headers hand-soldered on the back, where the CompuNet wordmark is silkscreened. The official build guide rates it "common" rarity and warns not to solder the LED above 300°C. No firmware, MCU, or design files are involved; no separate maker page or storefront listing was found beyond the official guide.
