The Sunset minibadge is a SAINTCON 2023 minibadge made by CompuKidMike (Michael Whiteley), who also runs MK Factor and has built several of SAINTCON's main conference badges over the years. Like most SAINTCON minibadges, it is a small PCB sized to plug into the con's minibadge exchange board rather than a standalone electronic badge.

The front carries a red-and-black silkscreen illustration of a forest skyline against a sunset (or sunrise), giving the badge its name. The back is populated with a single LED (marked D1) and a series resistor (R1), with no microcontroller - the badge simply lights up when connected to power through the minibadge board, along with the MK Factor logo and "compukidmike 'sunset' 2023" text.

As with most SAINTCON minibadges, this one appears to have been distributed through the con's peer-to-peer minibadge trading rather than sold at a storefront, so no price or production quantity could be confirmed.
