The Briefs Speaker Minibadge is a small participation award from SAINTCON 2023's "Briefs" community space, designed by SHIFTY. Briefs is a recurring track of short, informal talks (5 to 10 minutes, hard stop) where attendees share a home-lab project, a work story, or anything else worth a quick mention rather than a full-length session. Anyone who gets up and presents one of these short talks receives the minibadge - no purchase, raffle, or CTF involved.

The board itself is a simple beginner soldering kit: a single LED (D1) and a resistor (R1) on a small PCB, with front artwork showing a grinning yellow mascot character next to a microphone and the text "BRIEFS SPEAKER." The SAINTCON minibadge guide rates it BEGINNER difficulty and RARE rarity, reflecting that supply was limited to however many people actually got up to speak that year.

It is a companion to the separate "Briefs Community Badge," also designed by SHIFTY, which was given more broadly for engaging with the Briefs space; the Speaker variant specifically marks having presented.
