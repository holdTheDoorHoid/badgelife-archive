The "ICS Escape Challenge - I Survived" badge is a SAINTCON 2023 contest minibadge made by SHIFTY, given to attendees who completed an escape-room-style challenge built around industrial control systems (ICS) security. The challenge cast players as responders who had to identify and contain simulated cyber threats to restore operations at a fictional company, "Acme." It has a companion badge, "ICS Escape Challenge - I Failed," awarded to participants who did not finish the challenge in time.

The board carries one always-on white LED and one blinking red LED, soldered by hand using the single-pad SMD method along with a resistor and pin headers; SAINTCON's own materials rate it a beginner soldering difficulty. Beyond the community-submitted listing on minibadge.wiki, no maker page, repository, or press coverage turned up, so technical details like the MCU, exact LED part numbers, price, and production quantity are not confirmed.
