The SPACE COMMUNITY CUBE SAT BADGE is a SAINTCON 2023 minibadge tied to that year's Space Community village. The village ran an activity built around a simulated CubeSat: a large display showed the satellite orbiting Earth in real time, and attendees learned to decode the data it was "transmitting" and to try sending commands back and watching for a response. The badge itself commemorates that activity rather than implementing it — it's a beginner-friendly through-hole board (LEDs and resistors soldered single-pad style, then pin headers) with no stated microcontroller, display, or connectivity of its own.

It was made by a contributor going by "Jup1t3r" and classified on minibadge.wiki as a "Community" category badge with "Common" rarity, given out to anyone who visited the Space Community village at SAINTCON 2023. No maker page, repository, or storefront beyond the minibadge.wiki community listing could be found.
