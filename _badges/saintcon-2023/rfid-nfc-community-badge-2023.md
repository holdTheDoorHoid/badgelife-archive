The RFID/NFC Community Badge is one of SAINTCON 2023's official community minibadges, representing the con's RFID/NFC community and designed by SHIFTY, a prolific SAINTCON minibadge designer. Attendees earned the badge by participating in the community's on-site activities, which taught short-range wireless communication via RFID and NFC and how to use or secure it.

It is a simple beginner-level soldering kit: a small FR4 PCB with a single 1206 LED, a 1206 resistor, and 2-pin headers, all hand-soldered using the single-pad method. The front carries a yellow gorilla mascot logo reading "RFID/NFC Community"; the back holds the component footprints and is silkscreened "SAINTCON 2023 SHIFTY." It was rated common rarity and beginner difficulty in the official minibadge guide.

This is a distinct badge from the archive's existing RFID/NFC community minibadges for SAINTCON 2021 and 2022 (both designed by Jup1t3r), and from the separate 2023 "RFID Rocket" minibadge, also by SHIFTY.
