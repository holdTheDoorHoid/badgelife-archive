McRib In Memoriam is a SAINTCON 2023 personal minibadge by Sh33pr0ck, a small tongue-in-cheek tribute to McDonald's McRib sandwich. The front carries McRib sandwich art with the tagline "Tangy Temptation"; the back has a "RIP" tombstone graphic over the maker's handle, alongside the badge's electronics: a single LED (D1) and resistor (R1), with a three-pin JP1 jumper that lets the builder choose between the LED staying solid on or blinking.

It's a beginner-difficulty solder project with no microcontroller — just the LED, resistor, and jumper. The maker didn't sell it; per their own listing, the way to get one was to trade with them directly or simply ask, and the community wiki marks it "Super Rare."

No maker page, repository, or storefront for this badge or for Sh33pr0ck turned up beyond the minibadge.wiki community listing, so details like price, quantity produced, and whether the design files are public remain unknown.
