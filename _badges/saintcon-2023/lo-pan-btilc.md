The Lo-Pan BTiLC is a SAINTCON minibadge by maker Sh33pr0ck depicting Lo Pan, the sorcerer villain of the 1986 film *Big Trouble in Little China* (the "BTiLC" in the title). Two LEDs sit behind the character's eyes; the maker's own soldering note calls for a dot of hot glue over both the LEDs and the eye holes on the back of the board to diffuse the light for a brighter glow effect.

It is a personal, one-off style piece rather than a numbered production run: the community sheet records it under the "Personal" category with "Super Rare" rarity and no board-house or quantity information, and its listed soldering difficulty is "Beginner." The maker did not sell it; the sheet's "how to acquire" field simply says to trade with them or ask for one, consistent with the informal, in-person swap culture common to SAINTCON minibadges.

No independent maker page, repository, or storefront listing for this badge was found beyond its own entry in the minibadge.wiki community database, so technical details such as the exact LED part number, PCB shape and color, and any design files remain unconfirmed.
