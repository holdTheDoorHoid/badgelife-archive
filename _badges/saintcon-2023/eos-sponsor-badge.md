The EOS Sponsor Badge is a SAINTCON 2023 minibadge made by Jup1t3r (a longtime SAINTCON minibadge designer) to represent EOS, one of that year's conference sponsors. Like the other sponsor minibadges in the 2023 lineup, it was distributed for free to attendees who visited the EOS booth in the expo area, and it follows the same basic construction as the other 2023 sponsor badges: hand-soldered LEDs and a resistor added to a pre-populated board, then header pins for mounting on a standard minibadge holder.

Design specifics beyond the sponsor branding are not documented in the sources found; the maker's own text simply notes "EOS is another one of our great sponsors this year." No price, quantity produced, or hardware/firmware files were published for it, consistent with most SAINTCON sponsor minibadges, which are one-off promotional giveaways rather than commercial products.
