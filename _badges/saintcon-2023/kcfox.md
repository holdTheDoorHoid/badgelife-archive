KCfox is a SAINTCON 2023 "Personal" category minibadge made by Sh33pr0ck, celebrating the Kansas City Chiefs. It is a simple, beginner-level solder kit built around a single LED (D1); the board's back silkscreen instructs the builder to orient the LED's green dot/line toward the enclosed side of the footprint.

The maker did not sell the badge; per the community listing, it was obtained by trading with Sh33pr0ck directly or simply asking for one. The listing marks its rarity as "Super Rare," though no firm production count was published (the community database's quantity field reads 0, which appears to reflect that the maker never reported a number rather than a confirmed print run of zero).

No hardware files, firmware, board-house, or pricing information were published for this badge, and no maker page or repository could be found beyond the community-run minibadge.wiki record.
