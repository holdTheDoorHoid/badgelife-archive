The F.I.B. Tamper Challenge Badge is a SAINTCON 2023 contest minibadge, designed by SHIFTY and given out through the Tamper Evident Community's "F.I.B. Tamper Challenge" -- a hands-on game (organized by Amaltheia and Chucktesta) in which players sign up for a Beginner or Advanced tier, then try to break into a tamper-evident package, alter what's inside, and put it back together without leaving detectable evidence. The board is rated "Uncommon" rarity in the official minibadge guide and was earned simply by signing up for the challenge at the Tamper Evident Community's booth during the conference (October 24-27, 2023).

It's a small hand-solder kit: an FR4 PCB with two 1206 LEDs (D1, D2), two 1206 resistors (R1, R2), and 2-pin headers, assembled using the single-pad hand-soldering method common to SAINTCON minibadges. The front carries a red "TAMPER EVIDENT" void-stamp graphic; the back is silkscreened "2023 SAINTCON SHIFTY" alongside the component footprints.

This is a separate board from the similarly-named "Tamper Evident Community Badge," also designed by SHIFTY for SAINTCON 2023 -- that one represents the community itself, while this one is specific to that year's F.I.B. contest.
