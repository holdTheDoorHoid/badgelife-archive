Family Night - Lock Pick is a 2023 SAINTCON minibadge designed by TGalvez to represent the Lock Pick Community's activity at SAINTCON's Family Night event, an evening track aimed at attendees' families rather than the main con crowd. The badge itself is a small PCB with a lock-and-keyhole graphic on the front and Family Night branding, TGalvez's sunflower signature, and a "design by ZEVLAG 2023" credit on the back.

Electronically it is one of the simplest minibadges in the SAINTCON ecosystem: a single through-hole LED and one current-limiting resistor, soldered on the back of the board with enough standoff that the LED can be bent to shine outward, plus 4x 2-position pass-through headers on the front so it can be chained with other minibadges on a lanyard or holder. It was not sold; attendees earned it by attempting to pick a lock at the Family Night activity, and TGalvez kept a supply on hand afterward for anyone who missed the event but still wanted the full Family Night set (which also included Nerf Blaster and Rocket Building minibadges).
