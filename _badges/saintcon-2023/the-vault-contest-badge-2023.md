"The Vault" was SAINTCON 2023's physical-penetration-test contest, challenging attendees to bypass locks, doors, and safes of increasing difficulty for points before the conference ended. This minibadge, designed by SHIFTY, was one of the free contest minibadges handed out to attendees who signed up and took part -- rated "Beginner" difficulty and "Common" rarity in the official minibadge guide, distinguishing it from harder/rarer badges in the same year's set.

Electronically it is minimal: a small FR4 PCB with two 1206 SMD LEDs (D1, D2), a single 1206 resistor (R1), and 2-pin headers, assembled with the hand-solder single-pad method used across SAINTCON's 2023 minibadge line. The front artwork is a padlock dial and crossed pickaxe motif over "VAULT CHALLENGE" text in dark silkscreen on a white board; the back repeats "SAINTCON VAULT CHALLENGE SHIFTY" alongside the two LEDs and resistor.

This is a separate badge from the archive's existing 2021 "THE VAULT" entry -- same contest name reused in a different year, with its own board design.
