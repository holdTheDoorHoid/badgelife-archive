Marvthegrate is a personal minibadge that rogu3bull3t made for SAINTCON 2023, styled after the avatar they use online in various communities. By the maker's own description it "is just hubris" and includes a nod to a hobby they're known for. It was distributed by hand at the convention, reportedly near the scavenger hunt booth, sometimes in trade for a joke or some other small bit of fun rather than sold outright.

The listing marks the badge as beginner-friendly to solder and rare, and the maker provided a soldering jig alongside the parts for anyone who preferred not to hand-solder the leads. A separate video walkthrough of the soldering process is linked from the listing. No chip, LED count, colors, or production quantity are documented in the source, and no repository or storefront for the design was found.

The badge sits in a small in-joke chain with two other SAINTCON 2023 minibadges from the same community sheet: Berly's "Marvin the Martian" and Ray-Man's "K-9," made as a companion piece once Ray-Man saw Berly would be bringing a Marvin badge to the con.
