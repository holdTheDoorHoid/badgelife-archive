The 2024 NolaCon badge was the first entry in "Voodoo Heart," a three-year badge series built by the con's badge maker (credited as CylentK on Twitter and cyl3ntkn1ght on Instagram, identified via GitHub as Jack Lambert) as NolaCon's annual Learn-to-Solder kit. NolaCon previewed it on Instagram in April 2024, showing only a cropped corner of the badge and noting that "all badges are in hand and ready for the con" the following month.

The series continued in 2025 with a second badge named "Patches," whose own page describes the 2024 badge as "last year's badge" and promises a third, final piece to complete the set. No source found gives the 2024 badge its own name, full product photo, price, production quantity, or confirmed technical specs; the maker's public GitHub repo for the series' LED firmware (an APA106 addressable-LED sequence) is undated to a specific year and was not attributed to this badge without confirmation.

## History

First of three planned badges in NolaCon's "Voodoo Heart" series, each built for the con's Learn-to-Solder activity by the same badge maker. Followed by "Patches" (NolaCon 2025, badge #2).
