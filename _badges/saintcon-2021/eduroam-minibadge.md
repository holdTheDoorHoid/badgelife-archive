Eduroam is a global federation of education and research network operators that lets students, staff, and researchers get WiFi access at any participating institution using their home credentials. At SAINTCON, an Eduroam-branded minibadge has been handed out as an unofficial/booth item rather than sold, typically by talking to a sponsor or state education-network (UETN) contact rather than picking it up from a table.

The archive's discovery sweep found "Eduroam" named among SAINTCON 2021's unofficial and booth minibadges on the badge.gallery archive page, but that page gives no maker, image, or technical detail for the 2021 edition specifically. Separately, the community-run MiniBadge Wiki documents an "Eduroam Minibadge" recurring in 2022 (maker handle "Jup1t3r") and 2023 (maker handle "EDU-ROMEO"), each with its own PCB artwork, LEDs, and resistors soldered by the recipient, distributed the same way (find the right UETN/Eduroam contact and ask). No 2021-specific record with a photo or maker name has surfaced, so this entry documents what is confirmed for 2021 (that it existed and was distributed informally) while leaving design and technical fields empty rather than assuming they matched later years.
