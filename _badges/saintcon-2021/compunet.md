CompuNet is a SAINTCON 2021 minibadge designed by Jup1t3r, part of the community-driven minibadge tradition at the SAINTCON security conference in Utah. The small square PCB carries the "CompuNet" name in a rounded font beneath a large stylized copper-colored swoosh that reads as a logo mark rather than a literal object, set against a blue back panel.

Electrically it is a simple beginner-level build: two surface-mount LEDs (D1 and D2) driven through a single shared resistor (R1), with no microcontroller, display, or wireless features. The maker's soldering guide calls out orienting each LED by a green dot marking, which points to reverse-mount chip LEDs rather than standard top-emitting ones.

Beyond the minibadge.wiki community listing (which itself pulls from a JSON data export rather than a live page), no separate project page, repository, or storefront for CompuNet could be found, so pricing, quantity made, and availability remain unknown.
