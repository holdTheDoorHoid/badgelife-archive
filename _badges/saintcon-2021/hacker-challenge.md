The HACKER CHALLENGE minibadge is one of a large set of SAINTCON 2021 minibadges credited to a maker known as Jup1t3r on the community-run MiniBadge Wiki. Like the others in that year's set (PEOPLE BADGES, RED TEAM, THE VAULT, SCAVENGER HUNT, and more), it was distributed as a small solder-it-yourself kit: two through-hole LEDs on the front (recommended blue over orange) and one on the back, plus three resistors of differing values.

What sets this badge apart from its siblings is a small EPROM chip on the board. The listing's soldering notes say attendees who finished assembling the badge could bring it to the Hacker Challenge booth at SAINTCON to have the EPROM programmed, tying the physical solder project to that track's on-site challenge rather than to a purely decorative or blinky build.

No price, quantity produced, or acquisition details beyond "distributed as a kit at the con" were found; the maker does not appear to have a separate storefront, repository, or profile page linked from the wiki, and "Jup1t3r" could not be traced to a real name or other public profile.
