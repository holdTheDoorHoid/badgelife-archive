An unofficial minibadge from SAINTCON 2021's minibadge ecosystem, distributed at the WiCyS (Women in CyberSecurity) Utah community's booth rather than through the conference's official minibadge program. Like most SAINTCON minibadges of this era, it is credited to designer Jup1t3r. The artwork is a black, white, and gold PCB-silkscreen illustration of a silhouetted woman with flowing hair, captioned "Hack Like a Girl," tying the badge to WiCyS's mission of encouraging women in cybersecurity.

The archived official SAINTCON 2021 MiniBadges page describes acquisition simply as visiting the WiCyS booth space during the conference — it was a free give-away tied to the group's in-person presence at SAINTCON, not a sold or limited-run item as far as any source states. No pricing, print quantity, or technical specifications (microcontroller, LEDs, display) were found in any source, and none should be assumed; the badge may be an unpowered PCB piece rather than an electronic minibadge, but this was not explicitly confirmed.
