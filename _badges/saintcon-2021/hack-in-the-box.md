"HACK IN THE BOX" is a solder-it-yourself minibadge designed by Jup1t3r for SAINTCON 2021. The front carries a black cactus silhouette on a red PCB, with LED pads worked into the outline of the cactus's arms — green LEDs down one side, blue down the other — driven by a small logic inverter and a pair of resistors (62ohm and 10ohm) silkscreened on the board. The back carries the badge's name and a black-and-white court jester's cap, playing on "Hack in the Box" as both a name and a jester-in-a-box pun.

The maker's own build notes describe it as an "Advanced" solder job: the inverter is best done with hot air and solder paste, and the LEDs are mounted in matched blue/green pairs with attention to orientation. Beyond the community database record and its two board photos, no independent storefront, repo, or write-up for this badge was found, so its price, quantity made, and distribution at SAINTCON 2021 remain undocumented.
