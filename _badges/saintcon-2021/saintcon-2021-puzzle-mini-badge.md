A minibadge from SAINTCON 2021, seen only in a short build-video titled "SAINTCON 2021 Puzzle mini-badge how to" from the YouTube channel Soldering&3Dprinting (Herushan1). The video shows an orange PCB with a triangular eye motif and a matching white square top piece being assembled with hand tools, but carries no written description, so its actual name, designer, and function are not confirmed from the video page itself.

The badge does not appear among the 15 minibadges in SAINTCON's own 2021 build guide or in the community-maintained minibadge.wiki catalog for that year, which suggests it was an unofficial or booth-distributed design rather than one of the con's official releases. The same YouTube channel is later credited as the maker of a "Cham Cham Cham" puzzle minibadge at SAINTCON 2026, which points toward Herushan1 being a designer of puzzle-style minibadges in their own right rather than only a documentarian of other people's builds — but that connection is circumstantial and not stated on this video's own page.

No pricing, quantity, distribution, or technical details (chip, LEDs, display) could be found for this item. badge.gallery's "Unofficial and booth MiniBadges" page for SAINTCON 2021 is now reachable but turns out to be a summary page that points back to the same official archive already checked, rather than a badge-by-badge catalog — it does not name this item either.
