The HHC (Level 1) mini-badge is a SAINTCON 2021 minibadge tied to the con's Hardware Hacking Community (HHC), the group that runs the conference's soldering and assembly activities. It appears to be the first rung of a three-level soldering series alongside a "Level 2" badge (nicknamed "crossed irons") and a "Level 3" badge (nicknamed "3 Fairies"), each documented in its own build video by the same third-party YouTube channel, Soldering&3Dprinting. None of the three show up in SAINTCON's official 2021 MINIBADGE-Guide PDF or in the community-run Minibadge Wiki's 2021 dataset, both of which only cover that year's 15 themed badges (PEOPLE BADGES, HACK IN THE BOX, HACKER CHALLENGE, and so on) — so this level series looks like a separate, likely HHC-booth-specific set rather than part of the main annual badge lineup.

Beyond the fact that it exists and was built by at least one attendee on camera, little else could be confirmed. The maker/designer, price, quantity made, and technical details (MCU, LEDs, power) are not stated anywhere found, and the source video's thumbnail shows only a hand soldering loose parts rather than the assembled board, so no photo could be sourced for this entry.
