THE VAULT is a minibadge Jup1t3r made for SAINTCON 2021, part of that year's community minibadge set documented on minibadge.wiki. It is a beginner-difficulty soldering kit: builders place two LEDs (D1 and D2, each oriented by a green dot silkscreen marker) and a single non-polarized resistor onto the board.

The graphic side carries "VAULT CHALLENGE" branding with artwork of a masked, helmeted warrior holding a polearm beside what reads as a bank vault or safe door, suggesting the badge was tied to some kind of on-site challenge or puzzle at SAINTCON that year, though no further description of that challenge was found. The solder side carries the SAINTCON 2021 wordmark and the designer's stylized "JUP1T3R" signature alongside the component footprints.

No maker page, repository, storefront listing, or press coverage for this specific badge turned up beyond the minibadge.wiki community record, so price, quantity made, distribution method, and current availability are unknown.
