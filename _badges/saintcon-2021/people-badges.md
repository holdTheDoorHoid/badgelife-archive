PEOPLE BADGES is a SAINTCON 2021 minibadge made by Jup1t3r, part of the community-submitted minibadge tradition at SAINTCON (Utah's security conference). The board carries two LEDs (labeled D1 and D2) near its center, wired through a single shared resistor with no polarity to worry about, and the community wiki's soldering notes rate assembly as "Intermediate" difficulty — largely because of the LED orientation (green dot toward the top of the board) rather than any complex circuitry.

Beyond the physical build details, little else is documented: the submission on minibadge.wiki left the description, artwork theme, price, quantity made, board house, and acquisition method all blank, and no maker page, storefront, or press coverage for "Jup1t3r" or this specific badge could be located. The front and back photos on the wiki are the only visual record found.
