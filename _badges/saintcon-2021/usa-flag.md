The USA FLAG is a SAINTCON 2021 minibadge by maker Jup1t3r, part of the SAINTCON minibadge tradition where attendees collect and swap small PCB badges through the week-long conference. The board is cut and silkscreened to resemble the American flag, with a blue canton bearing white stars in the corner and red-and-white stripes filling the rest of the board.

It is a simple beginner-level soldering kit: two through-hole LEDs mount behind the canton (with the green cathode indicator oriented down and to the left, per the maker's assembly guide) along with a single non-polarized resistor. No microcontroller, display, or connectivity is present — it is a passive, LED-only badge with no SAO header.

No maker page, storefront listing, or press coverage was found beyond the community-run minibadge.wiki database, so pricing, quantity made, and how it was distributed at SAINTCON remain unknown.
