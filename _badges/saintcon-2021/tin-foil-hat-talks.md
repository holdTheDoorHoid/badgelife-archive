TIN FOIL HAT TALKS is a SAINTCON 2021 learn-to-solder minibadge designed by Jup1t3r, one of several minibadges the same designer made for that year's con (alongside PEOPLE BADGES, PRIVATE LTE, RFID/NFC BADGE, HACK IN THE BOX, and RED TEAM). It is a simple beginner-difficulty kit: a small green PCB carrying two reverse-mount LEDs (D1, D2) and a single non-polarized resistor (R1), with no microcontroller or other active electronics — its purpose is a soldering exercise rather than an interactive device.

The board art depicts an alien figure and a pair of hands framing a stylized "Z" logo on the front ("DESIGNED BY JUP1T3R"), and on the back an armored alien holding a shield emblem reading "TFHT" above "MMXXI" (2021). The name "Tin Foil Hat Talks" appears to tie the badge to a SAINTCON talk track or theme of that name rather than describing a standalone product.

No pricing, quantity made, or distribution details were published on the community sheet this entry came from, and no other page describing the badge could be found, so those fields are left blank rather than guessed.
