This is Level 2 of a tiered SAINTCON 2021 challenge minibadge nicknamed "crossed irons" for the X-shaped cutout/silhouette on its small square PCB. It surfaces only through a hobbyist build-guide video ("SAINTCON 2021 Level 2 mini-badge how to (crossed irons)", uploaded October 17, 2021 by the YouTube channel Soldering&3Dprinting), which walks through hand-soldering the board; the video's thumbnail shows a teal PCB badge with an X-shaped white silhouette being hand-soldered, with another (unrelated, orange) minibadge visible in the background.

SAINTCON minibadges are typically community-designed, solder-it-yourself boards traded at the con, and this one appears to belong to a multi-level challenge series (a "Level 1" companion is plausible but was not found). No maker's name, storefront listing, schematic, or bill of materials could be located, and the badge does not appear in the community-run minibadge.wiki catalog for 2021, so its designer and distribution details remain unknown.
