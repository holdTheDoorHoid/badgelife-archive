The Gadsden Flag minibadge is a SAINTCON 2021 minibadge by maker Jup1t3r, styled after the "Don't Tread on Me" Gadsden Flag design (a coiled rattlesnake). It was distributed as a simple soldering kit: the build guide calls for two LEDs and a single non-polarized resistor, with the LEDs placed so a green-marked indicator faces down and to the left. MiniBadge Wiki rates the soldering difficulty as beginner, consistent with SAINTCON's minibadge series, which are typically small learn-to-solder boards traded among attendees.

No description, price, quantity made, board house, or distribution details were recorded in the available source, and no separate storefront, repo, or maker profile for Jup1t3r turned up. The record's front and back photos are the only detailed evidence of the badge's actual design.
