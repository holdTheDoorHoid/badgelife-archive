The BIC Pick is a commemorative badge made by Blacks in Cyber (BiC) for DEF CON 33, marking the five-year anniversary of the BIC Village. It is shaped like an Afro pick, described by the makers as a tribute to "the bold, unapologetic pride that has defined our space since day one." It was made available for purchase and trade on-site at the village during DEF CON 33, with the maker's page noting that off-site sales would be announced separately.

Per the community badge sheet, the badge doubles as an SAO: it carries 18 NeoPixel-style LEDs and 6 SAO ports, so it can host other SAOs, plug into a host badge as an SAO itself, or communicate with other badges. It includes CTF and cipher-analysis challenges tied to the official BIC CTF tooling, and part of the badge is meant to be soldered together by the owner as a soldering-skills exercise. None of the hardware specifics in that description (exact MCU, LED part number) could be independently confirmed from the maker's own materials, so the technical fields are left blank pending a source that states them directly.

A YouTube video titled "DEF CON 33 - BiC Village - B I C Pick DEF CON 33 Badge Walkthrough" by Eli McRae appears to cover the badge's design in more depth, but its content could not be retrieved as text for this pass.
