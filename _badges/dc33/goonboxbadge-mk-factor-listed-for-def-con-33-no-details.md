GoonBoxBadge, credited on the sheet as "MK Factor," is listed among the makers expected at DEF CON 33 (2025), but the community sheet carries no title, description, image, or link for whatever they actually brought that year -- only the maker's name.

GoonBoxBadge is a returning badgelife maker with two prior entries in this archive: a Goon Box Badge at DEF CON 26 (2018; the archive's unresearched stub describes it as a sonic screwdriver with a TV-B-Gone) and the Cassandra SAO at DEF CON 32 (2024, a backlit flex-PCB SAO "for our Whovians," sold as a $20 kit). Their known public presence is the @GoonBoxBadge account on Twitter/X, which the DEF CON 32 sheet listing told buyers to watch for drops during the con. That account could not be reached during this pass (x.com returns an HTTP 402 error to automated fetches), and no web search turned up a DC33-specific project page, storefront listing, or press mention for GoonBoxBadge or "MK Factor." What this maker showed at DEF CON 33 remains unconfirmed.
