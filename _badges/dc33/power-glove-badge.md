The Power Glove Badge is a novelty electronic badge from GizmoLab, a maker based in Fossombrone, Italy, styled after the Nintendo Power Glove peripheral from the 1989 film "The Wizard." It carries a 0.91" OLED screen and three tactile buttons (black, blue, and red) that cycle through interactive messages on the display. The badge ships with a 3D-printed protective case and requires a rechargeable LIR2032 coin cell (3.6V) to run standalone, or can be powered through its SAO connector.

GizmoLab sells the badge through their Tindie store for $60, with an optional USB-C LIR2032 charger available separately for $3. As of research date the listing was still active with a handful of units in stock, shipped worldwide from Italy. No schematics, firmware, or design files appear to be published for this badge, and the maker's other public pages (Instagram, Linktree) describe general electronics/maker work rather than badge-specific history, so details like the MCU, LED count, and exact SAO header version could not be confirmed from available sources.
