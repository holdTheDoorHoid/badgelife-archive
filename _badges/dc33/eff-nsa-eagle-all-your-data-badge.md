The "All Your Data" badge is a PCB badge shaped after the Electronic Frontier
Foundation's parody of the NSA's official seal: a bald eagle wearing
headphones, clutching cables instead of arrows and olives, over a striped
shield and the words "ALL YOUR DATA" in place of the agency's motto. Two LEDs
glow red as the eagle's eyes. It was made and sold by Verbaal and H4r0ld,
running as H4r0ld & V3rbaal's House Of Hacker Horrors, at DEF CON 33 (LVCC
West Hall, Las Vegas, Thursday, August 7, 2025), with proceeds going to the
EFF. Tickets were sold through Eventbrite for $60; that listing shows sales
have since ended.

The same maker pair had previously listed a badge at DEF CON 32 and later
reran this exact "All Your Data" design at DEF CON 34, alongside a new
"Electric Fist" badge and a rerun of their earlier "Privacy Badger" design —
suggesting this is one entry in an ongoing yearly series of EFF-benefit
badges rather than a one-off.
