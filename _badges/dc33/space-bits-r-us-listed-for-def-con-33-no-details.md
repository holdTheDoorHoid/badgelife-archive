The community badge sheet's 2025 expected-makers tab lists "Space Bits 'R Us" with no further details -- no title, description, price, or link. No independent record of a 2025 (DEF CON 33) item from this maker could be found.

The name is documented from two years earlier: at DEF CON 31 (2023) "Space Bits R Us" was the team name adopted by a group of Hack-A-Sat satellite-hacking competitors (project lead Scott Almond), who built themselves an exclusive team badge (not sold to the public) and separately sold a "Space Pony" SAO at the Aerospace Village (see the dc31-space-pony-sao entry). A parallel sheet row for DEF CON 32 (2024) under the same maker name was researched previously and likewise turned up no public item. Whether this team returned for DEF CON 33 with new hardware, and what it was, is not established by any source found during this pass.
