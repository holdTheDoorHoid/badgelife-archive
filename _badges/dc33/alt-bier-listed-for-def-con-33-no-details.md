Alt_Bier (Richard Gowen) is a long-running #badgelife maker from the DFW area, known for a badge nearly every year since 2018 (LAN Tap, Akira, Godzilla vs. Blade Runner, 84, 3d6, Astable, Hom3c0ming, Future Badge, Cowboys vs Dinosaurs, and the DEF CON 32 "G0dzilla VS" badge). The community sheet lists them as an expected maker for DEF CON 33 (2025), but the row carries no title or details.

Research could not confirm what Alt_Bier released for DEF CON 33. Their DEF CON Social posts from 6–7 August 2025 show them at the con wearing a blinky badge and giving away "#badgelife goodies" in the linecon line, but nothing names the item. Their own maker hub (altbier.us) keeps what looks like a complete badge history, and it currently stops at their 2024 work — no 2025/DC33 project appears there, and the eBay store had no listings when checked. This entry is left as unknown rather than guessing; it should be revisited if a DEF CON 33 project by this maker surfaces later.
