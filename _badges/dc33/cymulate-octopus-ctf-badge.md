The Cymulate Octopus CTF Badge is a die-cut, illustrated octopus badge made by Abhinav SP of Hackerware.io for the security company Cymulate, handed out exclusively at Cymulate's booth at Black Hat USA 2025. A printed octopus - blue body, crimson tentacle undersides - sits above a banner carrying the Cymulate logo and four tactile buttons labeled MODE, CTF, 1, and 0.

Gameplay is built around the binary switches: holding the CTF button and entering a flag as a string of 1s and 0s lights up one more tentacle tip per correct answer, with the stated goal of "making the cyber kraken alive" by awakening every limb. A separate MODE button cycles the idle LED blink pattern. The maker's page does not disclose the microcontroller, exact LED count, unit price, or quantity produced, and no open hardware or firmware was found for it.

It appears on the DEF CON 33 community badge sheet, but the maker's own copy ties it to Black Hat 2025 rather than DEF CON - plausible given the two conferences' back-to-back Las Vegas scheduling and Hackerware's badges appearing at both.
