The "Blacks In Cyber Village Badge" entry, as filled in from the maker's own materials, refers to the same item documented elsewhere in this archive as the "BIC Pick": an Afro-pick-shaped badge made by Blacks In Cybersecurity (BiC) for the fifth anniversary of the B.I.C. Village at DEF CON 33 (2025). The maker describes it as a tribute to "our roots and the bold, unapologetic pride" of the community, evoking a pick that has "combed through challenges" alongside its members.

It was sold and traded on-site at the B.I.C. Village during DEF CON 33 rather than through general pre-registration or an online storefront; the maker's page said off-site sales would be "announced in the near future," but no later confirmation of that turned up. Blacks In Cyber also runs a separate Square merchandise store listing generic "PCB 'Blinky' Badges," but nothing on that store ties a distinct product to this village-badge entry, and its product details are not reachable without a JavaScript-capable browser.

No microcontroller, LED, display, or SAO-header specifications were published for this item by the maker, so those fields are left blank rather than guessed. Because the title, maker, event, price, and source page all match the archive's existing `dc33-bic-pick` and `dc33-bic-pick-sao` entries, this is very likely the same physical badge recorded a third time from the community sheet.
