If I Had One SAO is a joke Simple Add-On sold by the maker Penzz through their Uberflux storefront for DEF CON 33 (2025). Rather than housing real electronics, its silkscreen riffs on the "Ancient Aliens" meme, showing the wild-haired conspiracy-theorist figure pointing at a blank chip footprint next to the caption "This is where I'd put my MCU... if it wasn't for tariffs!" — a wry nod to the tariff-driven component shortages and price hikes affecting hobbyist electronics in 2025.

The board itself is a one-sided PCB with three pre-soldered top-view blue LEDs and three resistors, powered off the host badge's SAO header, so the only "function" is the LEDs lighting up when plugged in — there is no microcontroller on board. It ships with a shrouded male SAO 1.69bis (6-pin) connector and a lanyard connector, and sold for $10 through Uberflux, where it remained listed as in stock at last check.
