The Floridaman 2025 Badge is the paid admission credential for "Floridaman," a Florida-themed unofficial party held during DEF CON 33 week (2025) in Las Vegas and ticketed through floridaman.party, whose 2025 homepage said the badge "grants you one night" of the party. The community sheet lists joehacksalot as the badge's creator. The community sheet lists it at "$130/$180," most likely two ticket tiers, and notes that admission includes an open bar, with a house rule against photography without permission.

Beyond the party's own ticketing page, no independent source — maker page, GitHub, Hackaday.io, storefront, or press — could be found describing the badge as a piece of electronics (no confirmed MCU, LEDs, or display), so those fields are left blank rather than guessed. The floridaman.party site has since been replaced with a placeholder for its 2026 edition, and its 2025 "/badges" page was never captured by web archives, so this entry rests mainly on the original community sheet.
