d3fufu, made under the handle "DC Labubu," is a DEF CON 33 blind-bag solder kit: a small PCB SAO or badge shaped like a "Labubu"-style monster mascot, styled after the popular collectible toy line. It was sold for $80 as a mystery pack, with six common color variants and one rarer "chaser" color, so buyers didn't know which colorway they'd get until opening it — a format borrowed from blind-box collectible toys.

Beyond the community sheet entry, the only source located was the maker's own X/Twitter account (@d3fufu), whose bio tags the item under "#defcon #badgelife #labubu" and whose profile photo shows several of the finished badges side by side in different metallic colorways (blue, purple, and green were visible), each with a small "DEFCON" wordmark on the body. No Hackaday.io page, GitHub repository, storefront, or press writeup could be found, and the maker's tweets describing build details (chip, LED type/count, quantity produced) were not reachable without a logged-in Twitter session. As a result, technical specifics and current availability remain unconfirmed.
