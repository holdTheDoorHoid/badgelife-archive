Ironwood Cyber (also credited as "Ironwood Cyber Team") is a maker group that produced
badges for three consecutive DEF CONs: the TRON Badge at DC30 (2022), the Reactor Badge
(also called the Arc Reactor Mark II) at DC31 (2023), and an unnamed badge announced for
DC32 (2024) that added touch controls, audio, and haptic feedback. The community sheet
for 2025 lists them again as an expected maker for DEF CON 33, but with no title,
description, or links attached to the row.

No trace of a DC33 badge could be found. The team's GitHub organization
(github.com/Ironwood-Cyber) has not published a badge repository newer than the DC30
hardware share, and the Discord servers previously used for the DC31 and DC32 badge
companion games and mini-CTFs have both been renamed "SplinterOps" (formerly
"IronwoodCyber SummerGames"), with no visible mention of a 2025 badge. Separately, the
ironwoodcyber.com domain is now home to a cybersecurity company selling an autonomous
penetration-testing platform called Enlight; that company's documentation happens to be
hosted on the same GitHub Pages account that stores the DC30 badge hardware, which is the
only evidence connecting the two, but it does not establish that this company (rather
than the badge team specifically) had anything to do with DEF CON 33. Whether Ironwood
Cyber actually brought a badge to DC33, and if so what it was, remains unconfirmed.
