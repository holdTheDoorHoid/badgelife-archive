AND!XOR is a longtime DEF CON badge-making crew whose badges have drawn Hackaday
hands-on coverage from DEF CON 26 through 28. Their community-sheet entries in
this archive show a habit of placeholder titles: "???" for DC31 (later revealed
as 5n4ck3y-7r) and "Who really knows... just buy the damn thing" for DC32.

This DC33 (2025) row follows the same pattern: the sheet lists the title as
"REDACTED," the functions field as "MATT DAMON," and a contact email of
matt@damon.com. Nothing on AND!XOR's own site (which stops at the DC30 badge),
their Hackaday.io profile (last project DC29), their Shopify store, or Hackaday
press coverage identifies what, if anything, the crew released for DC33, so
the badge's real name, hardware, price, and availability remain unknown.
