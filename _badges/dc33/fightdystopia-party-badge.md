BigTaro's Badges listed a retro-TV-themed CTF badge for DEF CON 33 (2025) on the community badge sheet under the name FightDystopia Party Badge. According to that listing, it plays a collection of videos and GIFs, carries a CTF, and may detect certain signals from certain people nearby and let the wearer know they are around. The listing says the badge funds the Fight Dystopia party, and that a price and website were still to come when it was submitted in July 2025.

No independent confirmation of the badge's design, technical specs, price, or availability could be found. BigTaro's Badges has several other entries in this archive for the same event (Frogstar Badge, Gunslinger-B-Gone and Sheriff SAO, Submarine Badge and Pirate Flag SAO), but no maker page or storefront for this specific item was located.
