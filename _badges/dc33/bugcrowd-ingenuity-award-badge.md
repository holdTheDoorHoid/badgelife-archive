Bugcrowd's INGENUITY AWARD badge was made for DEF CON 33 (2025) by Abhinav SP of Hackerware.io, the same India-based studio behind several other DC33 badges (the Amazon Playing Card badge, ARCANUM Gospel Book badge, Cymulate Octopus CTF badge, F5 CTF badge, and others). Unlike those, this one was not sold or handed out on the floor — the community badge sheet notes it was "not sold, to be earned," suggesting Bugcrowd used it as a recognition award for a researcher or contest winner rather than general con swag.

Despite checking Hackerware.io's own site and attempting the maker's Hackster.io project pages (which returned Cloudflare blocks on every attempt), no dedicated write-up, photo, or technical breakdown of this specific badge could be located. Hackerware went on to build a separate "Bug Bounty Village" badge for Bugcrowd at DEF CON 34 (2026), but that is a distinct, later project and should not be conflated with this one. The sheet's own description, "Blinky with acrylic," is the only characterization available.
