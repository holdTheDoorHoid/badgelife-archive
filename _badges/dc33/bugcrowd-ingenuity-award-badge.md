Bugcrowd's INGENUITY AWARD badge is listed on the DEF CON 33 (2025) community badge sheet as made by Abhinav SP of Hackerware.io, the India-based studio that lists Bugcrowd as a client and is credited on the same sheet with several other DC33-era badges (the Amazon Playing Card badge, ARCANUM Gospel Book badge, Cymulate Octopus CTF badge and F5 CTF badge among them). Unlike those, this one was not sold — the sheet notes it was "not sold, to be earned," which points to an award rather than general con swag.

Despite checking Hackerware.io's own site and attempting the maker's Hackster.io project pages (which returned Cloudflare blocks on every attempt), no dedicated write-up, photo, or technical breakdown of this specific badge could be located. Hackerware went on to build a separate "Bug Bounty Village" badge for DEF CON 34 (2026), but that page does not mention Bugcrowd and it is a distinct, later project that should not be conflated with this one. The sheet's own description, "Blinky with acrylic," is the only characterization available.
