Recon Village's DEF CON 33 badge, "The Cyber Owl," is a badge built around a cyberpunk owl illustration under the year's "Cyber Vigilance" theme: intricate mechanical linework, a metallic blue-gray finish, and glowing pink LED eyes standing in for the owl's watchfulness, a nod to the reconnaissance and OSINT focus of the village. It continues a yearly line of one-off Recon Village badges that have included a soldier badge (DC32), a spy-silhouette badge with a speaker variant (DC31), a radar-themed badge (DC30), and a steampunk skull badge (DC27).

The community badge sheet for DC33 lists this item as a "Puzzle Prize," suggesting it was awarded to attendees who completed a challenge in the village rather than sold or freely handed out, but neither Recon Village's site nor any source found in this pass describes the puzzle itself, how many badges were made, or what electronics are inside beyond the LED eyes. No storefront listing, repo, or Hackaday.io project for this badge turned up in the sources checked.
