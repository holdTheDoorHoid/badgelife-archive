The DEF CON 33 community badge sheet listed "Florida Man" as an expected maker for 2025 with no further detail. Floridaman is the group behind an annual Florida-themed party held during Hacker Summer Camp in Las Vegas, and has produced a badge-as-admission-ticket for the party in prior years (see the DC31 entry, "The Party Badge"). A Wayback Machine snapshot of floridaman.party from August 3, 2025 confirms a 2025 edition: the homepage says the badge grants one night of entry during Hacker Summer Camp 2025 in Las Vegas, and a "Get Your Badge" button points to a `/badges` page. The page's hero image is a pink flamingo badge design whose file is named "Floyd-the-flamingo-badge-clear.svg", which is the only source for the name "Floyd."

The `/badges` sale page was never captured by the Wayback Machine, and the live site has since been replaced with 2026 party content. As a result, this entry can confirm the badge's purpose but not its price, quantity, materials, or whether it carries any electronics. It is treated here as a `badge` (an admission item, in the vein of the DC31 Party Badge) rather than an SAO.

The flamingo vector artwork could not be added to this archive, since the image-saving tool used here does not decode SVGs. Anyone revisiting this entry should look for a raster photo of the physical badge, plus pricing and distribution details, on the maker's social accounts or at future snapshots of floridaman.party.
