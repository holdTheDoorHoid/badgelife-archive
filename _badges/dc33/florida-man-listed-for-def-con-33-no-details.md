The DEF CON 33 community badge sheet listed "Florida Man" as an expected maker for 2025 with no further detail. Florida Man is the group behind an annual Florida-themed party held during Hacker Summer Camp in Las Vegas, and has produced a badge-as-admission-ticket for the party in prior years (see the DC31 entry, "The Party Badge"). Their own site, floridaman.party, confirms a 2025 edition and names that year's badge "Floyd the Flamingo," with a flamingo-character graphic used as the site's hero art and a "Get Your Badge" link pointing to a `/badges` page.

Because floridaman.party is a client-rendered single-page app, the badge sale page itself was not captured by web crawls at the time of this research, and the site has since been overwritten with 2026 party content. As a result, this entry could confirm the badge's name and purpose but not its price, quantity, materials, or whether it carries any electronics. It is treated here as a `badge` (an admission item, in the vein of the DC31 Party Badge) rather than an SAO.

A vector artwork file for the badge design ("Floyd-the-flamingo-badge-clear.svg") was located via the Wayback Machine but could not be added to this archive, since the image-saving tool used here does not decode SVGs. Anyone revisiting this entry should look for a raster photo of the physical badge, plus pricing and distribution details, on the maker's social accounts or at future snapshots of floridaman.party.
