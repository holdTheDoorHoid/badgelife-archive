Keyport, maker of the modular "Pivot" key organizer, released this DEF CON 33 edition of its MOCA 10-in-1 tool as convention-exclusive merchandise. The MOCA is a flat stainless-steel keychain insert about 2.7 inches long that packs ten everyday-carry functions into a single slim piece: a bottle opener, flat-head screwdriver, three wrenches, a ruler, a pry bar, a box cutter, and a cord cutter, all in a form factor compatible with Keyport's Pivot key organizer. The DEF CON 33 version is laser-etched with DEF CON branding rather than Keyport's standard markings.

It was sold as a standalone item and also bundled into a larger seven-piece "DEF CON 33 Everything Bundle" alongside a matching DEF CON 33 Pivot 2.0 (itself etched with the con's "Access Everywhere" circuit-board-style artwork), faceplates, a YubiStyle cover, and other Keyport accessories. Like the rest of the DC33 Keyport line, it is a mechanical accessory rather than an electronic badge or SAO -- there is no PCB, MCU, or lighting involved.
