The AI Jack Badge is technick's DEF CON 33 entry in an ongoing series of 3D-printed "Jack" collectible badges that has run since New Jack at DC27, followed by Monopoly Jack (DC29), King Jack (DC30), Pirate Jack (DC31), and an F CAESARS-themed Jack at DC32. Each year reworks the same punk-styled character in a new costume; for DC33 the theme was AI meeting the natural world, described by the maker as "a fusion of silicon and soil, circuitry and spirit."

The badge has no electronics - it is a glow-in-the-dark 3D print, sold with a lanyard, stickers, and occasional bonus loot items while supplies lasted. Buyers could choose a mohawk color (black, orange, purple, white, blue, or pink) and add a custom nameplate with their handle, in place of the default DEF CON 33 nameplate. It sold for $25 through technick's Tindie store, primarily for pickup at the convention, with remaining stock shipped afterward; the listing has shown as sold out since August 6, 2025.
