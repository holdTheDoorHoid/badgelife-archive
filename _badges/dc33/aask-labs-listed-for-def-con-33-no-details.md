The community badge sheet for DEF CON 33 (2025) lists "Aask Labs" as an expected maker but records no title, description, or other details for whatever they intended to bring.

"Aask Labs" is the name used by a maker who goes by "Aask" (personal site aask.ltd, Hackaday.io profile Aask42). Their site's writeup for the DEF CON 32 (2024) Infinite Wifi Portals credits Aask Labs as the creator, which matches a separate "Aask Labs" row on the DC32 community sheet. That writeup mentions a planned update for Supercon 2024 but nothing about DEF CON 33, and neither the site's project list nor the Hackaday.io profile shows a 2025 item. The storefront linked on the DC32 row, shop.truecontrol.org, is the trueControl / Whiskey Pirates shop and does not mention Aask Labs.

This entry is left as `status: unknown` pending a direct source from Aask Labs (a maker page, social post, or storefront) confirming what they actually produced for DEF CON 33, if anything.
