The community badge sheet for DEF CON 33 (2025) lists "BigFuckingBadge" as an expected maker but records no title or details for what they brought. BigFuckingBadge is the ongoing project of hexum064 (with Erin), running since DC26: a series of deliberately oversized, elaborate DEF CON badges hosted at bigfuckingbadge.com and on Hackaday.io, including a "Big Fucking Covid Badge," the DC30 Big Fucking Daft Punk Badge (an MP3/Bluetooth player with 500+ addressable LEDs styled after the Random Access Memories cover art), and the DC32 (2024) BigFuckingOOTBadge, an Ocarina of Time-themed badge with touch buttons and RGB LEDs, of which 50 were made and sold for around $100 through Hacker Warehouse.

No evidence of a DEF CON 33-specific release was found: the maker's Hackaday.io project pages stop at the 2024 BigFuckingOOTBadge, and targeted web searches for a 2025/DC33 BigFuckingBadge turned up nothing. It is unclear whether the maker skipped DEF CON 33, showed something that was never documented online, or simply hasn't posted about it yet as of this check. This entry is left as unknown rather than guessed; it should be revisited if a DC33 BigFuckingBadge project surfaces later.
