The community badge sheet for DEF CON 33 (2025) lists "BigFuckingBadge" as an expected maker but records no title or details for what they brought. BigFuckingBadge is the ongoing project of hexum064 and Erin: a series of deliberately oversized DEF CON badges documented on Hackaday.io and at bigfuckingbadge.com, including the DC30 Big Fucking Daft Punk Badge (an ESP32-based MP3/Bluetooth player with 500+ WS2812B LEDs styled after the Random Access Memories cover art) and the DC32 (2024) BigFuckingOOTBadge, an Ocarina of Time-themed badge with touch buttons and RGB LEDs, of which 50 were made with a $100 target price through Hacker Warehouse.

No evidence of a DEF CON 33-specific release was found: the maker's Hackaday.io project pages stop at the 2024 BigFuckingOOTBadge, and as of September 2026 their own site covers only the DEF CON 34 (2026) presale. It is unclear whether the maker skipped DEF CON 33 or showed something that was never documented online. This entry is left as unknown rather than guessed; it should be revisited if a DC33 BigFuckingBadge item surfaces later.
