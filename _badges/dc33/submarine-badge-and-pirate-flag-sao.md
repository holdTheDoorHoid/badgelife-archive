BigTaro's Submarine Badge was an unofficial DEF CON 33 (2025) badge that casts the wearer as the commander of a submarine, complete with a round display and speed/depth/heading/radar-style controls on the maker's own promotional page. The badge ties into a fully interactive adventure game: wearers navigate an underwater world filled with story characters, work through CTF challenges and pirate battles, and tackle hardware and software puzzles, including soldering challenges. It ships with six or more buttons, an exposed and programmable GPIO header, and an SAO port, and it was designed to interoperate with BigTaro's own Gunslinger-B-Gone badge from the same year.

The badge came bundled with an exclusive Pirate Flag SAO and stickers. It was sold through BigTaro's Tindie store (listed there at $90, "Online Always") and in person at DEF CON 33 through Hacker Warehouse, with additional sales channels via BigTaro's Bluesky account and a Signal group chat during the convention itself. The community badge sheet recorded a $160 price, which does not match the Tindie listing; that discrepancy is unresolved.

Details such as the MCU, LED count and type, battery, and connectivity were not published by the maker in the sources checked, and no open-source hardware or firmware files were found.

## Notes merged from the duplicate entry "Submarine Badge & Pirate SAO"

BigTaro's Submarine Badge & Pirate SAO is a combo listing on the Uberflux badge marketplace: an interactive, submarine-themed badge bundled with an exclusive Pirate Flag SAO and stickers. The Uberflux page describes a "Command Your Own Submarine" adventure with an underwater world full of secrets and story-rich characters, CTF-style challenges built around delivering packages, pirate combat, and an Atlantis discovery thread, along with hardware and software puzzles that include soldering challenges. The badge itself has a bright round display, six or more buttons, an SAO port, and exposed/programmable GPIO, and it is designed to interoperate with BigTaro's Gunslinger-B-Gone badge.

This appears to be the same badge documented separately as the DEF CON 33 (2025) "Submarine Badge and Pirate Flag SAO" entry, which cites the maker's own promotional page and a Tindie storefront listing with matching gameplay details and price. The Uberflux page itself never names a convention, so the event and year recorded here are carried over from that match rather than stated on this page.

At the time this listing was checked, Uberflux showed the item priced at $90 with 35 units remaining and 15 already sold, plus a flat $11 USPS shipping charge. The maker did not disclose the MCU, LED specifications, battery, connectivity, or open-source status on this page.
