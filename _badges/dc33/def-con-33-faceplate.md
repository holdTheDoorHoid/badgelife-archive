The DEF CON 33 Faceplate is a cosmetic swap panel made by Keyport, the EDC (everyday-carry) multi-tool company, for its Pivot, Slide, and Anywhere Tool key organizers. It carries custom printed artwork tied to DEF CON 33's "Access Everywhere" theme, letting owners of a Keyport tool snap on a con-branded look. It has no electronics, LEDs, or firmware — it is purely a plastic faceplate accessory, sold for $7 through Keyport's own storefront and mirrored on the Shop app.

Keyport released this alongside at least two other DEF CON 33 faceplates with more functionality: an NFC-enabled faceplate with a rewritable tag, and a glow-in-the-dark version of the NFC faceplate. Those are separate SKUs and would warrant their own entries.
