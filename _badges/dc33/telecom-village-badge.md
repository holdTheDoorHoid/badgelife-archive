Telecom Village is a DEF CON village dedicated to telecom and 5G security, running talks, hands-on CTFs, panel discussions, and workshops; it has appeared at DEF CON 31 through 33. The community badge sheet lists a "Telecom Village Badge" for DEF CON 33 (2025) with no link, price or description, and no maker page, storefront listing, repository, or photo describing the badge itself could be located as of this check — only the village's own general promotional presence (its website, which names PCBWay as its badge sponsor, and its @TelecomVillage X account, which was not accessible).

Because no source confirms the badge's construction, electronics, or distribution details, this entry keeps only what the sheet and the village's own confirmed presence support.
