Space Badge is an independent DEF CON 33 badge built by a maker going by Owen, described on the campaign as "designed, built and coded by a 16 yr old DEF CON enthusiast." It is billed as an open, co-op multiplayer, mesh-networked game badge with a sci-fi space theme: play happens over Wi-Fi mesh networking with no internet connection required, and the badge tracks XP and player progression as part of the game.

The project was funded through Kickstarter rather than sold directly, running from June 24 to July 25, 2025, with a $5,000 goal. It closed having raised $10,938 from 57 backers (218% of goal), which unlocked three stretch goals including an in-game avatar system and custom lanyards. The campaign listed a $160 pledge tier and noted the team had components already in hand before launch to reduce production risk. As of this check the Kickstarter is closed to new pledges and no separate storefront was found, so it is treated as sold out/limited to backers.

Beyond the campaign blurb, no source located gives the badge's microcontroller, LED count/type, display, or battery details, and no hardware or firmware repository was found, so those fields are left blank rather than guessed.
