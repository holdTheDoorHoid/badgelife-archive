Garrett, associated with the hacking-hardware retailer Hacker Warehouse
(hackerwarehouse.com), was listed as an expected maker on the community badge
sheet for DEF CON 33 (2025), but the sheet gives no further detail about what,
if anything, they brought.

Hacker Warehouse's storefront is built around reselling tools like the Flipper
Zero, HackRF, and RFID/NFC readers, but it also has a small "Badgelife"
category with two items: the Hacker Mindset Badge (an RP2040 badge with 14 RGB
LEDs and two SAO v1.69bis ports, $80) and the Cicada Invada DIY Kit (a
555-timer solder kit sold to raise money for Hak4Kidz, $30). Neither product
page mentions DEF CON 33, a year, or Garrett, and the company's site-news
archive has no 2025 badge announcement, so nothing found ties either item to
this sheet row. This entry is left as `unknown` rather than guessing; it
should be revisited if a photo, forum post, or vendor-table report surfaces
later.
