The ARCANUM Gospel Book Badge is a wearable "book" made for DEF CON 33 (2025) by Abhinav SP of Hackerware.io, commissioned for Jason Haddix's company Arcanum Information Security. It pairs a rigid PCB "cover," UV-printed with cover art and carrying the electronics on its back, with flexible-film "pages" that light up from their own edges — built as a layered stack of UV-printed polymer art, polymer, a laser-cut PCB LED layer, a polymer track/LED layer, and a vinyl backing. A MODE button cycles the glow patterns across the pages.

The badge frames itself as a tribute to Haddix's years of hacker research and writing, presented as "gospels" bound into a book worn around the neck. A crypto puzzle printed on the lanyard is the entry point into a CTF: solving it yields a binary sequence that is keyed into the badge via a sigil button to unlock further content, described on the maker's page as "walking the path towards digital enlightenment." It was distributed as a free badge drop at DEF CON 33.

Concrete hardware specifics — the MCU, LED count and type, and how many units were made — were not published on the maker's own pages found during this research, and are left blank here rather than guessed.

## History

Hackerware.io made a different, earlier "Arcanum Badge" for DEF CON 32 in 2024 (see `dc32-arcanum-badge`); the Gospel Book is a separate, unrelated design for the following year.
