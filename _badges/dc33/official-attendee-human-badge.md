The DEF CON 33 (2025) attendee badge, sometimes called the "Human" badge to distinguish it from press/goon/vendor variants, is a deliberately non-electronic design created by longtime DEF CON art director Mar Williams. Rather than a PCB with lights or a display, the badge centers on layered, 3D-style artwork that attendees explore by holding different colored lenses (and combinations of lenses) up to it, revealing hidden imagery — echoing the layered visual style Williams used across the con's signage and environment that year.

The badge ties into a con-wide challenge: attendees look for clues placed around the convention and bring findings to the Arts & Entertainment booth. Design credits beyond Williams include 3D-transformation work by collaborator @bon.bon.fin.fin, manufacturing refinement by ICSN, and (for the separate black badge) design collaboration with Bonnie Finley and @aimeevalentine.art.

At the start of DEF CON 33, a shipment delay meant attendees checking in through the cash line initially received temporary paper badges while the real badges caught up; DEF CON communicated updates via its socials and the Hacker Tracker app. Because 2025 was an explicitly "off year" for electronics, no chip, LED, or display information exists for this badge to record.
