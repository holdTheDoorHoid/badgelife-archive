Antihacker Hacker Club (handle 0xAHHC, based in San Antonio, TX) is a badgelife maker with an established Tindie storefront ("We drink and we solder things"), best known for the 2023 "Birds Aren't Real broSAO" — a blue bird-with-camera-head piece doubling as a brooch and an SAO. The community sheet for DEF CON 33 (2025) lists them as an expected maker but gives no title or details for what they actually brought that year.

Despite checking the maker's storefront (via the Wayback Machine, since the live site returned a Cloudflare challenge), their X account, and general web and press searches, no DEF CON 33-specific badge or SAO from this maker could be confirmed. It is possible they exhibited the existing broSAO again, released something that never got picked up by the archive or press, or did not ultimately release anything that year. This entry is left as unknown pending a source that names the actual item.
