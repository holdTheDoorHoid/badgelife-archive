"New AI Jack" is a duplicate sheet listing for technick's AI Jack Badge, the maker's DEF CON 33 entry in an ongoing series of 3D-printed "Jack" collectible badges running since New Jack at DC27 (Monopoly Jack at DC29, King Jack at DC30, Pirate Jack at DC31, an F CAESARS-themed Jack at DC32). For DC33 the theme was AI meeting the natural world - "a fusion of silicon and soil, circuitry and spirit," as the maker put it.

The badge has no electronics - it is a glow-in-the-dark 3D print sold with a lanyard, stickers, and occasional bonus loot items while supplies lasted. Buyers could choose a mohawk color (black, orange, purple, white, blue, or pink) and add a custom nameplate with their handle in place of the default DEF CON 33 nameplate. It sold for $25 through technick's Tindie store, mainly for pickup at the convention with remaining stock shipped afterward; the listing has shown sold out since August 6, 2025.

See dc33-ai-jack-badge for the primary record of this item.
