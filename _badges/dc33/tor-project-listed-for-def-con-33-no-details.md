The community badge sheet lists the Tor Project as an expected maker/presence at DEF CON 33 (2025), but with no further detail. The Tor Project's own event post for DEF CON 33 confirms it had a booth at the conference and was giving away "new Tor merch," without describing any electronic badge or SAO.

In past years a community hardware maker known as Seeess produced a recurring line of Tor-branded electronic SAOs and badges (documented on GitHub as Defcon-Tor-27 through Defcon-Tor-32, spanning DEF CON 27 through 32) sold to raise funds for the Tor Project, including a 2019 "purple onion" SAO and later mini-badge and lie-detector-badge variants. No equivalent "Defcon-Tor-33" project could be found for 2025, so it is unclear whether that series continued, paused, or simply has not been documented publicly yet. This entry is left as an unresolved stub pending confirmation of what, if anything, was distributed under the Tor Project's name at DEF CON 33.
