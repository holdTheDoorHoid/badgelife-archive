Keyport's "DEF CON 33 (DC33) Minimalist Bundle" is DEF CON 33-branded consumer merchandise sold through Keyport's own store, not an electronic badge or SAO. It packages three pieces of Keyport's everyday-carry key-organizer system: the aluminum Pivot 2.0 chassis, a MOCA 10-in-1 multi-tool insert, and a YubiStyle cover sized for a YubiKey. Keyport also sold a larger seven-piece "Everything Bundle" carrying the same DC33 branding (adding NFC and standard faceplates, an MRT tool, and a Sidekick) for $49 through the Shop app storefront.

None of the pieces contain a PCB, microcontroller, LEDs, or display -- it is a licensed keychain/EDC product line rather than hardware built for the badge/SAO ecosystem this archive tracks. It was originally logged from a search snippet during an unrelated research pass; confirming the maker's own listings shows it is out of scope for this catalog.
