Rabbit-Labs' DEF CON 33 "Pirates Plunder" badge shipped with a small bundled add-on the maker calls the Foxx SAO: a fox-themed accessory that lights up when it picks up a static electric discharge near the badge. It was included with every Pirates Plunder Badge kit (alongside a purple lanyard and a pre-installed 18650 battery) rather than sold on its own, so there is no independent product listing, price, or spec sheet for it at DC33 — everything known about it comes from the badge's own product page.

This is functionally a component of the already-catalogued Rabbit-Labs Pirates Plunder Badge (see that entry for the full badge writeup, team credits, and specs) rather than a separate purchasable item. A visually different, standalone "Full Color Foxx SAO" later appeared as its own product ahead of DEF CON 34, but that is a distinct, newer release and not this DC33 accessory.
