AI Village was present at DEF CON 33 (2025) as one of the conference villages, but no dedicated badge or SAO for that specific year could be confirmed from available sources. AI Village is known to have produced physical badges for adjacent years — a robot-dog RGB blinky badge for DEF CON 32 (2024) and a blinky badge/SAO for DEF CON 34 (2026), both designed by Abhinav Panda of Hackerware.io — but nothing equivalent turned up for 2025 specifically.

It is possible AI Village skipped a standalone hardware badge for DC33, reused a prior year's design, or simply has no public documentation of one. The aivillage.org event page for DEF CON 33 is behind Cloudflare bot protection and could not be fetched by an automated search; a manual visit or a direct question to AI Village organizers could resolve this.
