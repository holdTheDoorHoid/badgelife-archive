AI Village was present at DEF CON 33 (2025) as one of the conference villages, but no dedicated badge or SAO for that specific year could be confirmed from available sources. AI Village is known to have produced a physical badge for DEF CON 32 (2024) and is listed with a blinky badge and SAO for DEF CON 34 (2026), both by Abhinav Panda of Hackerware.io, but nothing equivalent turned up for 2025.

It is possible AI Village skipped a standalone hardware badge for DC33, reused a prior year's design, or simply has no public documentation of one. aivillage.org and the maker's Hackster.io pages could not be fetched by automated tools; a manual visit or a direct question to AI Village organizers could resolve this.
