The VETCON 2025 Badge was made for VetCON, the veteran-focused community event that has run alongside DEF CON since 2018, co-founded by Jim McMurry (ThreatHunter.ai) and William Kimble (Cyber Defense Technologies). For DEF CON 33, ThreatHunter.ai built the badge as a tongue-in-cheek homage to the military SINCGARS radio system, housing an ESP32 microcontroller and an LCD screen behind a recycled BlackBerry Q10 keyboard salvaged for its physical keys.

Functionally, the badge lets holders send short peer-to-peer Wi-Fi messages to each other directly, without any backend infrastructure — pitched by the maker as a way to send "tactical memes" and coordinate meetups within the VetCON crowd. It was sold pre-pay only through ThreatHunter.ai's online shop, with pickup restricted to the VetCON community space at DEF CON 33; no shipping was offered.

Specifics on LED usage and the exact production run size were not published on the listing or found elsewhere. The badge continues a yearly VetCON tradition also represented in this archive by the DC31 VetCON Badge and the DC32 "Red Tape Badge."

## Notes merged from the duplicate entry "VETCON 2025 Badge"

The VETCON 2025 Badge is ThreatHunter.ai's badge for the VetCON veterans' community space at DEF CON 33. Styled after a SINCGARS military radio, it's built from a recycled BlackBerry Q10 keyboard paired with an ESP32 and an LCD screen, giving it a deliberately "rugged retro-military" look.

The badge lets holders send peer-to-peer Wi-Fi messages to other badges nearby, used in the listing's own words to send tactical memes, summon beer, and coordinate badge drops within the VetCON space. It was sold pre-pay only through ThreatHunter.ai's Shopify store as a "limited run," with pickup restricted to the VetCON community area during DEF CON 33 and no shipping offered.
