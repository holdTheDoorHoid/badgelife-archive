The DEF CON 33 Everything Bundle is Keyport's flagship DC33-branded EDC kit, sold through the company's own store as part of its official DEF CON 33 vendor lineup. It gathers seven pieces around the aluminum Pivot 2.0 modular key organizer: two DC33-branded faceplates (one of which carries a rewritable NFC chip that can be programmed from a phone), the MOCA 10-in-1 multi-tool, a YubiStyle cover, an MRT, and a Sidekick. Unlike most entries in this archive it isn't a PCB badge or SAO — it's licensed hacker-conference merchandise built around Keyport's existing modular tool platform, with the NFC faceplate as its one piece of electronics.

Keyport also sold the same pieces individually and as a smaller three-piece "DC33 Minimalist Bundle," with this Everything Bundle positioned as the complete set at $49. Keyport's own copy describes it as "the last of the DC33 run," implying a single, non-reissued production batch, though no specific unit count is published.
