The VETCON 2025 Badge is ThreatHunter.ai's badge for the VetCON veterans' community space at DEF CON 33, continuing the studio's yearly VetCON badge series. Styled after a SINCGARS military radio, it's built from a recycled BlackBerry Q10 keyboard paired with an ESP32 and an LCD screen, giving it a deliberately "rugged retro-military" look.

The badge lets holders send peer-to-peer Wi-Fi messages to other badges nearby, used in the listing's own words for tactical-themed memes, summoning beer, and coordinating meetups or badge drops within the VetCON space. It was sold pre-pay only through ThreatHunter.ai's Shopify store, with pickup restricted to the VetCON community area during DEF CON 33 and no shipping offered.
