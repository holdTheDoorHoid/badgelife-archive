Ben's Badges is a small badge-making outfit (Ben, Hugo, and Chloe, per their site) best known for the HiVolt badge, a 1,984-LED scrolling-message badge built around a Raspberry Pi Pico and sold for $120 at DEF CON 32 in 2024. The community sheet lists them as an expected maker for DEF CON 33 (2025) but records no details about what, if anything, they brought that year.

Checking the maker's own store and about pages, their GitHub, and general web search in September 2026 turned up no trace of a DEF CON 33-specific product, announcement, or write-up — only continued references to the 2024 HiVolt badge, which by then was sold out. It is possible Ben's Badges skipped DEF CON 33, sold something in person without an online listing, or simply hasn't updated their site; none of that could be confirmed from available sources, so this entry is left as unknown pending better information.
