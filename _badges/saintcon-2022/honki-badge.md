The HONKI Badge is a personal SAINTCON 2022 minibadge made by C0n4rt15t and CompuKidMike (a frequent SAINTCON badge designer). It is a simple novelty: a mostly pre-assembled board that a solderer finishes by adding a switch, a speaker, and four 2-position headers, so that pressing the button makes it honk. The MiniBadge Wiki's build-guide data rates it Beginner difficulty and lists it under the "Personal" category rather than an official SAINTCON badge.

It was not sold or distributed through a normal channel; the build guide's only acquisition note is that getting one meant interacting with "Honki" in person at the convention, and the listed quantity made is unrecorded. A related badge from the same event, the "RAY-MAN Badge" (CompuKidMike & Ray-Man), shares near-identical description text and is documented separately.
