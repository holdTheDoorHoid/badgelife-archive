The Widow Badge is an official SAINTCON minibadge from 2022, designed by community member Jup1t3r. It isn't a badge for attendees generally - it exists to honor the "SAINTCON Widows," the spouses of the conference's organizers, recognizing the time those organizers spend away from home planning and running the event each year. Per the official assembly guide, the badge is "not generally available" and is instead given directly to members of the SAINTCON Widows group.

Mechanically it's a standard beginner-difficulty minibadge: hand-solder a set of LEDs and a resistor using the single-pad method, then the four 2-position headers that let it plug into a SAINTCON badge's minibadge wings. The guide rates it "Common" for rarity despite its restricted distribution, likely reflecting how the SAINTCON minibadge rarity scale tracks build simplicity rather than how hard the badge is to obtain.
