The Vault Badge is a SAINTCON 2022 minibadge made by Jup1t3r to mark the Vault Challenge, a physical-security-bypass event run at the con by JC and Snow of Snowfensive. Per the official SAINTCON MiniBadge guide for that year, the badge was rated BEGINNER difficulty and handed out at the Vault Area to anyone who either took part in the challenge themselves or watched others attempt it — making it a participation/observation token for the Vault event rather than something bought or built independently.

No maker page, storefront, repository, or photograph specific to this 2022 badge turned up in the sources checked, so its physical details (color, shape, LED count, MCU, if any) are left blank here rather than guessed. Jup1t3r also made the SAINTCON 2021 "THE VAULT" minibadge (a soldering-kit badge with two LEDs and fantasy-warrior artwork, catalogued separately as `saintcon-2021-the-vault`), and the Vault Challenge itself recurred at SAINTCON in other years under different badge makers (e.g. `saintcon-2017-vault-key-hole-official-minibadge`, `saintcon-2023-the-vault-contest-badge-2023`), suggesting "Vault" is a long-running SAINTCON challenge rather than a single badge series.
