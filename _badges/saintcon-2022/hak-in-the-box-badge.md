The HAK-IN-THE-BOX BADGE is an official SAINTCON 2022 minibadge made by Jup1t3r for Hack-in-the-Box (also styled HAK-IN-THE-BOX), an unofficial after-hours event that runs alongside SAINTCON each year. Attendees received the badge by visiting the Hack-in-the-Box event itself rather than through a separate purchase or drop.

The badge uses reverse-mount LEDs, which the maker's own build notes flag as more finicky to hand-solder than a typical through-hole LED, putting the badge at an "Intermediate" soldering difficulty. Assembly starts on the back side, with LEDs and resistors soldered single-pad style before the four 2-position headers (used to link it to neighboring minibadges) go on. Minibadge.wiki lists it as "Official" category and "Uncommon" rarity; no production quantity, price, or LED count is recorded.
