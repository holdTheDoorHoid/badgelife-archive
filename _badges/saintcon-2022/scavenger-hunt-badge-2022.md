The Scavenger Hunt Badge is a SAINTCON 2022 community minibadge designed by Jup1t3r, awarded to attendees who completed the con's Scavenger Hunt contest rather than purchased or freely distributed at a table. Its artwork is a snarling raccoon mascot above the words "SCAVENGER HUNT." Electronically it is a simple beginner-level build: a single LED and resistor hand-soldered using the single-pad technique, with a solder jumper letting the builder choose between a blinking or solid-on LED, plus the standard four 2-position pin headers used across the 2022 minibadge line for chaining badges together.

The badge is documented in the official 2022 MiniBadge Assembly Guide (page 26) alongside dozens of other community, sponsor, and personal minibadges from that year, but no separate maker page, Hackaday post, or storefront listing was found, so details like production quantity are unknown. The archive previously only held the 2021 edition of this badge; this 2022 version is a distinct yearly design.
