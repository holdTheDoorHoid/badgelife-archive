The Saint Khan Badge is a SAINTCON 2022 minibadge designed by bfcoder: a small green PCB bearing a hand-drawn portrait of Khan (from Star Trek) with a halo over his head and a Starfleet delta insignia, labeled "SAINT KHAN 2022." The pun is the whole point — "Khan" plus halo makes "Saint Khan," fitting SAINTCON's tradition of saint-themed minibadge wordplay.

Electrically it is intentionally simple: a single LED (D1) driven through one resistor (R1), with no microcontroller, listed as beginner difficulty to solder. Like other SAINTCON minibadges from the same set, it connects via four 2-position headers so it can be worn alongside other minibadges in a chain.

It was not sold; the assembly guide states it was only available by trading in person with bfcoder at SAINTCON 2022, and rates it "very rare." No separate storefront, repository, or design-file release has been found for it.
