This is a small 3D-printable accessory for the SAINTCON 2022 badge, not a badge or SAO in its own right. The SAINTCON 2022 badge (designed by compukidmike) has a "minibadge wing" extension where minibadges plug in via exposed header pins; this cover snaps over that port so the solder points on the underside don't catch on a shirt or jacket while the badge is worn. Two are needed per badge, one for each side of the extension.

The design was published for free on Thingiverse and mirrored on Printables by compukidmike, the same person who designed that year's SAINTCON badge. The original Thingiverse listing (thing:5573684) has since gone offline, but the file remains available on Printables, sized for an Ender 3 V2 at 0.2 mm resolution with no supports or rafts needed.
