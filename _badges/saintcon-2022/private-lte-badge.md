The PRIVATE LTE BADGE is a SAINTCON 2022 minibadge by maker Jup1t3r, promoting private LTE network technology. It is a simple learn-to-solder build: a black PCB with a single through-hole LED (D1) and a resistor (R1), assembled with three 2-position headers. One side of the board carries a yellow "TE" logo over the component pads; the other carries a "PRIVATE LTE — ARE YOU CONNECTED?" graphic with a wifi-signal icon.

Minibadge.wiki lists it as an "Official" category badge with "Rare" rarity and "Intermediate" soldering difficulty. Rather than being sold, it was handed out by people at SAINTCON promoting private LTE technology, who the listing says "hang out in the Leadership Track" — a scavenger-hunt-style distribution typical of SAINTCON's minibadge culture. No price, quantity made, or open-source hardware files were found for it.
