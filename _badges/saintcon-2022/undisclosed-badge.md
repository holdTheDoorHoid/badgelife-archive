The Undisclosed Badge is a personal minibadge designed by Sh33pr0ck for SAINTCON 2022, tied to the con's "Undisclosed" side event — a satirical, clue-hunt-gated event whose own promotional page insists "the existence of tfht.agency cannot be confirmed... but it also can not be denied." The minibadge carries that joke forward: the official 2022 MiniBadge Assembly Guide describes it as obtainable only by attending "the most exclusive event (assuming it exists)" and warns, tongue-in-cheek, that it is secretly a mind-control tracker tied to a "TFHT" conspiracy, pointing readers to the (real, in-fiction) tfht.agency site.

Despite the joke framing, the badge is a real, physically built board: the guide lists it as BEGINNER difficulty and RARE, and its assembly instructions call out two LEDs (D1, a color-changing LED; D2, solid-color) each paired with its own resistor (R1 purple, R2 green), soldered with single-pad hand-soldering steps and four 2-position headers. The front artwork shows a bigfoot-like figure being abducted by a UFO under a moon, labeled "TFHT"; the back carries the maker's handle, "Govna Mupp," "SaintCon 2022," and the component silkscreen.

No pricing, production quantity, or open-source design files were found for this badge. It appears to be a one-off personal/trading minibadge distributed informally in connection with the Undisclosed event rather than sold through any storefront.
