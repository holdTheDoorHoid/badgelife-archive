The Killer Whale Badge is a personal SAINTCON minibadge made by Z2Orca for SAINTCON 2022, their first badge design and first SAINTCON. The badge depicts a killer whale as a personal icon tied to the maker's handle. Like other SAINTCON minibadges it is a small PCB with LEDs and header pins meant to plug into the main conference badge.

Rather than being sold or freely distributed, the badge was given out informally: attendees got one by talking with Z2Orca and sharing some knowledge, skill, or story, in keeping with SAINTCON's minibadge-trading culture. It was catalogued as a "Personal" category minibadge and rated "Rare" in the community-maintained minibadge wiki, though no quantity, price, or technical specifications (MCU, LED type/count, colors) were published in the sources found.
