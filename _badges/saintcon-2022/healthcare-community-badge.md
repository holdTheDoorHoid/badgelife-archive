The Healthcare Community Badge is a SAINTCON 2022 minibadge made by Jup1t3r for the Healthcare Hacking Community, a track added to SAINTCON that year covering medical device security and the broader challenges of security in healthcare. It is a beginner-friendly, learn-to-solder kit: builders solder a small number of LEDs and a single resistor using the single-pad hand-soldering method, along with four 2-position headers, working the back side first. The maker's notes suggest covering the LEDs and the back's unmasked area with hot glue for a better diffused lighting effect.

The badge was not sold; it was given out for free at the Healthcare Hacking Community's booth at SAINTCON 2022 to attendees who stopped to talk with the community's volunteers. It is listed as an "Official" SAINTCON badge with "Common" rarity on the minibadge.wiki community database, the only source located for this entry.
