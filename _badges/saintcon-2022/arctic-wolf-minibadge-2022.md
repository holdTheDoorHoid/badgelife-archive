Arctic Wolf Minibadge was one of SAINTCON 2022's sponsor minibadges, designed by Jup1t3r for the cybersecurity company Arctic Wolf, which sponsored the 2022 conference. The board is a blue PCB cut into a triangular silhouette of a howling wolf's head, matching Arctic Wolf's own logo mark.

Two through-hole LEDs — one orange, one blue — sit behind the wolf cutout and are driven by a simple oscillator circuit that alternates them, so the badge's backlit logo blinks between the sponsor's brand colors. The SAINTCON assembly guide rates it a beginner-difficulty build (two LEDs, two resistors, and four 2-position headers, all hand-soldered single-pad style) and lists it as a common-rarity badge. It was given away at the Arctic Wolf sponsor booth on the expo floor rather than sold, which is standard for SAINTCON's sponsor minibadge line.

No separate maker page, repository, or storefront listing was found for this badge; the SAINTCON 2022 Minibadge Assembly Guide (page 36) is the only known documentation, and it is the source for every field above.
