The LEADERSHIP BADGE is a SAINTCON 2022 minibadge made for the conference's Leadership Community, a track aimed at C-level executives attending SAINTCON. It isn't sold or traded openly; attendees get one by participating in that Leadership track.

The badge is a simple, beginner-level soldering project built around a single LED. The LED is hand-soldered to the back of the board with roughly a half-inch standoff, then bent so it rests against the back of the board and shines outward; the instructions suggest covering the LED and surrounding area with hot glue to diffuse the light. A resistor and four 2-position headers round out the build, with soldering handled on the back side first.

Nothing about the badge's manufacturer, quantity produced, or design files was found beyond its minibadge.wiki listing, credited to makers Handsomkab and Jup1t3r.
