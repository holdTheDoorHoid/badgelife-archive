The HOMELABS CAT6 MINIBADGE is an official SAINTCON 2022 minibadge built by a four-person team (bfcoder, klipper, v0rtex, and yukia) that doubles as a simple Ethernet cable tester: it has an onboard Ethernet jack, and LEDs indicate the wiring of a plugged-in Cat6 cable. It was not sold — it was awarded to attendees who completed the Home Labs section of that year's SAINTCON Hackers Challenge contest, and the community record rates it "Uncommon."

Assembly is rated Advanced on the community wiki: the front-side LEDs and back-side resistors are soldered using the single-pad method, the Ethernet jack is press-fit into the board and then soldered on the reverse side, and header pins are added last. Beyond the community submission on minibadge.wiki, no maker-run page, repository, or storefront for this specific badge was found, so chip/LED specifics, exact production numbers, and open-source status remain unconfirmed.
