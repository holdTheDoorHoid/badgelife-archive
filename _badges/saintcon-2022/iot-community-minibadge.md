The IOT Community minibadge was one of the official SAINTCON 2022 minibadges, distributed for free to attendees who visited the IoT Community booth in the expo space. Its stated purpose was to introduce newcomers to "the entire and ever-growing world of vulnerable things that exist on the internet," tying its distribution to hands-on engagement with the booth rather than a purchase or raffle.

As a minibadge it is simple by design: assembly calls for soldering a single LED and resistor plus four 2-position headers, using the single-pad hand-soldering method common to the SAINTCON minibadge format. The build guide includes a deliberately confusing labeling gag — the LED is mounted on the "TOP" side while the resistor sits on the "BOTTOM" — pitched at beginner soldering difficulty. It carries an "Official" / "Common" rarity classification in the SAINTCON minibadge community catalog maintained at minibadge.wiki, attributed to designer Jup1t3r.

No independent write-up, repository, or storefront for this minibadge was located beyond the minibadge.wiki community database entry, so details such as exact LED type, PCB color, or production quantity are not confirmed and are left blank rather than guessed.
