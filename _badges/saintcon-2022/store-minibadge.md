The Store Minibadge was SAINTCON 2022's incentive for shopping at the conference store: buy anything from the store while supplies lasted, and you got this minibadge, designed by a maker credited as Pali. It carries the event's shield-shaped logo on a yellow PCB, matching the visual language SAINTCON used across its 2022 minibadge series.

Like most SAINTCON minibadges of that year, it ships as a small hand-solder kit rather than a finished piece: the official assembly guide walks builders through soldering a single LED and a resistor (both using the single-pad method) plus four 2-position headers on the back side first. The official guide rates it Intermediate difficulty and Uncommon rarity, in the same tier as several of the year's contest and interaction-based badges.

No independent storefront, price, quantity-made figure, or separate maker page turned up beyond the official assembly-guide PDF, so those fields are left blank rather than guessed.
