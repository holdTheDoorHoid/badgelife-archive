The Los Santos Challenge is a SAINTCON 2022 minibadge by the maker known as Jup1t3r, given out for taking part in (or watching) the "Los Santos Escape" challenge — one of SAINTCON's signature lock-picking and critical-thinking escape-room contests. Rather than being sold or randomly distributed, it functions as a participation token tied to that specific challenge room.

It's a simple, beginner-friendly solder kit: a single through-hole LED, a couple of resistors, and four 2-position headers, assembled back-side first. The maker's own build notes flag that the LED's polarity markings are hard to read and point builders to a reference image to get the orientation right.

No maker page, repository, or storefront could be found for Jup1t3r or this badge beyond its community-submitted listing on minibadge.wiki, so details like color, shape, price, and production quantity are not recorded anywhere and are left blank here rather than guessed.
