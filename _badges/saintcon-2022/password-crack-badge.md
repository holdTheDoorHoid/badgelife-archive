The Password Crack Badge is a SAINTCON 2022 minibadge by maker Jup1t3r, tied to the convention's password-cracking community booth. It carries two LEDs, labeled YES and NO, that a jumper sets to blink or solid mode, with additional center jumpers selecting which LED is active — a small interactive novelty rather than a powered electronic badge with its own MCU.

Distribution was informal: attendees got one by interacting with the people running the password-cracking community booth, rather than through a storefront or general drop. minibadge.wiki lists it as an "Official" SAINTCON minibadge of "Common" rarity with "Intermediate" soldering difficulty, but records no price and no reliable production quantity.

No maker-run page, repository, or storefront for this badge was found; the only source located is the minibadge.wiki community database, which itself credits Jup1t3r as the badge's author.
