The CHECKING LOGS BADGE is a personal SAINTCON 2022 minibadge designed by RuShan and hollyhacker, built around a lumberjack pun: a green, circular PCB shows two crossed rolling logs under the caption "EVERY DAY I'M CHECKING LOGS," with a single LED wired to backlight the artwork from behind the board. The back credits @hollyhacker and the SAINTCON Discord.

Per the 2022 SAINTCON MiniBadge Assembly Guide, it was rated "SUPER RARE" and "INTERMEDIATE" difficulty to assemble, and was not sold -- hollyhacker traded it directly with other attendees and was open to networking to get one. Assembly calls for soldering a resistor and a single LED (long leg through a circle-shaped hole in the board, bent to point up and backlight the design) plus four 2-position headers, likely for connecting to a minibadge chain.

No separate maker page, repository, or storefront for this badge was found; the assembly guide is the only known source.
