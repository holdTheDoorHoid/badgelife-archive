Valcom, a maker of paging and mass-notification systems, was SAINTCON 2022's top-tier ("UBER") sponsor, and gave out a sponsor minibadge at their expo-center booth to mark the partnership. The badge, designed by community minibadge organizer Jup1t3r, depicts Valcom's cybersecurity mascot: a rabid ferret.

It is a simple, beginner-level soldering kit built around a single LED (the build guide specifically warns that the LED's polarity markings are hard to read, and shows a diagram to get the orientation right) and a resistor, both hand-soldered single-pad style. A solder-jumper pair lets the builder choose between a blinking or solid-on LED, and four 2-position headers complete the assembly.

No maker page, pricing, print quantity, or open-source design files turned up in this pass; the MiniBadge Wiki catalog entry (sourced from SAINTCON's own 2021-2022 build guides) was the only source beyond the original sweep PDF confirming the badge is real and giving the details above.
