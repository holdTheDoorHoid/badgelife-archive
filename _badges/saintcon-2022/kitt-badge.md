The KITT BADGE is a personal SAINTCON minibadge by Sh33pr0ck, themed after KITT, the talking car from the TV series Knight Rider. It is a second run of the maker's very first minibadge design, originally made in 2018, brought back for SAINTCON 2022. The board runs on an ATtiny85 and was made in a run of only 6, with 2 available for trade at the con; the maker notes some units may already be pre-assembled ("due to special circumstances," in their words) and may carry either the traditional red LED scheme or an alternate green one.

Like most of Sh33pr0ck's minibadges from this year, it was distributed informally: the maker describes being found "wandering around" the conference floor, often while volunteering with one of SAINTCON's communities, and being open to interesting trades. No storefront, price, or published design files are known for this badge.
