The HACK minibadges are a set of four SAINTCON-style minibadges made by Jup1t3r for SAINTCON 2022, one badge for each letter of the word "HACK." Rather than being sold or handed out freely, the set was a trade item: Jup1t3r gave the badges to attendees who came by the BadgeLife community booth while they were present and talked about their first real hack. Each badge in the set carries four LEDs, hand-soldered using the single-pad method, with the cathode marked by a black dot on the silkscreen, plus a resistor and four 2-position headers per badge.

minibadge.wiki lists the set under the "Official" category with "Rare" rarity and "Intermediate" soldering difficulty. No pricing, production quantity, PCB color, or board house information was published for this item, and no separate project page, repository, or storefront for Jup1t3r was found.
