The Hardware Hacking Badge is a SAINTCON 2022 minibadge made by Hamster for the con's Hardware Hacking Community. Rather than being a novelty collectible, it's built as a teaching tool: a beginner-level soldering exercise that walks the builder through populating sockets, resistors, capacitors, and a 555 timer chip, then invites them to break the panel apart and experiment with basic analog circuits once assembled. It was given away for free to anyone who visited the Hardware Hacking Community area at the con rather than sold or raffled.

The minibadge.wiki community database, the only source located for this entry, lists it as an "Official" category badge with "Common" rarity and "Beginner" soldering difficulty. No maker page, repository, or press coverage beyond that community listing was found, so details like PCB color, board shape, and quantity produced are not documented.
