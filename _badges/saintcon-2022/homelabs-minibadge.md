The HOMELABS MINIBADGE was handed out at SAINTCON 2022 by HomeLabs, a community group new to the con that year offering instruction and resources for building a home lab. It is a simple learn-to-solder minibadge on a green PCB: three through-hole LEDs (blue, green, and red, each paired with its own current-limiting resistor) plus three 2-position headers, with the maker credits (bfcoder, Jup1t3r, klipper, v0rtex, and yukia) silkscreened on the board. Attendees got one by visiting the HomeLabs community booth and interacting with the group; the minibadge.wiki listing marks it "Official" category and "Common" rarity, with no fixed quantity recorded.

No maker-run project page, GitHub repo, or storefront for this badge was found; everything here comes from the community-run minibadge.wiki catalog, which is a secondary source rather than the maker's own words. Design files and BOM/EDA details are accordingly unknown.
