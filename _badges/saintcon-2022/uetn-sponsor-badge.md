The UETN Sponsor Badge is one of the official sponsor minibadges from SAINTCON 2022's community minibadge trading scene, designed by Jup1t3r on behalf of the Utah Education and Telehealth Network (UETN), which the SAINTCON assembly guide calls the conference's longest-supporting sponsor. The badge carries UETN's mountain-and-circuit logo on the front and a QR code linking to uetn.org on the back, along with the organization's full name in silkscreen.

Like most SAINTCON minibadges it is a simple beginner-difficulty soldering kit: a handful of LEDs (direction marked with a silkscreen dot), one resistor, and 4x 2-position headers that let it plug edge-to-edge into a trading board alongside other minibadges. It was not sold; attendees got one by finding a UETN employee at the con and interacting with them, and the guide rates it "rare" on its availability scale.

UETN returned with a near-identical "UETN Sponsor Badge" the following year (SAINTCON 2023), already cataloged separately in this archive.
