The INFOBOOTH BADGE is an official SAINTCON 2022 minibadge designed by SHIFTY and given away at the convention's InfoBooth. Rather than being sold, it was handed out while supplies lasted, and InfoBooth staff sometimes asked recipients to complete a small task before receiving one.

It is a simple beginner-level solder-it-yourself kit: a single LED and a current-limiting resistor, both single-pad hand-soldered, plus four 2-position headers. No microcontroller is used. The minibadge.wiki community database lists it under the "Official" category with a rarity of "Uncommon."

No maker page, repository, or storefront listing was found beyond the minibadge.wiki entry, so pricing, exact quantity made, and any design files remain unknown.
