The Bear Jump Badge is a personal minibadge designed by B3rJmp for SAINTCON 2022, part of the con's community minibadge tradition where attendees design, hand-solder, and trade small PCB badges rather than buy them from a table. Unlike the "official" SAINTCON minibadges that are sold or given out at a booth, this one was distributed the community way: the 2022 assembly guide simply instructs finders to "Find B3rJmp for one of these," and marks its rarity as SUPER RARE against a BEGINNER soldering difficulty.

Construction-wise, the assembly guide describes a double-sided board: LEDs and resistors are soldered using a single-pad hand-soldering method on the back of the board, plus 4x 2-position headers, with a suggestion to cover the back LEDs in hot glue for a better diffused lighting effect. A jumper lets the builder choose between a BLINK or SOLID lighting mode. Beyond that, no independent maker page, storefront, or photograph of the finished badge could be located, so its color scheme, exact shape, and LED count remain unconfirmed.
