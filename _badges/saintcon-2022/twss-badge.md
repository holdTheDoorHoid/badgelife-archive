The TWSS Badge is a personal minibadge made for SAINTCON 2022 by three designers credited as Katie, Berly, and Lynsey (the badge's own silkscreen lists them as "Berly, Lynsey, Katie"). Like most SAINTCON minibadges, it was not sold but given out in person: attendees got one by finding and interacting with one of the three designers at the con.

The badge is a joke about the American version of the sitcom *The Office*: the front shows a Michael Scott silhouette with the show's oft-repeated punchline "That's what she said," and the back reads "I can't believe you came." It is a simple kit build — solder two resistors and an LED on the back, then an LED and four 2-position headers on the front — rated BEGINNER difficulty and RARE on the assembly guide's rarity scale, with no microcontroller or display.

No maker page, repository, or storefront was found for this badge; the SAINTCON 2022 Minibadge Assembly Guide PDF is the only source located, and it is enough to confirm the badge is real, physical, and was actually distributed rather than merely proposed.
