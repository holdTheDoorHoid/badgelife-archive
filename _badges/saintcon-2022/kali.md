Kali is a personal, trade-only minibadge from SAINTCON 2022, made by Sh33pr0ck with design credit given to 2e08. Unlike the con's official or sponsor minibadges, it wasn't sold or given away at a booth — the build guide describes it as something you'd get by finding Sh33pr0ck or "Nate" at the convention and trading something unique for one, and the community-maintained Minibadge Wiki catalog rates it "Super Rare."

The board is rated Intermediate difficulty, attributed to its smaller part sizes. Assembly notes call for orienting the LED cathodes toward a green dot silkscreened on the board, and a jumper (JP1) that can be bridged one of two ways to select between a clock-driven blink and steady 3.3V operation; the instructions also suggest covering the LEDs and the back of the board with hot glue for a better diffused lighting effect.

No maker storefront, GitHub repository, or social profile could be located for Sh33pr0ck, so the badge's MCU, LED count/type, quantity made, and any design files remain unknown.
