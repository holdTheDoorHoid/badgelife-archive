The Hacker Skull is one of several minibadges Sh33pr0ck designed for SAINTCON 2022, described in the official assembly guide simply as "pretty much just a skull with red eyes." Like SAINTCON's other minibadges it was meant to be hand-soldered by attendees: a single LED (D1) with its cathode oriented toward a marked green dot, plus a JP1 solder jumper that chooses between driving the LED from a clock pin or running it steady off 3.3V.

It was not sold. The guide's listing for it reads "Find me wandering around. I'll trade for something I don't have," meaning the only way to get one was to track down the maker in person at the con and offer a trade — consistent with its assembly guide difficulty rating of BEGINNER and rarity rating of RARE.

No maker storefront, repository, or additional photos of this specific badge turned up beyond the assembly guide entry, so details like PCB color, exact LED type, and how many were made remain unknown.
