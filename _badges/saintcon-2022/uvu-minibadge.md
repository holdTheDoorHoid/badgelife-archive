The UVU Minibadge is a personal SAINTCON 2022 minibadge designed by Brian Peterson, an instructor at Utah Valley University, to show school pride for the UVU Wolverines. The PCB is cut into the shape of a block "U," finished in green soldermask with white silkscreen reading "UVU 2022," and populated with a single LED and resistor plus pin headers — a beginner-level build with no microcontroller or other electronics.

It was not sold or given away at a table; per SAINTCON's own minibadge assembly guide, attendees obtained one by finding a UVU-affiliated person at the conference and trading for it (or, as the guide puts it, "social-engineering" one out of them). The guide rates it "uncommon" on SAINTCON's rarity scale, meaning roughly 100-300 were believed to be in circulation, though no exact production count was published.
