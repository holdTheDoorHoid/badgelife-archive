This is a personal trader minibadge made by designer Ay.Aitch (also credited elsewhere in the same 2022 SAINTCON guide for an "AH - Moto" minibadge) to represent Utah State University at SAINTCON. Rather than being sold or handed out freely, it's awarded informally to USU-affiliated attendees, or to anyone willing to trade another unique badge, wear Aggie gear, or perform a rendition of "The Scotsman."

The board is a navy PCB with the USU "STATE" wordmark and block-U logo in a gold/copper silkscreen on the front. Electronically it's minimal: a single through-hole LED (cathode down) driven through one resistor, with four 2-pin headers along the top and bottom edges (likely for daisy-chaining or mounting alongside other minibadges). The guide rates it Beginner difficulty and Uncommon rarity, and suggests hot-gluing over the LED and the unmasked back area for a better diffused lighting effect. The back silkscreen carries a "HP - 2021" mark, suggesting the PCB design itself may originate from a prior year's badge that was carried into the 2022 personal-minibadges roster.

No independent storefront, repository, or social presence for this badge (or for the maker generally) turned up in a search, so distribution appears to be word-of-mouth/trade only, consistent with its "trader minibadge" description.
