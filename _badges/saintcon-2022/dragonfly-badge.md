DragonFly is a personal SAINTCON 2022 minibadge designed by Z1Dragonfly, a first-time SAINTCON attendee, as an icon for their own hacker handle. The PCB pairs a gold-on-blue dragonfly-and-flower silkscreen on the front with two LEDs (D1/D2) wired through resistors R1-R3 on the back, and it connects to a SAINTCON badge or other minibadges through the standard 4-position 2-pin headers used across the 2022 minibadge set.

Like most SAINTCON minibadges, it was not sold: the assembly guide instructs attendees to seek out the maker in person and "help her level up in the quest for knowledge" to receive one, and it is rated rare with a beginner assembly difficulty (single-pad hand soldering for the LEDs, resistors, and headers, with an optional hot-glue diffuser over the LEDs).

No maker page, repository, or storefront beyond the official SAINTCON 2022 MiniBadge Assembly Guide was found, so details like total quantity made, exact LED part/color, and whether design files were ever published remain unknown.
