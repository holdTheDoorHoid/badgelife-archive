This is a fan-made minibadge honoring the video game Portal 2, designed by SAINTCON regular bfcoder for the con's 2022 MiniBadge Assembly Guide. The front silkscreen shows the game's signature blue-and-orange portal doorways with a running figure and a slice of cake, a nod to the game's "the cake is a lie" motif. It was rated beginner difficulty and rare on the guide's badge-trading scale.

The board is a simple through-hole kit: two LEDs (orange and blue) each paired with their own resistor (68 ohm and 5.1 ohm respectively), plus a set of headers and a solder-bridge jumper on the back that lets the builder choose between a solid-on or blinking lighting mode. The maker's assembly instructions call out starting on the back side, using the single-pad hand-soldering method, and finishing with a dab of hot glue behind the LEDs to diffuse the light through the front artwork.

Like most SAINTCON minibadges, it was not sold; attendees obtained one by trading directly with bfcoder at the convention. No separate storefront, repository, or social media post about it was found beyond its page in the official assembly guide, so quantity made and exact print date are unknown.
