The HackerHotel 2025 badge was produced by Badge.Team for HackerHotel, the small annual Dutch hacker gathering held in February at Hotel de Werelt on the Veluwe. Its existence is confirmed by a talk the badge team gave at the event itself -- "Hackerhotel Badge talk: our workflow, challenges and other fun things," recorded on media.ccc.de -- but no public repository, schematic, bill of materials, or photo of the badge has surfaced. Badge.team's own documentation site, which covers the 2019, 2020, 2023, and 2024 HackerHotel badges in detail, has no page for the 2025 edition.

A separate, more widely publicized talk at HackerHotel 2025 covered the PCB design of "Tanmatsu," a badge built around the then-unreleased ESP32-P4 for the WHY2025 event in August 2025 -- that is a different piece of hardware, made for a different (larger, outdoor) conference, and is not the item this entry describes.
