The BIC Soul Glo Badge is a Special Add-On (SAO) made for the Blacks In Cybersecurity (BIC) Village at DEF CON 34 in 2026, sold through BIC's own online store. The front carries a "Soul Glo" graphic — two afro-haired figures over a burst shape reading "Soul Glo," a nod to the fictional hair-care brand from the film *Coming to America* — along with the BIC Village logo and website. The back has a numeric grid keyed to a color legend (white, red, green, yellow, blue, magenta, cyan, black), a format BIC has used on past badges for collect-and-compare style games at their village.

According to a Discord post from the maker, who signs the board "SAO BY SHYFT," the badge deliberately has no MCU and is not interactive; it was built to be cheap, with a roughly $3 per-unit production cost and a $10 target sale price, and BIC ordered a run of 200 units for DEF CON 34. No independent coverage of the badge's actual event distribution, sell-out status, or in-person reception was found; the storefront listing itself is a client-rendered page that would not report a live price or stock status to automated fetching.
