The Metropolis AI SAO is an art-deco automaton head add-on made by coryallegory for DEF CON 34, styled after "Maria," the robot from Fritz Lang's 1927 silent film *Metropolis*. It plugs into a badge's standard SAO header and lights a single red LED as a glowing eye when powered — a simple, sculptural nod to one of science fiction's earliest and most iconic robot designs.

The SAO was sold fully assembled for $20 through the maker's Uberflux storefront. By the time this entry was researched, the listing showed all 49 units sold and out of stock, with a note that more stock might become available after DEF CON for mail order. No hardware files, firmware, or open-source release were found for this piece, and technical details like the MCU (if any) and specific LED part were not published.

The same maker, coryallegory, also produced the Vibe Coder SAO at DEF CON 33 and the Viber Coder Dog SAO at DEF CON 34.
