The Brain in a Jar SAO is a small add-on board by H4irl1n3 M0gg3r5, made for DEF CON 34 (2026) alongside the crew's larger Zombie Badge / Survivor Badge project. The PCB is shaped like a mason jar holding a brain drawn half organic and half mechanical, with two LED pads (LED1, LED2) and "BRAAAINS..." silkscreened along the bottom. The maker's site tags it with a "WEAR AT YOUR OWN RISK" warning and describes a single interactive trick: tapping the SAO triggers an effect on every Zombie Badge within range, tying it into the wider infection-themed CTF the crew ran on the con floor.

No pricing, production quantity, MCU, or open-source design files are published for the SAO. The maker's acquisition section states plainly that their DC34 badges "drop throughout the conference" rather than being sold, and points people to r/Defcon and the @Zeroo_patient account on X for drop timing; the SAO is not called out separately from that statement, so it is presented here as a likely free drop rather than a confirmed one.
