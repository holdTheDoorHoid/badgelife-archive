The Day of the Dead Turkey is a Day-of-the-Dead-styled, sugar-skull-decorated turkey minibadge from distinctm1nd's "Cabinet of Distinctm1nd" shop, sold for DEF CON 34 in two art revisions, v1 and v2. Each is a soldering kit built from 1206-or-larger components, so it's approachable for less experienced solderers, and each carries a single LED that buyers could choose in blue or yellow at checkout.

The badge does double duty: worn as an oversized minibadge on a host badge's minibadge rail, or fitted with a pin back and a CR2032 coin cell to stand alone on a lanyard. Both versions sold for $12 through the maker's Shopify storefront, with a preorder option for pickup at DEF CON (Aug 6-8, 2026); as of this check, both v1 and v2 listings show sold out.

No hardware files, firmware, or press coverage turned up for this piece, so it is documented here from the storefront listings alone.
