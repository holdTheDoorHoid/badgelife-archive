The EFF "Electric Fist" BadgeLife item is a DEF CON 34 fundraiser piece sold through Eventbrite by H4r0ld & V3rbaal's House Of Hacker Horrors, with proceeds going to the Electronic Frontier Foundation. It is one entry in a recurring line of EFF-themed badge-life charms this same team has produced for DEF CON: the listing's own promotional image shows the new DC34 design — a circular emblem combining the EFF wordmark, crossed lightning bolts, and a raised fist — displayed next to limited reruns of a DC33 design (a winged eagle labeled "ALL OUR DATA") and a DC32 design (a badger), both shown clipped to an EFF-branded lanyard.

Tickets were priced at $70, with the listing framed explicitly as a way to support EFF's digital-privacy and online-freedom advocacy rather than as a product sale in its own right. The Eventbrite page for the DC34 run covered August 7-9, 2026 in Las Vegas and shows sales as ended, indicating the run sold out or closed with the con. No independent maker page, GitHub repo, or Hackaday.io project could be found describing construction details, and nothing in the available sources indicates this item is electronic (no MCU, LEDs, or display are mentioned or visible), so those fields are left blank rather than assumed.
