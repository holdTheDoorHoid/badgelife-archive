The "fuck" Note SAO is one of five items BigFuckingBadge produced for DEF CON 34 (August 2026), part of the maker's recurring "OffensiveSAO" lineup alongside the DFIU and IFLFU SAOs. Rather than only plugging into a host badge, it can also run standalone as a small battery-powered lapel badge with a magnetic clip, letting the wearer pick a mood/message to display in the moment.

It sold for $20 through BigFuckingBadge's presale (arranged by email) and drops at DEF CON 34's BadgeLife Village; as of this research the maker's site marks it sold out. No hardware specifications (MCU, LED type, display) or design files are published for it.
