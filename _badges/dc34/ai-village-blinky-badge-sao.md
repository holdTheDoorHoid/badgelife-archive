Abhinav Panda, working as Hackerware.io, designed the AI Village badge for DEF CON 32, and the DC34 community sheet lists this entry as "AI Village Blinky Badge & SAO" - a blinky badge paired with a companion SAO for the 2026 event. Public research did not turn up a maker's write-up, storefront listing, or press coverage specific to the DC34 (2026) edition, so this entry records only what the sheet and the maker's track record establish.

The same maker built AI Village's badge for DEF CON 32 (2024): a pair of robot dogs rendered in UV print with a reflective, "metal-look" varnished surface, whose RGB dog-tag blinked, whose eyes shifted color slowly, and whose horns pulsed as if signaling, all powered by two CR2032 coin cells, documented on Hackster.io. Whether the DC34 edition reuses or updates that design, what its MCU and LED setup are, and how it was distributed (con badge vs. add-on SAO, free vs. sold) could not be confirmed as of this check, so those fields are left blank rather than assumed to match the prior year.
