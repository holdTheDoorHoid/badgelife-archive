The FloridaMan 2026 badge is the entry credential for FloridaMan, an annual Florida-themed party held during DEF CON 34 in Las Vegas, sponsored by Sophos, Onyx, Mimecast, Gurucul, and NATECORP. Buying the $90 badge got attendees into the party (open bar included) and, per the store's own pitch — "step into the arcade, play some games, and land on the leaderboard" — doubled as a game token for a live, arcade-themed hacking challenge with an online scoreboard. The community badge sheet describes the mechanic as an "NFC and Online Hackable Game," suggesting the badge carries an NFC tag that ties into the challenge, though no maker page spells out the technical implementation.

As of this research pass the badge is sold out — the product page reads "No longer shipping. Come to DEF CON!" — and no hardware specifics (microcontroller, LEDs, display, power, SAO support) or open-source files were published anywhere findable. No Hackaday.io project or GitHub repository for the badge turned up. The listed maker on the community sheet is "Floridaman," with the store and party site crediting @jonathansinger; contact details (Discord, email, handle) came from the original sheet import and are preserved as-is.
