BigFuckingBadge's PORTAL Gun SAO is a small companion piece to their DEF CON 34 flagship, the oversized PORTAL badge (a Companion-Cube-shaped 14"x14" badge). Styled after the video game's iconic gun, the SAO plugs into the badge's SAO header and cycles through a blue portal-mode light, an orange (the maker jokingly says "or was it red?") portal-mode light, and off, with the maker's page teasing an additional interaction between the SAO and the main badge that isn't otherwise specified.

It sells for $20 as part of BigFuckingBadge's DC34 lineup, offered through presale (Venmo, PayPal, CashApp, or cash) ahead of the con and picked up in person at scheduled BadgeLife Village "drop" times during DEF CON 34. The maker's page lists quantities as limited but does not give an exact production number.

No public hardware or firmware repository could be confirmed for this SAO: two GitHub links carried over from the community sheet (`portal-gun-sao-dc33` and `portal-gun-sao-dc33-code`) both return 404 and are absent from the maker's current public repo list, so the MCU, LED type, and open-source status are left unconfirmed rather than guessed.
