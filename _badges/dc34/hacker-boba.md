The Hacker Boba is a beginner-friendly soldering kit from Brooke's Bytes (maker handle 000000widow), shaped as a glowing cup of boba milk tea and built as a keychain. It uses no microcontroller: three RGB LEDs flash on their own once power is applied from a CR2032 coin cell, switched by a physical slide switch, so there is no firmware for a builder to configure. A printed QR code on the badge links to a Google Maps search for nearby boba shops.

It was sold through the maker's Uberflux storefront for $10, with drops timed to the DEF CON 34 Badge Life Village (Aug. 7 and 8, 2026, 4PM sessions). All 54 units made were sold, and the listing was marked sold out as of early September 2026. No hardware files, firmware, or third-party coverage of the badge were found; the storefront listing is the only substantive source.
