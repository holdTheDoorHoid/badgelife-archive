The PORTAL Badge is BigFuckingBadge's entry for DEF CON 34: a 14-inch by 14-inch, Companion-Cube-shaped badge themed around Valve's Portal. It is touch-activated and includes working "portal" effects, mini-games, and audio clips pulled from the game, along with a set of unlockable achievements and at least one easter egg. It ships alongside a separately sold companion SAO, the PORTAL Gun (blue/orange portal modes, plus off), which is designed to interact with the main badge.

The badge was offered as a limited-quantity presale at $120, rising to $150 if bought at the convention through Hacker Warehouse. BigFuckingBadge also planned drops at the BadgeLife Village across several time slots during DEF CON 34. No chip, LED, or open-source hardware/firmware details could be confirmed — the maker's GitLab share link for the project did not yield readable content through available tools, so those fields are left blank pending further research.
