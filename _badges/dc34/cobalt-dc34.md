It appears in the "DC34 @ LVCC" section of trueControl's own badge/addon information site, listed alongside the maker's other DC34-era work (Retro Memories 26, Telephreak 19, The Wand), but as of this research the item's own dedicated page on that site has not yet been published — the site explicitly notes that most of its pages are still empty and under construction.

Because being a corporate commission, the details of the badge's design, electronics, and distribution have not been made public, and no secondary coverage (press, forums, social posts) turned up during this research pass. This entry should be revisited once trueControl publishes the dedicated page or the item surfaces elsewhere.
