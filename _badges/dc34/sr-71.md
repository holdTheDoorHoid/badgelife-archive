The SR-71 is a DEF CON 34 giveaway from Lockheed Martin's Aeronautics Cyber Range (ACR) team, fielded at the Aerospace Village. Rather than a conventional wearable, it works as a trading-card-style item built around NFC: cards use NFC peer-to-peer and NFC reader modes to support a collectible card game, and collectors who complete a full set are entered into a raffle for Lockheed Martin and ACR swag.

The badge is not sold; it is only obtainable by participating in a CTF competition the ACR team runs at their Aerospace Village booth and on their Discord server. No independent coverage of this specific item — maker page, Hackaday.io project, repository, or press — could be found; the only additional link on record, an X/Twitter account for the ACR team, could not be reached during research. Lockheed Martin's ACR team previously brought the F35 badge to the Aerospace Village at DEF CON 33, so the SR-71 likely continues that lineage, though that connection has not been confirmed by a source.
