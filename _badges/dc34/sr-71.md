The SR-71 is a DEF CON 34 badge from Lockheed Martin's Aeronautics Cyber Range (ACR) team, run from their booth in the Aerospace Village. According to the team's community-sheet listing, the badge carries a trading-card-style game that uses NFC peer-to-peer and NFC reader modes; collectors who complete the full card set are entered into a raffle for Lockheed Martin and ACR swag.

The badge is not sold. The listing says it is only winnable, through a CTF competition the ACR team hosts at the Aerospace Village booth and on their Discord server. No independent coverage of this item (maker page, Hackaday.io project, repository, or press) could be found, and the team's X/Twitter account could not be read during research. The same team fielded the F35 badge at DEF CON 33, so the SR-71 appears to continue that line, though no source states the connection.
