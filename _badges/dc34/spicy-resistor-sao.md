The Spicy Resistor SAO is a circular, resistor-themed add-on board made by bud-lightbeer and sold through UberFlux for DEF CON 34 (2026). About 50mm across, it uses onboard LEDs to give the impression of a glowing resistor, and ships with a 180-degree adapter so it plugs into DEF CON 34's SAO headers; the design is stated as 3.0V tolerant, making it compatible with both that badge and other standard 3.3V SAO hosts.

It sold for $10 through UberFlux's online storefront and at the DEF CON 34 Badge Life Booth (Saturday, August 4), with all 47 units sold out by the time this entry was checked. No schematic, firmware, or Gerber files were published alongside the listing, and the specific MCU or LED part number was not stated.
