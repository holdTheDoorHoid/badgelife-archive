This is a rerun of H4r0ld & V3rbaal's House Of Hacker Horrors' first EFF BadgeLife design, a badger-head badge referencing EFF's Privacy Badger browser extension, originally sold for DEF CON 31 in 2023. Three years later, when the duo launched their third EFF design ("Electric Fist") for DEF CON 34, they offered this badge and their second design (the "All Your Data" Eagle, originally from DEF CON 33) as checkout add-ons "for those who missed out — or want to complete the set." As with their other EFF badges, proceeds went to the Electronic Frontier Foundation.

The badge is a PCB-style badger head in black, white, and gold, worn on an EFF-branded lanyard alongside a sheet of EFF stickers, matching the look of the original DEF CON 31 release. Orders were online-only through Eventbrite, with pickup at DEF CON 34's Demo Labs (which H4r0ld and V3rbaal, longtime DEF CON Goons and CFP reviewers, help run); unclaimed orders were donated to "a broke hacker." The event has since concluded and Eventbrite shows it sold out.

No technical details (microcontroller, LEDs, or display) are given anywhere in the maker's own listing, and this add-on's own price was not independently confirmed beyond the community sheet's $70 figure.
