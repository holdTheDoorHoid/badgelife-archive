The Baby Bender is a small robot-shaped SAO (Shitty Add-On) made by sqearlsalazar, sold under the Tindie listing "#badgelife Parallel Baby Robot (each)" with the tagline "Bite my shiny SAO" — a nod to Bender, the robot character from *Futurama*. It shipped fully assembled in a choice of gold, silver, or white-plated finishes, each fitted with fast color-changing LEDs that light up when the SAO is plugged into a powered badge header or run off an external supply.

The DEF CON 30 community sheet lists it at $10, available through the Hardware Hacking Village at DC30 (2022), with the sheet note pointing to the Tindie page as a preview of what the piece looks like rather than as the point of sale. The Tindie listing's own product photos are dated 2019–2020, suggesting the design predates DC30 and was carried forward or re-offered at the con; the listing is now marked out of stock. No maker's page, repo, or press coverage beyond the Tindie storefront could be located, so details like the MCU, LED part number, and total quantity made remain unconfirmed.
