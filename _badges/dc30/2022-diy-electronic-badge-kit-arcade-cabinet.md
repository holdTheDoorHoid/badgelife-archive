The 2022 Arcade Cabinet DIY Badge is a learn-to-solder electronic badge kit made by The Diana Initiative, a Las Vegas-based information-security conference for underrepresented groups in the field, for their Maker Village at the 2022 event (August 10-11, 2022, The Westin Las Vegas, held the same week as DEF CON 30). The badge's PCB is shaped like an arcade cabinet, part of a recurring practice at Diana Initiative's Maker Village of giving attendees a hands-on soldering project each year.

Little else about the kit could be confirmed directly: the maker's own pages describing it (on dianainitiative.org) returned a 404 when fetched during this research pass, and the sheet's original source link — an archived Eventbrite ticket page for the 2023 conference — is a general ticket listing rather than a page about the badge, and could not be reached at all in this session. Search-engine indexing of the Diana Initiative's Maker Village 2023 page suggests the 2022 badge was later kept on hand as a reference project (with a build video, written instructions, and flashing instructions/code) alongside a newer 2023 "Pair-o-Dice" badge, but this could not be verified against the actual page text, so it is noted here only as low-confidence corroboration. Chip, LEDs, price, quantity, distribution numbers, open-source status, and photos of the badge remain unknown.
