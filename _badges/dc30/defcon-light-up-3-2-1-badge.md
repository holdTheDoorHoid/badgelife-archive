Joshua McCoy, a 16-year-old high school student, designed this LED PCB badge as his first PCB project after attending DEF CON for the first time and falling for the "badgelife" scene. The badge is a 2-layer board powered by a CR2032 coin cell, with LEDs and resistors on the front and space for the battery on the back. Its design riffs on the well-known DEF CON attendee joke about surviving the con on "3 hours of sleep, two meals, and 1 shower."

McCoy posted the project to PCBWay's sponsorship program, seeking a manufacturing sponsorship to produce roughly 50 units to hand out at DEF CON 30 (2022) in Las Vegas, offering to feature PCBWay's branding on the badge packaging in return. He shared a pick-and-place file and a bill of materials via MEGA links on the listing and said he would provide Gerber files to anyone who emailed him directly.

No independent coverage, maker social media, or repository for this badge turned up in research; the PCBWay sponsorship page is the only source found, so it isn't confirmed whether the badge was ultimately fabricated and distributed at the con.
