The GrrCon 0xA 10th Anniversary Skull is a badge listed on the community badge-tracking
sheet for DEF CON 30 (2022), credited to GrrCON with design work by Hak4Kids. The name
suggests it marked GrrCON's tenth anniversary that year, with a skull motif and a "0xA"
(hexadecimal for ten) reference in the title. The sheet lists a $60 price and a
production run of 100, ordered or picked up via a Google Form link and reportedly hand
delivered at DC30.

No independent source could be found to confirm these details or add further specifics
(maker/chip/LED information, images, or current availability). The order form linked
from the sheet has since gone offline (404), GrrCON's current website has no archive of
the item, and web and social searches for the title turned up nothing. The entry is left
largely as imported, with the shape and theme tagged as "skull" based on the title alone.
