The Artful Joker SAO was made by the team at Deepnet.store for DEF CON 30 (2022). It reimagines the Joker in a style the Tindie blog described as "a blend of graffiti and old-timey comic book artwork," with red LED eyes and a blue teardrop animation on the face, designed to complement the white-and-black look of that year's official badge.

Coverage of the piece comes from a single Tindie blog roundup of DEF CON 30 badges, published in August 2022, which noted that Deepnet.store planned to open a Tindie store for it "very soon" but had not done so at the time of writing. No follow-up storefront listing, GitHub repo, or Hackaday project page for the SAO has been found, so its technical specs (microcontroller, LED type, SAO header version), price, quantity made, and current availability remain undocumented.
