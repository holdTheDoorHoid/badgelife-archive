DC Punks - the maker duo of Kay Kidoutai and NeonPlaidPants - appears on the DEF CON 30 (2022) community badge sheet with a table at the Hardware Hacking Village, but the sheet row carries no item name, description, or price, only the maker's name (recorded there with a typo, "Kay Kidoutsi"). This is the earliest sheet appearance found for the duo, who went on to release a documented string of independent badges and SAOs in following years: a "Sh*tPad SAO" at DC31, and three items at DC32 (a pink Y2K countdown badge, an "Edgerunner License," and a "Rick Roll SAO"), sold through their Tindie store and promoted from their dcpunks.computer site and Linktree.

No title, image, or description for whatever they brought to DEF CON 30 specifically could be located: their own site's earliest Wayback Machine snapshot dates to September 2023, well after DC30, and its image assets all belong to later releases. Their Linktree confirms the correct spelling of both members' names and links (Tindie, Mastodon, Bluesky, kidoutai.com) but gives no founding date or history predating July 2023. This entry is left as `unknown` for the item itself, matching the same gap already documented for this duo's DC33 sheet listing.
