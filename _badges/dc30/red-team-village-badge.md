Red Team Village sold this as its first ("inaugural") DEF CON badge, offered for $75 through a dedicated Shopify storefront (rtv-swag.myshopify.com) ahead of DEF CON 30 in August 2022, with pickup at the village's space at the Flamingo. Beyond the price and that ordering arrangement, no further details about the badge's design, electronics, or production run survived on either the storefront or Red Team Village's own site by the time of this research pass — the Shopify shop has since been taken down entirely, and the one archived crawl of the product page only recorded a redirect to the store's password screen rather than the listing itself.

Red Team Village went on to produce further badges for later DEF CONs under the same "RTV" branding (see the DC31 "Pip-Boy" and DC34 "D20" entries), suggesting this DC30 badge was the first in an ongoing series, though no source found during this pass explicitly frames it that way.
