Rogue's Village — a magic-and-social-engineering-themed community at DEF CON — sold this poker chip badge through Four Suits Co, the magic collective behind the village. Billed as "the first ever Rogues Village badge," it is a genuine full-clay casino poker chip stamped with the Rogue's Village DEF CON 30 logo, sold for $10 through the Four Suits Co online shop with fulfillment promised before the August 2022 conference.

Rather than an electronic SAO or standalone badge, it ships with a 3D-printed holder and lanyard clasp so it clips onto a wearer's main con badge as swag, but it can also be pulled free and used as an actual poker chip — the listing notes it doubles as a prop for "secret moves" the Rogue's Village crew taught in person at the village and over Twitter. The listing described availability as limited; no specific print run number was published. The original store page has since gone offline (404), so this entry is sourced from a July 2022 Wayback Machine capture of the live listing.
