The Daft Helmet SAO is a small add-on board from Georgia-based maker Twinkle Twinkie, shaped like a gold-and-black robot helmet in the style of Daft Punk's stage costumes. It plugs into a badge's SAO header (a keyed 2x3 / SAOv2 connector, backward-compatible with older 2x2 headers) and runs on the host badge's 3.3V rail, lighting up with a static rainbow glow rather than any programmable or addressable pattern.

It sold on Tindie for $20, fully assembled and bundled with a SAOv2 female connector so buyers without a compatible badge could still wire it up. As of the most recent check the Tindie listing shows the seller "on a break," so the item is not currently orderable; no design files, firmware, or LED part numbers are published on the product page.
