The Mando Kitty SAO is a cat-shaped simple add-on sold by Twinkle Twinkie for DEF CON 30 (2022), pairing a gold-and-purple helmet styled after The Mandalorian with glowing pink "This Is The Way" text. It shipped pre-assembled for $20 with a SAOv2 (2x3 keyed) female connector, and the maker notes it also mates with older 2x2 SAO headers, though a connector upgrade is recommended for a sturdier mechanical fit.

Twinkle Twinkie sold several similarly themed SAOs (Avocado, Black Cat, Daft Helmet) around the same event through the same Tindie storefront. Beyond the maker's product listing, no build files, chip details, or third-party coverage of this specific piece turned up; the Tindie page itself currently shows the listing on pause rather than actively taking new orders.
