The Biology Girl Soldering Kit is a learn-to-solder badge designed by Steph Piper of Maker Queen (also credited on the community sheet under her Elkei Education studio), depicting a cartoon biologist rendered on PCB in a jewel-toned green, blue, and silver "stained glass" style. Like the rest of Piper's soldering-kit line, it is a beginner electronics project: builders solder on an LED, a slide switch, and a coin-cell battery holder, then wear the finished piece as a pin via an attached badge clasp.

The kit sold for $9.95 through Adafruit, which also notes it won a 2019 ROAR! Awards Gold Winner for Best Teen/Tween Product. Piper distributes her soldering-kit badges through Adafruit, Mouser, BlinkyParts, and Elecrow rather than exclusively at conventions, and the community sheet's inclusion of it in the DC30 (2022) badge list suggests it was carried or shown at that year's DEF CON. As of this check, Adafruit no longer stocks it and directs buyers to a related "Space Girl" badge instead.
