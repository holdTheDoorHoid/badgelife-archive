TwinkleTwinkie & Wire's Clock Badge is the pair's DEF CON 30 (2022) indie badge: a wearable digital clock shaped like a gray, googly-eyed fuzzy character. It leans into a VCR-clock aesthetic, including the classic blinking "12:00" display after a power loss, and drives its time display and color animations through 30 addressable RGB LEDs.

The badge runs on either 2x AA batteries or USB-C power, selected with a battery/USB toggle switch rather than a conventional on/off switch. To stretch battery life to about 18 hours, the display goes dark after 10 minutes without a button press and then wakes briefly before the top of each hour. It was sold assembled through the maker's Tindie store for $90, including two AA batteries and a silver lanyard, though it did not ship internationally.

Beyond the Tindie listing and the maker's own Hackaday.io project page (titled "TwinkleTwinkie & Wire's 2022 Indie DEF CON 30 Badge"), no build files, firmware repository, or detailed technical writeup (MCU, display panel, LED part number) could be located, so those fields are left blank rather than guessed.
