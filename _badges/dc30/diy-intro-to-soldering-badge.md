The DIY Intro to Soldering Badge was a $20 beginner soldering kit sold around DEF CON 30 (2022) by Hak4Kidz, a nonprofit that runs youth-focused ethical-hacking events and villages at conferences including DEF CON and GrrCON. The community badge sheet lists 150 units and describes ordering through a Google Form ahead of the con, with pickup or hand delivery at DC30 itself; that form has since gone dead, and no archived Hak4Kidz page from 2022 documents the badge's design.

Hak4Kidz sold a similarly priced ($20) "Learn to Solder Badge and SAO" kit the following year for DEF CON 31, built around their robot mascot Tinker with solder-it-yourself RGB LED eyes and a SAO connector, sold through their Hak4Kidz Lab storefront on Tindie. That listing's description -- framing the kit as reviving in-person Hak4Kidz activities -- makes it plausible this DC30 badge was the first run of the same recurring soldering-practice line, but no source directly ties the two together, so this entry does not carry over the later kit's specific design details.
