BadgePirates is a Kansas City-based, SecKC-affiliated badge-making crew active since at least 2017, known for badges at BSides KC, BSides STL, OzSec, SANS Hackfest, and CactusCon, among others. The community sheet for DEF CON 30 (2022) records BadgePirates as a verified maker/attendee that year under the title "Arrr!! Badge," but the sheet itself notes no details were released about the badge.

Research into BadgePirates' own portfolio site turned up a badge image labeled "BSides KC 2022" — a ray-gun-shaped PCB with concentric coil rings — but nothing tied to DEF CON 30 specifically, and no title, image, or description referencing "Arrr" or a pirate theme. Their GitHub org page returns a 404, and their Hackaday.io and X/Twitter profiles could not be reached to check further. Without a source that actually shows or describes this DC30 item, the entry is left as unknown rather than assumed to be the BSides KC piece.
