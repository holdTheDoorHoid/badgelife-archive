The 3-2-1 Badge was the debut project of EkhoBadges (maker "Ekho"), a first-time badge designer who gave the badge away for free at DEF CON 30 in 2022. Rather than selling it or trading for stickers, EkhoBadges distributed the badge through small, unannounced "drops" around the convention -- each drop offering 5 to 15 badges, one per person, with locations revealed in real time over Twitter and Telegram. As of a pre-con update in July 2022, the maker had 55 units on hand across 4 planned drops.

By the maker's own account this was a simple first attempt, made under part-shortage and production constraints, and the design and theme were deliberately kept secret until the week of the convention. It runs on a replaceable CR2032 coin cell. The maker's promotional site (ekhobadge.sbs) has since gone offline, and no snapshot survives from after the design reveal, so details like the badge's look, MCU, LED count, and the final quantity actually handed out at DEF CON 30 could not be confirmed.
