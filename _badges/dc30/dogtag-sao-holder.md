The DogTag SAO Holder is not a badge in its own right but a piece of wearable infrastructure for the #badgelife SAO ecosystem: a metal dog tag fitted with a 2x3 female SAO header and a CR2032 battery holder, so any standalone add-on can be plugged in, powered, and worn as jewelry away from a host badge. Each purchase from maker Sqearsalazar (sqearlsalazar) includes two dog tags, two pin-and-clutch sets for cloth mounting, two CR2032 battery holders with batteries, two lanyards, and two ornament strings, along with the two SAO connectors themselves.

It was sold at the Hardware Hacking Village at DEF CON 30 and has also been a recurring item in the maker's Tindie store since at least 2019, going in and out of stock over subsequent years rather than being a single con-specific run.
