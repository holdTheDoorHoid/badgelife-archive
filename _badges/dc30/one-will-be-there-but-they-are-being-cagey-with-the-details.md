DC801, the Salt Lake City DEF CON group, was listed on the DC30 (2022) community
badge sheet as planning to bring something, but the sheet's own entry says only
that they were "being cagey with the details" -- no name, link, or description
was ever filled in.

Research for this entry could not turn up what DC801 actually brought to DC30.
The group has run an ongoing hardware badge platform, the Black Mage Badge
(nRF52840-based, with a 2.4" touchscreen and a custom game engine), across
multiple DEF CONs starting at DC28 (2020), with a major "Chapter 2" content
release for DC32 (2024) -- but nothing found ties a DC30-specific release,
repository, or announcement to that platform, and no separate DC30 badge project
surfaced either. It's possible DC801 simply reused an existing badge that year
without a fresh public writeup, but that can't be confirmed from available
sources, so this entry is left as unknown pending better information.
