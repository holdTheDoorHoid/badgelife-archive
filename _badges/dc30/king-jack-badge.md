The King Jack Badge is a 3D-printed novelty badge that seller "technick" (maker NiCk) made for DEF CON 30 in 2022, sold on Tindie for $25 (with volume discounts for 5 or more) and picked up in person at DEF CON or BSides Las Vegas rather than shipped. It depicts the maker's recurring character "Jack" wearing a jewel-encrusted crown, marking DEF CON's 30th anniversary, and comes with a matching DEF CON 30 nameplate. It has no electronics: no microcontroller, LEDs, or display, and is not part of the SAO ecosystem.

The badge is the third entry in an annual series: NiCk began handing out "Jack" badges informally between DC 25 and DC 26, then started selling them at DC 27 as "New Jack" (roughly 240 sold across BSides LV, DEF CON, and DerbyCon that year). DC 29 followed with "Monopoly Jack," styled with a top hat and monocle, which sold out. King Jack, for DC 30, continued the tradition with a crown. The Tindie listing was marked out of stock as of the researcher's check.
