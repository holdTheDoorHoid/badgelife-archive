The L0nely Hackers Club SAO Holder is a hand-assembled badge made by H4r0ld & V3rbaal's House of Hacker Horrors for DEF CON 30 (2022). Rather than carrying its own display or microcontroller, it works as a wearable hub for other SAOs: it has four badge-side SAO connectors, three surface-mount LEDs with matching resistors, and runs on two CR2032 coin cells. A lanyard was included with each unit.

Beyond its function as an SAO expansion board, the badge hides a cryptographic challenge that, when solved, points to Lonely Hackers Club meetup information at DEF CON — a nod to LHC's identity as a hacker social group that has produced a badge for the con most years since.

It sold for $40 through the makers' Tindie store. As of research, that store listing is still online but marked as "taking a break," so current stock and total quantity produced could not be confirmed.
