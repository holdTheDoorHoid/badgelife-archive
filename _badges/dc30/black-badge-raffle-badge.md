The Black Badge Raffle is a long-running, unofficial DEF CON tradition: a community-run raffle giving entrants a shot at a previous year's official DEF CON black badge (itself a free-entry-for-life pass to the con). For DEF CON 30 (2022), the raffle's Twitter/X account, @DefconRaffle, announced this badge/ticket item alongside a separate $5 SAO, with drop times to be posted on the account itself.

According to the archive's DC31 (2023) entry for the same maker, leftover DC30 Black Badge Raffle badges were still being resold a year later at $100 each in very limited numbers — indirect confirmation that this item was made, distributed, and sold out at DC30. Beyond that, the specifics of this DC30 item (final price, exact design, and confirmed quantity distributed versus the "80 will be available" figure from the original community sheet) could not be independently verified: the @DefconRaffle Twitter/X account is no longer reachable through automated tooling, and the nearest Wayback Machine snapshot from August 2022 did not preserve any tweet content.
