The L0nely Hackers Triple Threat is an unofficial DEF CON 30 (2022) badge made by TechGirl's House of Blinky for the Lonely Hackers Club, an online Discord-era hacker community not affiliated with DEF CON itself. Rather than one PCB, it's three independent mini-badges that spell out "LHC": the L plays a Tetris-like block game, the H puts on a randomized LED light show, and the C transmits infrared codes that can switch IR-equipped devices on or off. The set ships with a custom lanyard and two included CR2032 coin cells, plus a requirement for three AA batteries that are not included.

The badge sold for $50 through the maker's Tindie store. As of the 2022 listing it had gone off sale with a note that it would return "after DC30," and no later restock or re-release could be confirmed. No hardware files, firmware, or MCU/LED specifications were published or found, so those fields are left blank rather than guessed.
