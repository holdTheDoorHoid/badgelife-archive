The H2HC 2024 Hacker ID Card is a credit-card-sized (CR80 format) printed identity card that Brian of security-bits.de produced on site at Hackers to Hackers Conference 2024 in São Paulo using a double-sided 600 DPI card printer. Unlike the PCB-based electronic badges from earlier H2HC years (2015, 2017, 2018) that the same maker and others contributed, this is a non-electronic paper/PVC card meant to double as a reusable name tag and a "business card" of quick links.

The front carries the attendee's photo and handle or name; the back holds up to four scannable QR codes (for example a vCard, X/Twitter handle, and GitHub profile) plus an optional date of birth. The first edition, debuted at H2HC 2024, came in five colors (blue, green, orange, pink, red) with five different name-tag-side layouts. Attendees could get a card printed in person at the conference or by emailing their photo/art, handle, name, and desired QR links to cardprint@security-bits.de; Brian's printer refused designs meant to imitate a formal state-issued ID, though flags and similar flourishes were allowed.

There is no price or fixed production run — cards were made to order and given away rather than sold, so this entry treats it as a free conference credential rather than a commercial product.
