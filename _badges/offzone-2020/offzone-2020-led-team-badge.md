BI.ZONE designed this badge for the CTF final that was meant to close out OFFZONE 2020, the Russian security conference it runs. The board carried the logos of the teams competing in the CTF, paired with one RGB LED per team, so that everyone holding a badge could see the live state of the competition reflected in color and lighting pattern on their own board.

BI.ZONE finished the concept, the board design, and even a full manufacturing run — enough boards, by the team's own tally, to cover about 80 square meters, prompting an office joke that they could floor an apartment with the leftover PCBs. The 2020 event was then cancelled outright because of the COVID-19 pandemic, so the badge was never distributed or worn; 2021 followed with no badge at all before OFFZONE resumed with a new badge design in 2022.

No source found gives the badge's MCU, LED part number, dimensions, or any production/design-file details beyond the "80 square meters" figure — BI.ZONE's retrospective article is a brief historical mention rather than a technical writeup, and it is the only coverage of this specific badge available.
