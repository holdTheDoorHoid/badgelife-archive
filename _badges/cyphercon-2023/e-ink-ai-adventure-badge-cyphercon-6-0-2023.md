The e-ink AI Adventure Badge was TYMKRS's official electronic badge for CypherCon 6.0, held March 30-31, 2023 in Milwaukee under the theme "Underground Singularity (AI)." Instead of blinky LEDs, the badge centers on a 3.7" e-paper display (416x240 pixels, roughly a 4-second refresh) running a text-adventure game structured like an old-school webring, letting attendees click or button through a small world of in-badge "webpages."

To build the game, TYMKRS developer Whisker wrote a custom scripting language called "dogteeth" specifically for authoring the badge's adventure content. TYMKRS documented the build in a Reddit post to r/Tymkrs and posted a demo video, "Cyphercon 6.0 Electronic Badge," to Vimeo.

Technical details beyond the display (MCU, LED count, battery, price, and production quantity) were not stated in any source reached during this research pass.
