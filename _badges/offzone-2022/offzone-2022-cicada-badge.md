The OFFZONE 2022 badge is shaped like a cicada in silhouette, a deliberate double reference: to the in-game faction from *The Last of Us* and to the hacker collective "Cicada 3301." Made by Russian cybersecurity firm BI.ZONE for its OFFZONE conference in Moscow, the badge continued the event's tradition of doubling as the payment device for the conference's internal currency.

New for 2022, the badge carried two 4-pin "shitty add-on" (SAO v1.69bis) connectors -- a standard originally developed for DEF CON -- so attendees could design and bring their own add-ons, whether purely decorative PCBs or fully functional devices. OFFZONE's organizers published the connector specification ahead of the event and ran a community contest inviting attendees to submit add-on designs.

The badge is one entry in a yearly line BI.ZONE has produced for OFFZONE since 2018 (a Java Card badge in 2018, a floppy-disk badge in 2019, an LED team badge planned but never distributed in 2020 due to the pandemic cancellation, and the cicada badge marking the event's 2022 return). Technical specifics for the cicada badge itself -- microcontroller, LED count, display, and production quantity -- were not disclosed in the source material found.
