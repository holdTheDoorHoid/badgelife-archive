The HITB Electronic Badge was the official electronic conference badge for Hack In The Box Security Conference (HITBSecConf) 2021 Singapore, built by Techmaker, a "Friend of HITB" embedded-electronics team. It was previewed live at the event in a dedicated HITB Studio session, "Hack At The Studio: Building the Ultimate Hacker Badge," held on August 27, 2021 with Techmaker alongside HITB's Dhillon "L33tdawg" Kannabhiran and Sreeman Shanker, and the session was also recorded and posted to YouTube.

Beyond confirming the badge existed and who made it, none of the pages found in this pass describe its hardware, appearance, price, production run, or how (or whether) it reached attendees at the fully-online 2021 Singapore event. Techmaker separately maintains its own embedded-electronics community (techmakerua, blog.techmaker.ua) with a "Hardware Badge" tag on its Medium blog that may cover this badge or a related one in more detail; that page could not be read in this pass because Medium's Cloudflare protection blocked automated access.
