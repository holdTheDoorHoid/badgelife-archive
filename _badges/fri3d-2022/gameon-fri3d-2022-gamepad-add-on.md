GameOn is a gamepad add-on built by Wim Van Gool for Fri3d Camp's 2022 badge, designed to turn that badge into a handheld game console. The board adds a joystick, a set of face buttons (Start, Select, A, B), optional shoulder buttons mounted on the underside, and an option to use two bottom pads as a touchpad instead.

Beyond controls, the add-on includes a microSD card holder and a small speaker amplifier, letting games on the badge load additional content and play audio. According to the project page, early units were given to developers around the event to build games for the platform, though it's unclear whether the add-on ever saw a wider release beyond that early-adopter group.

No repository, storefront, or design files were found for this project beyond its Hackaday.io page, so its microcontroller, exact bill of materials, and availability details remain undocumented here.
