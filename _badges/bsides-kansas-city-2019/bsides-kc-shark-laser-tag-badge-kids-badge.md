BadgePirates made this as the kids' badge for BSidesKC 2019, sold as a Tindie listing at $34.99 (or $40 each for two or more). Attendees had to solder three components onto the board themselves as a learn-to-solder exercise before it worked.

Once assembled, the badge plays the "Baby Shark" song at startup, then acts as an infrared laser-tag badge: pressing the "Pew" button fires an IR shot, and each badge can absorb three hits (with LEDs going dark per hit and the badge vibrating) before needing a 15-second reset to play again.

The Tindie listing does not name the microcontroller, LED type/count, or how many units were made, and gives no shipping/availability status as of this check, so those fields are left blank rather than guessed.
