The Glitch Gallery is a camera-shaped SAINTCON minibadge made by Pips for 801 Labs' "Glitch Gallery" event, held on the Thursday of SAINTCON at theFINDlabs. Two hundred were made through JLCPCB and given out to people who attended the event, rather than sold.

It's a simple solder-it-yourself keepsake: attendees solder on an LED, a resistor, and pin headers, rated "Intermediate" difficulty. No microcontroller, display, or SAO header is documented, and the maker's listing doesn't name the LED's color or part number.

The minibadge.wiki record for this badge sits in the 2026 data export, but its own conferenceYear field is stamped 2025 -- likely a submitter typo, or the badge may actually belong to SAINTCON 2025. This entry is kept under saintcon-2026 per the file's placement; the discrepancy is noted here in case a future check settles which year is correct.
