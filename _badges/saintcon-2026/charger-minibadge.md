The Charger Minibadge is a small SAINTCON add-on by Pips that turns any badge's power rail into a USB-C charging port. It has no microcontroller or LEDs of its own — it is a passive pass-through board that plugs into (or otherwise draws power from) a host SAINTCON badge and exposes a USB-C connector, delivering up to 500mA to charge a phone, a pair of headphones, or even another badge. It ships pre-soldered, with only pin headers left for the recipient to solder on before use.

Pips made 35 of the boards, fabricated through JLCPCB, for the SAINTCON 2026 "Personal" minibadge category. According to the maker, they can be obtained by trading with Pips in person or by buying one through Pips' Tindie store, though no direct Tindie listing was found to confirm current price or stock.
