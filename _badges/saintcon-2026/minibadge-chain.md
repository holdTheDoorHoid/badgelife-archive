The Minibadge chain is a small PCB accessory made by Pips for SAINTCON's 2026 minibadge scene. Rather than being a badge in its own right, it is a wiring/linking board: builders measure and cut lengths of wire, solder them end to end onto the board, and add pin sockets so that several SAINTCON minibadges can be strung together into a single chained display. It carries 3.3V/5V power between the linked boards, meant to be powered either by the maker's own "Minibadge Devboard" or any suitable external supply.

Two hundred units were made through JLCPCB, and the wiki rates the soldering difficulty as "Beginner," fitting its simple through-hole wiring assembly. The listing points buyers to the maker's Tindie store, but that page could not be independently verified here — the storefront currently sits behind a Cloudflare challenge that blocked both automated fetch attempts — so price and live availability are left unconfirmed.
