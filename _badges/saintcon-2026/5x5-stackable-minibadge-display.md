The 5x5 Stackable Minibadge Display is a badge accessory made by Pips (also the creator behind minibadge.wiki and several other SAINTCON 2026 minibadges, including a "Minibadge chain" and a "Minibadge Display Devboard") for the SAINTCON 2026 minibadge scene. As its name implies, it appears to be a PCB sized to hold a 5x5 grid of standard minibadges, intended to be used with Pips' companion display/power boards so that a wall or shelf of minibadges can be lit and mounted as one unit rather than as individual loose boards.
