The Perfboard prototyping minibadge is a bare-bones minibadge by the SAINTCON regular Pips: rather than a fixed design, it is a small green PCB laid out as a perfboard grid with a minibadge's connector pins already soldered on. Someone who wants to build their own one-off minibadge at the conference can hand-solder LEDs or other small components onto the grid without needing to design and fabricate a custom PCB first — "solder LEDs and let it rip," as the listing puts it.

Pips had 200 of these made through JLCPCB for SAINTCON 2026 and lists them as acquired by trading with the maker in person rather than by purchase. No hardware files, firmware, or further write-up were found beyond the community listing.
