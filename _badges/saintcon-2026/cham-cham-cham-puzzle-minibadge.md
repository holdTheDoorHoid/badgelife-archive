The 참참참 (Cham Cham Cham) Puzzle Minibadge is a SAINTCON 2026 minibadge made by the YouTube creator behind the channel "Soldering&3Dprinting" (@Herushan1). It is based on the Korean children's hand game "cham cham cham," reworked as an electronic puzzle: an RGB light pattern cues a direction (left, right, or center) that the player has to match or counter, similar to the original game's mechanic.

The maker posted an earlier preview video showing the game concept and light pattern still being tuned, followed by a "final design" video confirming the minibadge's design was locked in ahead of SAINTCON 2026, with assembly instructions and an explanation of the puzzle promised in person on the Wednesday of the conference. No storefront, repository, or build guide has surfaced yet, and details like the microcontroller, LED type/count, price, and quantity have not been published anywhere found during this research.
