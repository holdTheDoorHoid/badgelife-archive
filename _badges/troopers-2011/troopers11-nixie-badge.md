The TROOPERS11 Nixie Badge was the conference badge for TROOPERS11, an IT security conference held in Heidelberg, Germany in April 2011, designed by Jeffrey Gough. Its centerpiece is a glowing nixie tube that displays a running numeric score, which conference organizers could update through a special programming interface as attendees took part in various activities and challenges.

Beyond the score display, the badge built in a few playful and hacker-friendly touches: the Cat-5 network cable used as a lanyard also served as the badge's power switch, and a hidden capacitive-touch pad let wearers reveal secret messages on the nixie digit. The board also carried spare SMT and through-hole prototyping pads, and attendees were reported to have added their own LEDs and speakers to their badges during the event.

No schematic, bill of materials, or firmware for the badge has been recovered in public sources, and neither the microcontroller used nor pricing and production-quantity details are documented. The high-voltage nixie driver circuitry was reportedly protected with heat shrink and silicone encapsulation for safety.
