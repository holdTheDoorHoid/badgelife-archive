BSides Tampa 2018 offered an "Electronic Badge Assembly" activity as one of its hands-on tracks, alongside a Lock Pick Village, a Car Hacking Village, a CTF competition, an escape room, and resume review. More than 750 attendees took part, according to a writeup by St. Petersburg College's Cybersecurity & Digital Forensics Club, the only independent source found that confirms the activity actually happened.

No source found describes the badge's actual design: no maker credit beyond the event's host (the Tampa Bay chapter of (ISC)2), no chip, LED, or display specification, no price or production quantity, and no genuine photo of the item. A photo cited by badge.gallery as documenting this badge is a Wikimedia Commons stock image simply captioned as assorted hacker-con badges, with no connection to BSides Tampa stated anywhere in its own description — it was not used here.
