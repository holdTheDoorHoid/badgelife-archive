The DEF CON 23 (2015) official attendee badge broke from the electronic-badge tradition of prior years: it was a genuine 7-inch vinyl record, fully mastered and playable on a turntable rather than merely a decorative disc. Each side carried encoded text and ended in DTMF audio tones, continuing DEF CON's tradition of embedding a puzzle in the badge itself. The Human badge's encoded line read "Mystery is the source of all true art and science," while the Blue attendee variant carried "be cool but care." Beyond the general Human/Blue/Green attendee tiers, DEF CON issued separate vinyl badges for Press, Vendor and Goon roles, all part of the same pressing run.

Sitting apart from the vinyl badges was the Uber badge, built on a completely different substrate: a Lichtenberg figure — a fractal branching pattern etched into a block of PMMA acrylic by exposing it to a 5-million-volt particle accelerator — with four different radioactive samples embedded at its corners (a uranium-doped glass marble, a tritium vial inside a crystal skull, Trinitite collected near the Trinity test site, and additional uranium ore). Designer L0stBoy (Ryan "1o57" Clarke) described each point of the badge as housing "a different form radioactive material."

Little pricing, production-quantity, or exact color-per-tier detail turned up in the sources checked; the badges are documented mainly through the original defcon.org badge page, a fan writeup, an Internet Archive audio rip of the vinyl, and an active secondary market of collectors trading individual tier badges on eBay and Discogs a decade later.
