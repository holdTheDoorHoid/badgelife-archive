The Security BSides Jacksonville 2018 badge was a DIY solder kit organized by maker Kirball, planned as three similar but visually distinct variants for attendees, volunteers, and speakers, with sponsor and donor versions floated as a stretch goal if enough backing materialized. The Hackaday.io project page frames it as a call for help: Kirball was seeking badge sponsors and volunteers to help prep boards for bagging and to staff a soldering station at the conference itself.

No build log, schematic, firmware, or bill of materials was ever posted to the project page, so the badge's electronics (microcontroller, LEDs, power source) are undocumented. It is unclear from available sources whether the badges were ultimately produced and handed out as described or whether the project stalled at the planning/fundraising stage.

Kirball is also credited with the related BSides Orlando 2018 badge (a simpler discrete marquee kit given to attendees), the unofficial DEF CON 26 "#RescueOps" badge, and the Hack FSCJ fundraiser PCB badge, suggesting an active run of badge-kit projects for Florida-area cons and causes around 2017-2018.
