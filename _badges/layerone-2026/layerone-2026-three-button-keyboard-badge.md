The 2026 LayerOne badge is a three-button USB macro keyboard, designed by KRS, mmca, and charliex, with charliex contributing the artwork: a self-printed UV resin layer over the board. The badge also includes LED "blinkies." The conference program leaned into the joke that a full keyboard is unnecessary in 2026, labeling the badge's three keys for "AI-assisted programming" — Y, 2, and Enter, standing in for "Yes," "Always allow," and "Submit."

After the badges were distributed, riverside and Holly stepped in to fix an electrical issue affecting the whole batch by hand, according to LayerOne's own wrap-up post.

No technical specifics beyond this were published: the maker's posts do not name a microcontroller, LED type or count, display, price, or production quantity, and no photo of the finished badge could be found on LayerOne's site.
