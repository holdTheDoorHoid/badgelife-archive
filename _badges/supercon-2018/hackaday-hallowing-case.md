This is a 3D-printed protective case for the Adafruit HalloWing board, the electronic conference badge/giveaway that Hackaday handed out to attendees of the 2018 Hackaday Superconference. Maker todbot remixed an existing clip-on case design found online, adapting it to snap around the HalloWing board and its battery, and added Hackaday's Jolly Wrencher logo to the shell before printing it. The project was posted to Hackaday.io on November 25, 2018.

The case itself has no electronics — it is a purely mechanical accessory meant to protect the skull-shaped HalloWing board it clips onto. Design files (a Fusion 360 source file and an STL ready for printing) are shared for free on both Hackaday.io and Thingiverse, so anyone with a 3D printer and a HalloWing board can make their own.

## Make your own

Download `HackadayHalloWingCase.stl` (or the source `HalloWing Case Hackaday v2.f3d` Fusion 360 file) from the Hackaday.io project page or the linked Thingiverse listing, then 3D print it and snap it around an Adafruit HalloWing board and its battery.
