Max (Hackaday.io user shmuma) designed this 3D-printable case for the 2018 Hackaday Superconference badge as a lighter, easier-to-print alternative to an existing enclosure design. Uploaded to Hackaday.io in June 2019, the case protects the badge's PCB from the back while leaving its keyboard exposed and usable. Max built five of the badges with his daughter as an educational project.

The design was modeled in Fusion 360 and shared as nine STL files, including separate bottom and outer case pieces, with versions both featuring and omitting the Hackaday skull logo (the logo version needs multi-part printing on single-extrusion printers). The files are freely available on both Hackaday.io and Thingiverse; no price or production quantity applies since it was released as an open design rather than sold.
