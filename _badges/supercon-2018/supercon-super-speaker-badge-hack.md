Evan Juras built this as a hack of his own 2018 Supercon badge rather than a standalone badge design: he pulled the badge's stock 1-watt speaker and replaced it with a 5-watt speaker driven by a 7-watt amplifier, running the whole thing at 18 volts instead of the badge's normal supply. The result, as he put it, turned the badge into "an obnoxiously loud boombox" capable of roughly 90dB output — loud enough that in his own demo video of it at full volume, the recording microphone couldn't handle it cleanly.

The Hackaday.io project page is the only documentation of the build. It was posted as a completed project but never followed up with the promised circuit details, firmware, or files, so there is nothing to build from beyond the description and photo. It appears to be a one-off, not something distributed or sold.
