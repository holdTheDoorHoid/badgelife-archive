BSidesAugusta gave out a printed circuit board "badgelife"-style badge at BSidesAugusta 0xA, the tenth edition of the Augusta, GA cybersecurity conference, held Saturday, October 7, 2023. The organizers teased its return in a LinkedIn post and a matching Facebook video, describing it as "highly sought-after" and available for one day only, exclusively at the event.

Per the teaser, the back of the badge carries a link to a set of challenges along with hashes for attendees to crack, tying it into the con's CTF-style culture. No individual designer or fabricator was credited separately from the BSidesAugusta organization, and neither the technical specifics (microcontroller, LEDs, display, power) nor pricing or production quantity were published in any source found. No storefront listing, repository, or photo of the finished badge itself turned up in this research pass.
