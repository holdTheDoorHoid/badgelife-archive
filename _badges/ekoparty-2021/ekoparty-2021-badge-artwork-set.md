Ekoparty's 2021 conference badges were paper/print role-and-competition credentials rather than an electronic build — the con's first electronic badge came the following year in 2023. Ekoparty's own website hosts a set of numbered attachment pages, each showing one artwork variant: attendee, speaker, and workshop roles, ACFT competition placements for 1st through 3rd place, an ICFT player variant, and a "Lado B" (side B) variant.

No source describes the physical materials, print quantities, or how the badges were distributed to attendees — these pages exist only as design references published well after the event (the WordPress posts are dated March 2023). The entry is best understood as a design/artwork record of Ekoparty 2021's badge program rather than documentation of a manufactured item with its own specs.
