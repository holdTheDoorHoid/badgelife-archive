The Quadcopter Badge is a DEF CON 25 (2017) creation by the handle b1uN7, tied to the Whiskey Pirate crew: a wearable badge shaped like a Jolly Roger pirate skull that doubles as a small flying quadcopter with brushed motors and LED-lit arms. It pairs with a separate two-joystick controller board styled as a pirate's treasure map. Hackaday, covering the project a week before the con, reported a planned run of roughly 25 units and noted a light-only "bandana" variant for wearers who did not want the flying hardware.

Beyond that initial writeup, few details have surfaced: the article does not name the badge's MCU, LEDs, display, or battery, nor does it confirm a final price or whether the full run of quadcopters actually flew at closing ceremonies as planned. No design files, firmware repository, or maker storefront were found during this pass.
