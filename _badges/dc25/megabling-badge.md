Megabling is a one-off, oversized triangular badge that Blenster (Ben Hibben, of LVL1 Hackerspace) built and wore at DEF CON 25 in 2017. Hackaday's roundup of that year's hardware badges singled it out as an answer to the question "how bright can a conference badge really be?" — the badge's LED array is described as extremely bright, and the back of the board is dominated by power-regulation circuitry needed to run it from six 18650 lithium cells.

Unlike Blenster's other badge projects (the 2017 "Mr. Blinky Bling" WiFi scanning badge and the 2018 DEF CON 26 Indie Badge, both documented on his Hackaday.io profile with their own project pages), Megabling has no dedicated writeup, repository, or storefront that could be found — it appears to have been a personal show piece rather than something distributed to other attendees. No LED count, MCU, price, or quantity is known.

At the same con Blenster was also seen wearing a hand-soldered DerbyCon 2016 black badge of his own design, which Hackaday covered in the same section but which is a separate, unrelated item.
