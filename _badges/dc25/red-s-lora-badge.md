Built by the maker known as Red, who had previously made hardware badges for the SkyDogCon group, this badge was a one-off built specifically for DEF CON 25 in 2017. It sniffs LoRa packets and displays the results on two onboard screens, running on a Propeller chip with an ESP8266 and a LoRa module handling connectivity. Red reportedly wrote the firmware on the plane on the way to the conference.

The badge was described by its maker as a development platform first and foremost, with a mix of momentary-press and DIP switches left in place for further tinkering rather than a finished, polished product. No storefront, repository, or maker's own write-up has surfaced beyond the brief mention in Hackaday's DEF CON 25 hardware badge roundup, so details like price, quantity made, and availability remain unknown.
