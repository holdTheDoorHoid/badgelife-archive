"Ohm" is the name of Tindie's mascot dog, and this kit is the self-solder lapel-pin badge shaped like his head that Tindie handed out at DEF CON 25 in 2017. Brian Benchoff designed the PCB — his third badge project for that DEF CON — and Tindie's Jasmine Brackett and Shulie Tornel distributed the finished kits at the con. Each kit includes a custom PCB, two self-blinking LEDs standing in for the dog's eyes, and a coin cell and holder; there is no microcontroller, so the "blinking" comes from the LEDs themselves once soldered in.

This entry and `dc25-tindie-blinky-led-badge-v1` describe what appears to be the same physical badge, surfaced independently by two different research sweeps from two different Hackaday DEF CON 25 roundup articles. The sibling entry has richer, maker-sourced detail (price, production quantities, and design files pulled from Brian Benchoff's own Hackaday.io project page); this entry is kept separate per the archive's one-entry-per-task research rule, with a note pointing to the likely duplicate.
