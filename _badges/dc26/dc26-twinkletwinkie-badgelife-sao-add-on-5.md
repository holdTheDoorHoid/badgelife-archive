TwinkleTwinkie's Badgelife SAO Add-on #5 is a Shitty Add-on made for DEF CON 26 in 2018: a red, hat-shaped PCB with white LEDs that spell out "Make Add-ons Shitty Again" when the board is plugged into a compatible badge's SAO header (or powered directly at 3.3V). The maker notes using black hot glue behind the LEDs to stop light from bleeding out the sides of the letters, a common badgelife fabrication trick for legible lit text on a small board.

It was sold through TwinkleTwinkie's Tindie storefront alongside their other DEF CON 26 add-ons; the listing is now marked as no longer available. It also turned up bundled into a third-party "TwinkleTwinkie Badge Add-on Set" resale listing on eBay, though that listing could not be fetched directly to confirm its contents. No public information was found on the exact LED part, microcontroller (if any), production quantity, or original price.
