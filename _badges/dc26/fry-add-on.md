The Fry Add-On is a shitty add-on (SAO) by maker @sqearlsalazar, made for DEF CON 26 in 2018 during the early wave of badge-add-on creativity that Hackaday's badgelife coverage documented that year. The PCB is cut into the shape of Fry from Futurama in his "shut up and take my money" meme pose, printed in red and white to match the character, with a 2x2 SAO header visible on his shirt. A single LED is mounted in the wad of cash he's holding out, lighting it up when the add-on is powered from a host badge.

No dedicated project page, storefront listing, or repository for the Fry Add-On turned up in this pass, so its chip, price, quantity made, and current availability are unrecorded. The same Hackaday gallery credits @sqearlsalazar with a second, companion add-on from the same drop, a "Baby Bender" piece (also Futurama-themed) — that appears to be a distinct item from the archive's existing "Baby Bender Sqearlsalazar" entry, which is filed under DC30 rather than this 2018 wave, and is worth a look by whoever maintains that entry.
