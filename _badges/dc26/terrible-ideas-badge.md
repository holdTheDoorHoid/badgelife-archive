The Terrible Ideas Badge is an independently made electronic conference badge built around an ATmega328, with three capacitive touch pads on the board. Hackaday's write-up notes the pads "aren't yet operational," so as built the badge functioned mainly "for blinky enjoyment" rather than any interactive game. Roughly 200 units were made.

The badge's own name is a wink at the running joke among independent badge makers that taking on a small-run electronics project for a hacker con is, itself, a terrible idea — while also being exactly the kind of hands-on manufacturing challenge that draws people into the "badgelife" scene.

The badge was built for DEF CON 26 (2018) rather than DEF CON 27, which is the only con year documented for it; it surfaces in the historical record solely through Hackaday's later DC27 pictorial roundup, which included a photo of it alongside that year's crop of badges. No maker name, storefront, or source files have been found.
