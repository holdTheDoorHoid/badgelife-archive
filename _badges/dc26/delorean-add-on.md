A Back to the Future-themed DeLorean SAO shown off at DEF CON 26 (2018), designed by @reanimationxp (Drew Alden), @tr_h, and @ssldemon. It was displayed on one of the "add-on totems" used that year to show off add-ons that weren't plugged into a badge, and ran on roughly 3V supplied by its own pair of batteries rather than power drawn through a badge's SAO header.

In a comment on the Hackaday recap that photographed it, Drew Alden said the car's windows were meant to be plated in copper but the fab house botched that step, and mentioned the team might do a second production run if there was enough interest. No record of that run, a price, a quantity made, or any storefront/repo turned up in this pass — the Hackaday article and its comment thread are the only sources located.
