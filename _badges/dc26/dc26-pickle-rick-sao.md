Pickle Rick is one of several Rick and Morty-adjacent add-ons in awkward intelligence's "Harbinger" collection of Shitty Add-Ons made for DEF CON 26 (2018), a set that also included pieces styled after Nick Cage, Bob Ross, and the maker's earlier "Shitty Calvin" design. The maker's Hackaday.io project page for the collection lists a "pickle rick sao.zip" design file, uploaded in March 2019, described simply as "Pickle Rick Shitty add-on."

According to the project page, these add-ons were not sold individually: the maker gave them out as freebies to people who purchased sets of the other Shitty Add-Ons at the con. Beyond the existence of the design files, the page does not describe the Pickle Rick piece's specific electronics, LED count, or dimensions, and no photo clearly identifiable as this particular add-on (rather than another piece from the same collection) could be found.

## Make your own

Gerber and design files for the Pickle Rick add-on are published as a zip archive ("pickle rick sao.zip") on the project's Hackaday.io file list, alongside files for the collection's other add-ons. No firmware or bill-of-materials link was found specific to this piece.
