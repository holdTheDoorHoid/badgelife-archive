Mad Cat is a Shitty Add-On (SAO) shaped like a grinning Cheshire Cat, made by TwinkleTwinkie for DEF CON 26 in 2018. It has no microcontroller: white LEDs are reverse-mounted on the FR4 PCB so they shine through from behind, lighting up the cat's eyes and toothy grin when plugged into a host badge's SAO header.

The design is open on Hackaday.io, where TwinkleTwinkie posted the KiCad project files and Gerbers alongside the writeup shortly after DC26. The project page notes the SAO was also sold on Tindie, though the specific listing, price, and production quantity were not confirmed from available sources.
