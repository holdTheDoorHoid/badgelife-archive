"Darkwing Duck" appears on thelogicofpi's Hackaday.io project "SAO #Badgelife add-ons," a running list of shitty-add-on (SAO) ideas the maker was working through ahead of DEF CON 26 in 2018. It is one of several items marked "(in works)" on that page, alongside a Gizmo Duck SAO and others, with no dedicated writeup, schematic, bill of materials, or photo tied to it specifically.

No evidence was found that this design was completed, photographed, or distributed at DEF CON 26 or afterward. The project page has not been meaningfully updated in roughly eight years, which suggests the idea may have been abandoned before it left the concept stage, but no explicit cancellation notice exists either.
