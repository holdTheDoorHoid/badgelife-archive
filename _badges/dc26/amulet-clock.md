The Amulet Clock is a wearable necklace built around a vacuum fluorescent display (VFD) tube, shown off among the unofficial hardware at DEF CON 26 in 2018. Hackaday's own badge roundup calls it out explicitly as not really a badge — it carries no con theme, puzzle, or SAO header — but included it anyway for the novelty of running a VFD, and its accompanying -30V power supply, on something worn right against the neck.

Beyond that one paragraph and two photos (the necklace lit and worn, and a disassembled shot of the PCB stack and VFD tube), no further trace of the project surfaced: no maker name, no GitHub or Hackaday.io page, no chip, price, or quantity. Hackaday's write-up mentions the creator hoped to publish a detailed design article in POC||GTFO, but no such article could be located.
