TwinkleTwinkie's Badgelife SAO Add-on #6 is a Shitty Add-on made for DEF CON 26 in 2018, sold through the maker's Tindie storefront. It depicts a small character whose eyes and fuse light up in white when powered, running on the standard 3.3 V SAO supply. Units shipped assembled, with a separate 2x2 SAO header included unsoldered so the buyer could choose how to mount it — either via a breadboard or a "Shitty Add-on Totem."

The listing has since been marked as no longer available on Tindie, and a related item also circulated on eBay as part of a DEF CON 26 badge add-on set. Neither page states the LED type or count, the onboard chip (if any), the price charged, or how many units were made, so those details are left blank here pending further sources.
