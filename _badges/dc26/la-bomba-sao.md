La Bomba is TwinkleTwinkie's sixth Shitty Add-On, made for DEF CON 26 in 2018. It is a small cartoon-bomb-shaped SAO with white LEDs lighting up a pair of eyes and a fuse, powered directly off the 3.3 V SAO rail with no on-board microcontroller. The board is a 2x2 (SAO v1) design and shipped with an unsoldered header so buyers could solder it on themselves or test it on a breadboard.

TwinkleTwinkie sold assembled units through their Tindie shop; the listing has since been marked "Product Retired" and is no longer available. The maker published the KiCad schematic and Gerber files on the Hackaday.io project page, and Hackaday staff singled out the board's back-silkscreen artwork for praise.

Neither source states the LED part number/count, unit price, or production quantity, so those fields are left empty rather than guessed.
