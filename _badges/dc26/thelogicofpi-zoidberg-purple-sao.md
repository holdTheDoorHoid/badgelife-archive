"Zoidberg (Alternate Universe - Purple)" is a shitty-add-on (SAO) designed by hacker thelogicofpi as part of a larger personal project, "SAO #Badgelife add-ons," made for DEF CON 26 in 2018. The piece renders the Futurama character Dr. Zoidberg as a small PCB in purple, deliberately chosen to match OSH Park's signature purple solder mask — an "alternate universe" take alongside a gold alternate-universe Bender from the same order, playing on each board's stock PCB color as an in-universe costume swap.

The maker's July 2018 project log describes ordering the first batch of four such SAOs from OSH Park together: the purple Zoidberg, a gold Bender, a purple-haired Leela, and a Tubbs (Neko Atsume) board eating from a "golden bowl." An order-confirmation screenshot from that log shows the Zoidberg board (`zoidberg.kicad_pcb`) at 1.55 x 1.23 inch, a 2-layer board, produced in OSH Park's standard batch-of-three panelization.

No information was found on whether the board carries any active electronics (LEDs, an SAO header, a microcontroller) or is a purely decorative/passive PCB, nor on pricing, total quantity beyond the one visible order, or whether it was ever distributed to anyone besides the maker. It does not appear to have been sold commercially; treat it as a one-off hobby SAO documented on the maker's Hackaday.io project page.
