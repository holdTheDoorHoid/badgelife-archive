The Frida SAO is a Shitty Add-On made by lemons4all for DEF CON 26 in 2018. Rather than a purely electronic design, it leans into ornament: the board is decorated with five 10 mm clear lucite tulip flower beads, giving it a floral, jewelry-like look distinct from the LED-matrix SAOs common that year. The project page notes it was designed alongside a companion "RBG SAO" from the same maker for the same event.

The maker shared the complete board design as a KiCad project (schematic, PCB layout, custom footprint library, and a full gerber set) in a zip file on the project's Hackaday.io page, making it straightforward for someone else to fabricate their own copy. The footprint library includes a 3.0 mm flat-top LED footprint, suggesting the board carries at least one LED, though the exact LED count and behavior were not confirmed from the sources available.

Beyond the Hackaday.io project page, no pricing, production quantity, or distribution details for the Frida SAO were found, and the maker's own Hackaday.io profile could not be reached to check for further documentation or related badges.

## Make your own

Design files are open source, published as a KiCad project (`frida.kicad_sch`, `frida.kicad_pcb`), including production-ready gerbers, at https://cdn.hackaday.io/files/1615096910001056/frida_sao.zip.
