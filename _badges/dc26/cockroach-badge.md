The Cockroach Badge is an unofficial DEF CON 26 (2018) badge made by a hardware hacker known as Red (Twitter handle @noidd), covered in Hackaday's "All The Badges Of DEF CON 26 (vol 4)" roundup. It is a large PCB, at least 14 inches long, cut into the shape of a cockroach with spiny, articulated legs branching off the body.

To keep the delicate leg cutouts from snapping off, the maker ran a pair of long antennae parallel to the body on either side of the board, giving the fragile legs extra mechanical support. Hackaday's writer noted they never got a photo of the badge powered up and in action, only the bare, unpopulated board; the site's own intro blurb for the roundup describes the set of badges (this one included) as "filled with LEDs," but no LED count, type, microcontroller, price, or production quantity is given anywhere in the source, and no dedicated project page, repo, or storefront for it could be found.

Because the only confirmed source is a single third-party photo essay rather than the maker's own page, most technical and availability fields are left empty rather than guessed.
