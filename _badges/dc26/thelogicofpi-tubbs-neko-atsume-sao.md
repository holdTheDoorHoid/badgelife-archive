"Tubbs (nekoatsume)" is a small SAO (Simple Add-On) shaped like Tubbs, the perpetually-eating fat cat from the mobile game Neko Atsume, designed by Hackaday.io user thelogicofpi as part of a personal project called "SAO #Badgelife add-ons" ahead of DEF CON 26 in August 2018. The maker's July 31, 2018 project log describes ordering "the first 4 SAO from OSHPARK, which were especially suited for their purple and gold pcbs," alongside three other cartoon-themed SAOs from the same batch: an alternate-reality gold Bender, a purple Zoidberg, and a purple-haired Leela. Of the four, "tubbs eats out of a golden bowl" in the maker's own words — the board's silkscreen depicts the cat curled up next to a bowl shape, rendered in the gold-on-purple OSH Park finish.

The board itself measures 1.78 x 1.14 inches (45.3 x 28.8 mm), a 2-layer PCB designed in KiCad (file name tubbs.kicad_pcb) and ordered from OSH Park in a batch of three. The back carries "tubbs" and "Now Loading" silkscreen text along with SAO header pads. Unlike the Leela, Bender, and Zoidberg SAOs from the same order — which later appear lit up with LEDs in the maker's gallery photos — no photo of an assembled or illuminated Tubbs board turned up in the available sources, and the project log does not mention it having any electronics beyond the SAO header; it may have been a simple passive/silkscreen-only piece rather than a lit add-on.

No price, distribution details, storefront listing, or public design files (Gerbers/schematic) were found for this specific SAO; it reads as a small personal batch rather than a badge given away or sold at scale.
