The Why Not Zoid-Berg? Futur-o-rama SAO is a small purple add-on board made by PCB PINS (Tindie seller thelogicofpi) for DEF CON 26 in 2018. It depicts the alternate-universe, purple-skinned version of Dr. Zoidberg from the *Futurama* episode "The Farnsworth Parabox," with two onboard LEDs standing in for his eyes; they light up once the SAO is plugged into a powered host badge.

The board follows the DEF CON 26 SAO standard, running at 3.3V with its SDA/SCL pins left unconnected. It shipped with the LEDs and their two 137-ohm SMD resistors pre-soldered, but with two pairs of header pins left unsoldered to keep shipping costs and breakage down; the maker offered to assemble it fully for buyers without a soldering iron. It sold for $10 (or $8 each for two or more) through the maker's Tindie store, and by the time this entry was researched the listing had been out of stock since around May 2021, with the seller's page noting they had relocated and were working on restocking.
