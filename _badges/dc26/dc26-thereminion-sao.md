Thereminion is a minion-shaped shitty add-on (SAO) built by awkward intelligence for DEF CON 26 in 2018, part of a larger self-titled "Harbinger" line of SAOs the maker brought to that con. Its circuit is a modification of Forrest Mims' classic audible light meter design, turning the badge into a small light-controlled theremin: ambient light level changes the pitch of the sound it produces. The maker did not sell Thereminion on its own — it was given away as a bonus to people who bought one of the other add-ons in the same line.

The project's Hackaday.io page bundles Thereminion in with several other one-off SAOs from the same batch (Brrr, Shitty Calvin, Mr Robopoly, a Sound/Circuits Lab prototyping board, and a galaxia synthesizer), and gives it only a brief two-line description rather than its own writeup. Gerber files for the board ("Thereminion Gerbers", gerbersfixxxx.zip) are posted on the project's files page, dated July 25, 2018, so the PCB design is available even though no firmware or schematic-level writeup accompanies it.

## Make your own

Gerbers for the board are posted on the project's Hackaday.io files page as `gerbersfixxxx.zip` (https://hackaday.io/project/159952/files). No firmware, schematic, or bill of materials was found alongside them, and no EDA tool is stated.
