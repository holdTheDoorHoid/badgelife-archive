TwinkleTwinkie's Badgelife SAO Add-on #3 is a "Shitty Add-on" made for DEF CON 26 in 2018, sold through the maker's Tindie store. It depicts a big green alien whose eyes are wired to green LEDs, which glow when the board is powered over the standard 3.3v SAO connector — either plugged into a compatible badge or demoed on a breadboard or SAO totem.

The listing gives no open-source hardware or firmware links, no stated price or production quantity, and no chip (the board appears to be a passive LED add-on with no microcontroller). By the time it was checked in 2026 the Tindie listing showed the seller on a break and the product marked retired, so it is treated here as sold out rather than still available. A related eBay listing packaging TwinkleTwinkie DEF CON 26 badge add-ons together could not be re-fetched to confirm exactly what it included.
