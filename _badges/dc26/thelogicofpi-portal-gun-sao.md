A Rick and Morty-themed shitty add-on planned by hacker "thelogicofpi" for DEF CON 26 (2018): a portal gun paired with a portal-shaped background piece, "Portal Gun + Portal BG." It appears only as a single line in the to-build list of the maker's "SAO #Badgelife add-ons" Hackaday.io project, alongside other in-progress cartoon-themed pieces (Darkwing Duck, Gizmo Duck, Pickle-Rick + Portal BG, Wizard Hat, Megaseeds, Slippery Stair, Tiki, Electro-storm).

The same project documents four other SAOs — Leela, Bender, Zoidberg, and Tubbs — as completed, with dated photos from August 2018. Portal Gun + Portal BG has no such record: no photo, no project log entry, and no note marking it finished. Whether it was ever fabricated is unknown from available sources.
