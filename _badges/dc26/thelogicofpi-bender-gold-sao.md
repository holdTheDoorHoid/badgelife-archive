"Bender (w/sunglasses also GOLD)" is a Futurama-themed shitty add-on (SAO) by Hackaday.io user thelogicofpi, part of a small 2018 set of Simple Add-on boards built for that year's cons. The set paired an "alternate reality" color scheme across four completed designs: this gold-PCB Bender, a purple Zoidberg, a Leela with purple hair, and a Tubbs with a gold bowl. The four were ordered together from OSH Park on July 31, 2018, specifically because OSH Park offered purple and gold solder-mask finishes, which is how the "GOLD" variant of Bender came to exist alongside a more standard-colored one.

The maker's own photos show the board wired up on a breadboard and lit red through its sunglasses and mouth, but the project page does not document the microcontroller, LED part, power source, price, or how many were made — it reads as a build log rather than a sales listing, and there's no evidence it was ever sold or distributed beyond the maker's own con badges. No hardware files, firmware, or fab-share links are linked from the project.
