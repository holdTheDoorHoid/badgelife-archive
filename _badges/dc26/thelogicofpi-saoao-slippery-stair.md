"Slippery Stair SAOAO" is a planned SAO add-on named on thelogicofpi's Hackaday.io project "SAO #Badgelife add-ons," a personal to-do list of designs the maker intended to build for DEF CON 26 in 2018. It appears in a group of three "SAOAO" items alongside "Wizard Hat SAOAO" and "Megaseeds SAOAO" — separate from the same list's Disney (Darkwing Duck, Gizmo Duck) and Rick and Morty (Portal Gun, Pickle-Rick) items — but the page never explains what the "Slippery Stair" name refers to. Unlike some other entries on the same list, which are explicitly flagged "(in works)," this one carries no status note either way, and no photo, schematic, or later post documents whether it was completed or handed out.

Beyond the single line on that to-do list, no independent record of this item could be found — no dedicated project page, storefront listing, repository, or press coverage. It is documented here as a planned design of uncertain completion status rather than a confirmed, distributed badge.
