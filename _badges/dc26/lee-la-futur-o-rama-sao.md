The Lee-La Futur-o-rama SAO is a Standard Add-On badge shaped like Leela, the one-eyed captain from the animated series Futurama. Made by thelogicofpi, trading as PCB PINS out of Sacramento, California, it was originally created for DEF CON 26 (2018) and built to that year's SAO spec: 3.3V power delivered through the header, with two pre-soldered LEDs (driven through a pair of 137-ohm SMD resistors) that light up Leela's arm computer/gun and her single eye when plugged into a powered badge.

PCB PINS sold it on Tindie for $10 each, or $8.50 apiece for orders of two or more, with the header pins left unsoldered so buyers could choose their own. The listing had gone out of stock by December 1, 2019, and as of this check (September 2026) remains unavailable; the maker's listing notes they had moved and were still sorting out which products to bring back. The maker mentions design documentation on Hackaday.io, but that page was not located during this pass.
