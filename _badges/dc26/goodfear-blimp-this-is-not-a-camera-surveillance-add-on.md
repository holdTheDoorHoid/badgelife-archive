Chris Gammell built the GoodFear Blimp as a from-scratch DEF CON 26 badge shaped like the Goodyear Blimp and covered in LEDs, but ran out of time to finish its firmware before the con — the hardware was complete, but the badge itself was never distributed (see the separate entry for that badge, dc26-goodfear-blimp).

As a consolation project, Gammell put together a second, much simpler board: a small add-on shaped like a surveillance camera, riffing on the "This is Not a Camera" stickers that had circulated at DEF CON 25 the year before. Rather than functioning as a badge or SAO with documented electronics, it appears to have been a quick giveaway piece — Gammell spent DC26 handing the boards out in person. No pricing, production numbers, or technical details (MCU, LEDs, display) have surfaced in any source found so far.
