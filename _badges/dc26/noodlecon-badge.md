Sarah Petkus, a Hackaday alum best known for her Noodle robot project, made this badge for DEF CON 26 (2018) as the credential for "NoodleCon" — her own small con-within-the-con celebrating Noodle. The board is driven by an ATmega328 and runs off a CR2032 coin cell, with 10 LEDs on the rear producing a backlight effect through the front artwork.

The design leans heavily on PCB art: mirrored solder-mask lettering on the front and rear gives the board a "see-through" look, paired with bold silkscreen patches and gold-plated copper accents, including a small decorative squiggle worked into the "C" on the front.

No pricing, production quantity, availability, or public design files were found; both sources found (an OSH Park shared-projects post and the Hackaday DEF CON 26 badge roundup it reposts from) describe the badge but do not link a store, repo, or Gerbers.
