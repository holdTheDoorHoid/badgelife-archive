Nick Cage Shitty Add-on is a Nicolas Cage themed SAO by the maker awkward intelligence (Awkwardai), made as part of "The Harbinger," their collection of add-ons for DEF CON 26 in 2018 — by their own account, their first year designing electronic badges. The collection includes functional pieces (a 555-timer LED sequencer called Shitty Calvin, an SAO-header board called Mr Robopoly, an audio-reactive Thereminion) and at least one explicitly "static," non-electronic piece (Bob Ross). The project page does not say which category Nick Cage falls into.

The only trace of it is a design-file archive, nick cage soa.zip, added to the Hackaday.io project's files page in March 2019, alongside gerbers and files for the rest of the collection. No photo of the finished piece, and no mention of how many (if any) were fabricated or handed out, could be located.
