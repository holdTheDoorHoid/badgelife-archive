TwinkleTwinkie's Badgelife SAO Add-on #4 is a "Shitty Add-on" made for DEF CON 26 (2018) badges: a small PCB shaped like a purple cat whose white LED eyes light up when the add-on is plugged into a badge's SAO header and powered at 3.3v. The back of the board is coated in black hot glue, a common badgelife trick to keep the LED light from bleeding out the sides of the board instead of showing only through the eyes.

The add-on was sold through TwinkleTwinkie's Tindie store and shipped unsoldered, with a separate 2x2 SAO header included for the buyer to solder on themselves. As of this research pass the Tindie listing is marked "Product Retired," and the maker's store page notes they were taking a break from selling, so the item is no longer available new; a copy has since resurfaced as a secondary-market listing on eBay bundled with other TwinkleTwinkie DEF CON 26 add-ons.

No hardware or firmware files were confirmed as published for this specific add-on during this pass, so `make_your_own` is left empty rather than guessed.
