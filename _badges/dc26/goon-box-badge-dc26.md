The Goon Box Badge was an independent (non-official) badge made for DEF CON 26 in 2018, run under the Twitter handle @GoonBoxBadge, whose bio at the time read "An Indie badge for DEFCON 26." It took the form of a sonic screwdriver - a nod to Doctor Who - with a built-in TV-B-Gone circuit that lets the wearer switch off televisions in range, a classic badgelife prank feature. A recipient posted at DEF CON 26 that they had "already turned off my TV" with theirs, tagging #defcon26.

Beyond that, little can be confirmed: the maker's account has since disappeared from Twitter/X, and no storefront, price, quantity, schematic, or photo of the badge could be recovered. The one contemporary photo linked from a fan's tweet no longer resolves, on Twitter's CDN or in the Wayback Machine.
