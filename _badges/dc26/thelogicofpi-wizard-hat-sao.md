The Wizard Hat is one of several shitty-add-ons (SAOs) that hacker thelogicofpi listed as planned work in a Hackaday.io project titled "SAO #Badgelife add-ons," created July 26, 2018 ahead of DEF CON 26. The project's description reads "Many projects to build before defcon" and lists roughly a dozen planned pieces — Darkwing Duck, Gizmo Duck, a Portal Gun, a Pickle Rick add-on, a Tiki, an Electro-storm sign, and the Wizard Hat among them — several of which are explicitly marked "(in works)."

Wizard Hat carries no such marker, and it does not appear in the page's separate list of "Completed basic SAO" pieces (Leela, Bender, Zoidberg, and Tubbs), which the maker did finish and photograph. No image on the project page is captioned or otherwise identifiable as the Wizard Hat specifically, so it is unclear whether it ever moved beyond a planned concept. No chip, LED, pricing, or distribution details for it were found anywhere on the page or in further searching.
