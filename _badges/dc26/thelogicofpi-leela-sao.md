The Leela SAO is one of a set of Futurama-character shitty add-ons designed by hobbyist maker thelogicofpi for their "SAO #Badgelife add-ons" project. It depicts Leela, Futurama's one-eyed captain, holding her ray gun, with a description on the project page noting a light-up eye, gun, and armband. The maker had the design fabricated in purple on OSH Park's PCB service specifically because "Leela's hair is purple," as part of a first batch of four SAO designs (alongside an alternate-universe gold Bender, an alternate-universe purple Zoidberg, and a Tubbs the cat SAO) ordered on July 31, 2018, ahead of DEF CON 26.

A project photo shows a panel of finished purple Leela SAO boards next to the matching Zoidberg design, confirming the boards were fully fabricated and assembled rather than remaining a concept. Beyond that, the maker's pages do not give further build detail (microcontroller, LED type, SAO connector version) or say how many were made, what they cost, or whether any were sold, given away, or traded — this appears to have been a small personal/gift-style badgelife run typical of the era rather than a commercial product.
