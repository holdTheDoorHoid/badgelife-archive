The Future-O-Rama Golden Bend-her Robot SAO is a Simple Add-On board from PCB PINS (Tindie seller thelogicofpi, based in Sacramento, CA) shaped after an alternate-universe, gold-finished take on Bender Bending Rodriguez from Futurama. The maker's listing states it was originally created for DEFCON 26 (2018). It plugs into a badge's standard SAO header (3.3V and GND) and lights two pre-soldered LEDs, driven through a pair of 137-ohm SMD resistors, to give the robot's mouth a glow. The board ships with two pairs of header pins left unsoldered to keep shipping costs down, though the seller notes they can pre-solder them on request for buyers without soldering equipment.

It was sold on Tindie for $10 each (or $8 apiece for orders of two or more) and was still listed there as of this research, marked out of stock since June 14, 2024. The listing references a Hackaday.io project page for further documentation, but that page could not be located during this research pass, so no hardware or firmware files are recorded here.
