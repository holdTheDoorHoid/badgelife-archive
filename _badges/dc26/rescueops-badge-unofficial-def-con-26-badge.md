The #RescueOps Badge was an unofficial DEF CON 26 (2018) badge concept from a small team led by a Hackaday.io user going by Kirball, with nopestradamus, adamcwhooper, and Remo also credited. The idea traced back to DEF CON 25, after the team had to help a drunk friend find his way back to his hotel room late one night -- the badge was conceived as a way to prevent that: a wearable with Bluetooth low-energy connectivity for messaging other badges or texting friends, plus an LCD screen showing a map of the Las Vegas venue for navigation.

Development seems to have stopped early. The project page's only logged progress is an order for Texas Instruments CC2640 BLE sample chips in August 2017, and the team's plan to launch a Kickstarter to fund the badge appears never to have happened. No photos of an assembled badge, no pricing, and no unit count are documented anywhere the project can be found, and the Hackaday.io page has not been updated since. It is treated here as a shelved/cancelled project rather than one that was actually built or distributed at DEF CON 26.
