Nibbler is a Shitty Add-On (SAO) made by TwinkleTwinkie, handed out in a small, unspecified batch at DEF CON 26 in 2018. The maker's own project page gives almost no technical detail beyond the name and a single photo of the board; no chip, LED, display, price, or firmware information is published.

Shortly after the con, TwinkleTwinkie released the design as open hardware: a KiCad project with Gerber files is downloadable directly from the Hackaday.io project page (uploaded September 10, 2018), so that others who missed out on the original handout could have their own boards fabricated.

## Make your own

Download the KiCad/Gerber package from the project's file list on https://hackaday.io/project/161117-nibbler and send the Gerbers to a PCB fab of your choice (e.g. PCBWay or OSH Park) to have your own Nibbler SAO made. No bill of materials or firmware is published alongside the files.
