The DEF CON 26 Shitty Add-On Totem is a bare breakout PCB by the maker known as Benchoff, shared on OSH Park on February 22, 2018. It is a long, narrow (0.61 x 8.16 in) two-layer board carrying four Shitty Add-On (SAO) headers, letting someone plug in and display up to four SAO modules side by side without needing a host badge to power them. It functions purely as a mechanical and electrical extender rather than an active device — there is no onboard microcontroller, LEDs, or display.

The board was made available as an OSH Park shared project, where it can still be ordered directly (three copies for $24.75 as of this check). No separate maker write-up, Hackaday.io post, or press coverage of the Totem was located; the OSH Park listing itself, including its board files, is the only source found.
