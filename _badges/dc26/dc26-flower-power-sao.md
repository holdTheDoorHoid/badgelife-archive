The Flower Power SAO is one of several 4-pin add-on designs awkward intelligence grouped under "The Harbinger Shitty Add-on Badges," a set of SAOs made for DEF CON 26 (2018). Unlike the collection's other named pieces — a Gucci Mane-inspired dual-LED flasher, "Shitty Calvin," "Mr Robopoly," "Thereminion," and "The Galaxia" — Flower Power is not described in the project's own write-up at all. It surfaces only as a file, `flowerpower.zip`, uploaded to the project's file list on March 4, 2019, carrying the one-line description "Flower Power shitty add-on."

Because the design files were shared roughly six months after DEF CON 26 itself, it is unclear whether Flower Power was actually built and handed out at the con or added later purely as a shareable design alongside the maker's other pop-culture SAOs (Pickle Rick, Bob Ross, Nick Cage) uploaded in the same batch. No pricing, quantity, chip, or LED information is available from any source found.
