Gigs (gigstaggart) built this name tag for DEF CON 26 (2018) on a whim about a week before the con. It is a hand-wired protoboard badge rather than a designed PCB: 67 LEDs wired in parallel, with no current-limiting resistors, driven straight from four batteries and smoothed by two supercapacitors. Gigs's own reasoning, as quoted by Hackaday, was that it only had to survive one weekend.

The badge was covered in Hackaday's "All the Badges of DEF CON 26 (vol 2)" roundup, which is also the only source found for it — no maker storefront, repository, or social post has turned up to confirm price, quantity made, or whether it is still around. Nothing suggests it was ever sold or distributed beyond being worn by its maker at the con.
