Mr. Blinky Bling was an indie badge proposed for DEF CON 26 (2018) by Ben Hibben, who goes by Blenster on Hackaday.io, working with a collaborator credited as "The Hat." Rather than a typical blinky or CTF badge, it built its identity around a social game: wearers who met in person could trade "White Blocks," and each trade triggered a "Proof of Work" animation on the receiving badge, in a nod to blockchain mining. The same block could not be traded back to farm score, so the game rewarded meeting new people rather than repeatedly swapping with the same person. The project also described rarer, non-swappable colored blocks with special effects, and the badge was built to support the standard DEF CON 26 Shitty Add-On (SAO) header.

The Hackaday.io project page is the only source that could be located for this badge. It does not include a Files or Components section, so no microcontroller, LED, display, or battery details are documented, and there is no confirmed price, production quantity, or availability status. The page indicates the makers intended to fund production through a Kickstarter campaign, but does not link it or state whether it was run or successful, so it is unclear how many (if any) physical badges were ultimately produced or distributed.
