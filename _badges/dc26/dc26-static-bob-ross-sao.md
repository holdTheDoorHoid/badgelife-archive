"Static Bob Ross Add-on" is one of several shitty add-ons (SAOs) that Atlanta-based maker awkward intelligence grouped under "The Harbinger Shitty Add-on Badges," a set of small DIY badge accessories made for DEF CON 26 in 2018. The collection otherwise leans on Atlanta in-jokes (a Gucci Mane face-tattoo lightning flasher, a "Shitty Calvin" LED sequencer) and simple 555-timer or transistor-driven blinky circuits, but the maker's own project write-up never actually describes the Bob Ross piece.

The only trace of it is a design-file entry, bobross.zip, added to the project's Files page on March 4, 2019 — months after DC26 — labeled simply "Static Bob Ross Add-on." Nothing else about its chip, LEDs, artwork, or how (or whether) it was ever distributed is documented publicly; the "static" in its name implies it has no animation, unlike some of the maker's other add-ons.
