This entry documents a Hackaday.io post by Peter Giancola, not a distinct badge or SAO. In it, Giancola describes soldering a modification onto his own official DEF CON 26 (2018) attendee badge to change all of its LEDs to blue, an aesthetic tweak he made "for fun" and admits he can't fully explain the motivation for. The write-up is mostly a story about the difficulty of finding solder in Las Vegas mid-conference (a nearby Fry's Electronics was sold out, and he eventually sourced supplies from a Lowe's) rather than a build log with reproducible details.

There is no schematic, firmware, bill of materials, or indication that this modification was ever offered to anyone else — it was a single attendee's personal hack on the badge every DC26 attendee already received, not a badge or SAO that was designed, produced, or distributed as its own product. As such it doesn't fit this archive's definition of a badge/SAO entry.
