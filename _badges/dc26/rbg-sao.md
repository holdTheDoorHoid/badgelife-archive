The RBG SAO is a Shitty Add-On (SAO) badge accessory made by PCB artist lemons4all for DEF CON 26 (2018). It was published to Hackaday.io on September 28, 2018, alongside a design-files archive (`RBG_sao.zip`) containing the board layout; the maker's project notes mention that additional artwork files were still to be added, though it isn't clear whether that ever happened. As with lemons4all's other work, the project emphasizes PCB art over documented functionality — the listing does not specify an LED chip, count, colors, or physical shape, so those details are left blank here rather than guessed.

No storefront listing, price, quantity, or distribution details were found for this SAO; it appears to have been shared primarily as a design/build-your-own project rather than sold or distributed as a finished item. lemons4all's Hackaday.io profile lists two sibling DEF CON 26 SAO projects — an "LSP SAO" and a "Frida SAO" — that were not otherwise covered by this entry.

## Make your own

The maker's Hackaday.io project page hosts a downloadable design-files archive, `RBG_sao.zip` (~207 KB), linked directly from the project. No separate firmware repository or bill of materials was found; anyone wanting to build one would need to work from the contents of that archive.
