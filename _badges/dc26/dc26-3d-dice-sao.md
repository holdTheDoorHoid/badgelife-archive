The 3D Dice is one of several Shitty Add-Ons in awkward intelligence's "Harbinger" collection, made for DEF CON 26 (2018) and documented on the maker's Hackaday.io project page. Unlike the collection's other pieces - a Minion, an ice-cream cone, a Monopoly-man Bitcoin design, a Calvin-and-Hobbes figure, and the Galaxia synth/prototyping board - the dice is not described in the project's write-up at all. It survives only as three separate design-file archives labeled for a top, a set of sides, and a bottom, implying it was built as a small stack of PCBs soldered together into a six-sided die rather than a single flat board.

The maker states that the Harbinger-era add-ons were not sold individually, only handed out as part of complete Shitty Add-On sets, which is presumed to apply to the dice as well though it is not called out by name. No image of the assembled piece, no chip or LED information, and no pricing or quantity details were found in any source checked.
