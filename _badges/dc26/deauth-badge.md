The DEAUTH Badge is a non-electronic wearable made by El Kentaro for DEF CON 26 (2018). It started as a 3D-printed piece that was sanded smooth and finished with several coats of gold spray paint, then covered in large jewels and hundreds of tiny glass beads packed into the gaps between them, giving it a bejeweled, almost jewelry-like look rather than the blinky-PCB style typical of the badgelife scene.

Coverage of the badge is limited to a single line in Hackaday's "All the Badges of DEF CON 26" roundup, which credits El Kentaro and notes that the badge's polished, reflective surface made it difficult to photograph in focus. No information on price, quantity made, or how it was distributed has surfaced; it is treated here as one of the many one-off indie badges shown off at DC26 rather than a mass-produced release.
