The DEF CON 26 Stamp Badge is a free, open-source badge design by elkentaro, made for DEF CON 26 (2018) and shared for the #badgelife community to print and wear at "hacker summer camp." Unlike most entries in this archive, it isn't an electronic badge — it's a 3D-printable part released under a Creative Commons Attribution license, with both ready-to-print mesh files and the native Fusion 360 source file made available.

No storefront ever sold physical units; anyone with a 3D printer could produce their own, which is reflected here as a free give-it-away/build-it-yourself release rather than a purchase or limited drop. The maker's own description is brief, simply inviting people to print it and enjoy the badgelife scene at the con.

## Make your own

The design is open source. Grab the printable files from the Thingiverse listing, or the native Fusion 360 project at https://a360.co/2L38FOG if you want to edit the source model before printing.
