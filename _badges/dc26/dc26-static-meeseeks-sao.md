A static (non-animated) Mr. Meeseeks shitty add-on, part of "The Harbinger Shitty Add-on Badges" — a
collection of small SAO designs that Georgia-based maker awkward intelligence put together for DEF CON
26 in 2018. The Meeseeks piece is a one-off tribute to the character from *Rick and Morty*, distinct
from the maker's other designs in the same collection (several of which, like Shitty Calvin, do have
LED animation — hence "static" in this one's name).

Almost nothing beyond its existence and design files could be confirmed: the Meeseeks SAO shows up only
as a file entry (`shittymeseeks.zip`, uploaded March 2019) in the project's Files tab, with no dedicated
log post, photo, price, or production count anywhere on the project page or the maker's profile. It is
unclear whether it was ever populated as physical boards and given out at DEF CON, or only ever existed
as design files shared after the fact.

## Make your own

The zip file `shittymeseeks.zip` (253 KB) is available from the project's files list at
https://hackaday.io/project/159952/files and should contain the board files for the design, but its
contents were not individually inspected during this research pass.
