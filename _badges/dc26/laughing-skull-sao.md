TwinkleTwinkie's Laughing Skull is a 2018 Shitty Add-On (SAO) depicting the grinning skull mascot of The Vortex, a bar and burger restaurant in Atlanta, GA that the maker described as a favorite spot to eat and take tourists. TwinkleTwinkie posted the project to Hackaday.io in September 2018 as part of that year's Shitty Add-Ons collection, associated with DEF CON 26.

Only a small number were produced ("I only produced a few of these," per the maker), and it does not appear to have had a public storefront listing, so it is treated here as sold out. When powered, the skull's oversized eyes glow bright red, though the underlying LED part, count, and driving MCU were not documented on the project page and could not be confirmed elsewhere.
