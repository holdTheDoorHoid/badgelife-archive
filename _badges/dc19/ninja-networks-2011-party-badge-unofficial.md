The Ninja Networks DEF CON party was an invite-only gathering that ran alongside DEF CON from 2009 onward, and in its early years (DC17, DC18) came with its own limited-run electronic badge. A DEF CON forum thread titled "*UNOFFICIAL* The 2011 Ninja Badge / Party" was set up for that year's edition, which is presumably where this archive entry originated.

However, a separate forum post from the organizer, "Ninja Badge / Party 2011 - CANCELED," explains that the 2011 event never happened: the organizer had spent the month before DEF CON 19 hospitalized, which made planning and building the traditional party and its badge impossible, and the whole thing was called off. Ninja Networks' own badge-history page lists pages for DC17, DC18 (2010), and DC20/NinjaTel, but has no DC19 entry, consistent with no badge having been produced that year.

No hardware details (chip, LEDs, display, price, or quantity) exist to record here because, as far as the available sources show, no badge was ever made for DEF CON 19.
