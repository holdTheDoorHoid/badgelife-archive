The DerbyCon 2016 Black Badge was the black badge — the conference's top prize, traditionally granting the holder free admission to future events — for DerbyCon 6.0 "Recharge," held in Louisville, Kentucky. It was designed and built by Blenster (Ben Hibben), who runs the badge shop Mr Blinky Bling. The badge is packed with a dense array of individually animated LEDs at a pitch tight enough that Hibben placed and soldered each one by hand with a soldering iron, a detail Hackaday's DEF CON 25 hardware-badge roundup singled out as "incredible."

Hibben went on to build a follow-up black badge for DerbyCon 7.0 "Legacy" in 2017, and his own write-up of that later badge describes the 2016 badge as having had over 170 animated LEDs and capacitive touch — though that page could not be fetched directly for this pass (it returned a Cloudflare challenge), so those specifics are reported here with lower confidence and are not filled into the structured tech fields.
