Hackfest, the Quebec City hacker conference, introduced an electronic badge for its Capture the Flag competition in 2019, held at the Plaza Hotel edition of the event. Hackfest's own event history describes the addition as "an immediate hit with participants," and the 2019 CTF recap lists "Hardware Badge" among that year's challenge categories, alongside SDR, Kubernetes, and web-hacking tracks.

Beyond confirming that the badge existed and was used as a CTF challenge, no further documentation has surfaced: no schematic, PCB files, bill of materials, firmware repository, or maker-team build notes have been publicly recovered, and no photograph of the badge itself could be located. The badge's specific chip, LED count, display, power source, and production quantity therefore remain unknown.
