This kit is a learn-to-solder badge from Hak4Kidz Lab, the electronics-education arm of the Hak4Kidz youth hacking conference. Rather than a microcontroller, the board uses two RGB LEDs whose color is mixed by hand with three potentiometers, so the build teaches basic through-hole soldering (resistors, a switch, a CR2032 battery holder) without requiring any firmware. It also carries an SAO connector, letting it plug into a host con badge once assembled. A QR code silkscreened on the board points builders to Hak4Kidz's own written assembly instructions.

A YouTube video from a Blue Team Con 2022 attendee shows this specific kit being soldered together at that event's Hak4Kidz village, which is why this entry is tied to Blue Team Con 2022. The kit itself, however, appears to be a general Hak4Kidz Lab product (sold on Tindie for $20) rather than a badge produced uniquely for that con — the archive holds a separate entry for the same "Learn to Solder Badge" line at DEF CON 31, and the maker describes it as restarting in-person Hak4Kidz builds broadly rather than tying it to one show.

No hardware or firmware files were found published for this kit, and its current availability, unit count, and exact SAO header revision could not be confirmed from the sources checked.
