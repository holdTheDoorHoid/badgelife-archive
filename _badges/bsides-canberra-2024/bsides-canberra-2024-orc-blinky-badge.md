BSides Canberra's Hardware Village has run every year of the conference, giving attendees a soldering kit to build on-site with volunteer help. For 2024 the kit was an orc-shaped PCB badge wired as a simple 555-timer astable ("blinky") circuit: a 555 timer chip, LEDs, a diode, resistor and capacitor, powered by a 9V battery, with no microcontroller, display, or wireless features. It was pitched as simple enough for soldering beginners.

The badge is documented mainly through an attendee's personal build writeup (kawing-ho), who soldered one at the con with help from Hardware Village volunteers, briefly shorted an LED, and swapped it for a working one. No official BSides Canberra product page, price, production quantity, or published design files were found, so those fields are left empty rather than guessed.
