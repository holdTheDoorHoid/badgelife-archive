Jacob (Hackaday.io handle Jaycop) designed this parametric plastic enclosure for the 2019 Hackaday Superconference badge, a Game-Boy-shaped FPGA badge. Modeled in Fusion 360 from the badge's own PCB dimensions, the case exposes cutouts for all the board's ports and lets a user adjust button sizes, corner fillets, port-to-cable tolerances, and overall case thickness before printing. It is one of at least two independent enclosure projects made for that year's badge.

As of the project's last documented update in late November 2019, the design was still a single solid shell without a power switch or cartridge slot; the maker's notes list a split case, a power switch, SAO connector cutouts, and light pipes for the badge's LEDs as planned but not-yet-completed additions.

## Make your own

The Fusion 360 source file (`HAD2019_gameboy.f3d`) is published both directly on the Hackaday.io project and in a linked GitLab repository, so anyone with the 2019 Supercon badge can download and 3D print their own copy, adjusting the parametric dimensions to their printer and preferences.
