The Wireshark 0x4e00 is a small pin badge made by TwinkleTwinkie and Nick Pisarro for Hackaday Supercon 2019, shaped like a cartoon shark curled inside a spool of wire. It runs on an ATtiny13A microcontroller and a CR2032 coin cell, and its one trick is playing the "Baby Shark" tune through a 12mm piezo buzzer — a gag badge built around one of the smallest, cheapest AVR parts Microchip makes.

The main production run was sponsored by JLCPCB, with three extra boards fabricated through OSH Park's shared-projects service, where the design (a 4-layer, 3.94 x 2.05 inch board) remained available for others to order. The makers published the full schematic, Gerbers, and firmware (with a Windows flashing script) on the project's Hackaday.io page, making it fully open source and reproducible.

No price, exact production quantity, or LED count is documented anywhere in the sources found; the badge appears to have been a free giveaway/gag item at Supercon rather than a sold product.
