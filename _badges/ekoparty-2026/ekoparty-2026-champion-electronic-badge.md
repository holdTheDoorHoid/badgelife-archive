Ekoparty, Latin America's largest hacking and cybersecurity conference, is bundling a limited-edition electronic badge with its Champion-tier ticket for its 2026 edition (October 7-9, Buenos Aires). This continues a run of electronic badges the conference has produced in recent years (2023 and 2024 with Electronic Cats, 2025 with Lucas Leal, Jorge Crowe, and Fábrica Marciana), but as of this writing the 2026 badge itself has not been shown: no chip, LED, display, or enclosure details have been made public.

What is confirmed is that the badge is exclusive to the Champion ticket (AR$265,000, rising to AR$280,000 in mid-September 2026) and is not included with the cheaper Legacy ticket (AR$85,000), nor sold separately. A September 1, 2026 Instagram post from the conference's own account teases it as "el EKO Badge 2026," promising it will be "not just a credential" -- language that echoes past years' badges being interactive electronics rather than plain lanyard cards -- but stops short of naming a maker team or any specs.

This entry will need another pass once the conference runs and the badge is actually revealed or photographed.
