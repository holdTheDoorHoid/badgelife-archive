The White Dragon Noodle Bar badge is an independent, unofficial badge from DEF CON 27 (2019), named for the neon-lit noodle stall from *Blade Runner*. Its maker built a 3D-printed faceplate with internal baffles filled with hot glue to diffuse the badge's LEDs, and paired that with a white silkscreen layer on the PCB that reflects light back out through the faceplate — together giving the badge the soft, glowing neon-sign look the film's set dressing is known for.

Twenty of these badges were produced. Hackaday's 2019 pictorial guide to DC27's unofficial badges is the only substantial source found on this badge; it credits no individual or team by name, and no separate maker page, storefront listing, or hardware repository for it turned up in searches. The chip used, LED count and type, and how the badges were distributed at the con remain unknown.
