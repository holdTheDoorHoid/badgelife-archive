The Hack For Satan badge is an independent, unofficial badge made by the "Hack For
Satan" team for DEF CON 27 (2019), part of a recurring series the team has produced
across several DEF CONs (a DC25 hardware repo, "devilbadge," is public on the
team's GitHub). It is coin-cell powered and includes a Rigado Bluetooth
module that lets badges interact with one another; Hackaday's roundup of DC27
badges noted attendees clustering the badges in groups of five to trigger
interactive effects.

Beyond that, little public detail survives about the DC27 edition specifically.
The team's own site teased the badge ("A new demon will arrive at DEF CON 27")
but does not describe its hardware, and no DC27-specific repository turned up
under the team's GitHub org, so chip, LED count, display, price, and production
quantity are unknown. A resale listing on eBay refers to this badge as "Hack 4
Satan 3 Virus Badge," suggesting it was the third badge the team released, but
that naming has not been confirmed on a primary source.
