The Popcorn Bucket Badge is a DEF CON 27 (2019) creation by Kredence and Ajax_409: a working plastic popcorn box, cut down in size, fitted with backlit "kernels." Nested among the kernels are twelve add-on headers, letting other attendees plug their own SAOs into the badge -- though the makers themselves noted on Hackaday's DC27 round-up that it "looks better without the extras in place."

Kredence separately released a "Popcorn SAO" (github.com/Kredence/DC27_Popcorn_SAO) the same year, which appears to be a distinct, smaller stand-alone add-on rather than this badge (see notes). No pricing, production quantity, hardware files, or component specifications for the Popcorn Bucket Badge itself were found; the maker's GitHub repo for it holds only a single photo and no documentation.
