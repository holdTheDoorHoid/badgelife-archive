The Dumpster Fire SAO is a small "Shitty Add-On" made by Harbinger LTD (Andrew Nicholson, trading as Awkward AI) for DEF CON 27 in 2019. According to the maker's own Hackaday.io writeup, it started as a joke idea made after finishing their TV3Y3 badge project, and turned out to be an awkward size to execute as a SAO. Rather than use a dedicated flame-flicker driver chip, the maker lit three LEDs and diffused the light through hand-applied hot glue to approximate a flickering flame — a cheap, low-parts-count way to sell the "dumpster fire" joke about a troubled software project.

The board itself has a matte green solder mask with white silkscreen and an exposed gold-immersion finish, and includes an on/off switch for the LEDs. It was sold fully assembled on Tindie for $15; as of the last check the maker's shop lists it as temporarily unavailable with no orders being taken. No schematic, firmware, or hardware source files could be found, and the listing does not state how many units were produced.

Note that a separate, unrelated SAO also named "Dumpster Fire" exists from a different maker (Untitled Electronics) — the two should not be confused.
