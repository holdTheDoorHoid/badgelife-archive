The New Jack Badge is technick's first entry in what became a recurring "Jack" badge series, sold ahead of DEF CON 27 in 2019. It is a 3D-printed, non-electronic badge depicting Jack with a piercing that doubles as a lanyard attachment point, spiked hair, and a stated "add-on system to upgrade him in the future." Technick described it as the character's first redesign, meant to reflect that "infosec is everybody's responsibility."

It was sold through Tindie in two forms: a standard printed version and a custom printed version that let buyers add a name or alias (up to 10 characters), each priced at $20 and offered in several color/print options (including glow-in-the-dark). Both were pickup-only, distributed at BSidesLV, DEF CON 27, and DerbyCon in 2019; both listings are now marked sold out. The badge has no LEDs, MCU, or SAO header — it is a physical keepsake rather than an electronic badge, distinguishing it from most other entries in technick's later, more elaborate Jack badges.
