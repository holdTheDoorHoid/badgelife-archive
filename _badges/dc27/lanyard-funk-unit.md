The Lanyard Funk Unit is a homemade wearable made by Robert Ballecer, a personality on the This Week in Tech (TWiT) network, for DEF CON 27 in 2019. Rather than plugging into another badge as an SAO, it stands on its own as a lanyard accessory: an Arduino Nano drives a ring of 24 RGB LEDs, and the whole assembly sits behind a 3D-printed diffuser that spreads the light into a soft glow rather than showing individual pixels.

Ballecer brought the badge to Hackaday's DEF CON 27 breakfast meetup, where it was photographed both worn and opened up, showing the Nano and LED ring wiring inside. He made ten of these in total. No project page, firmware repository, or storefront listing has been found, so it isn't clear whether the design was ever shared, what (if anything) it cost, or how the ten units were distributed beyond being worn and shown off at the con.
