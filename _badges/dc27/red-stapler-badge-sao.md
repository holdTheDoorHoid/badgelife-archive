The Red Stapler Badge SAO is a novelty add-on made by TeamID64F (maker handle s3gfault) for DEF CON 27 in 2019. It is shaped like the red Swingline stapler from the film *Office Space*, one of a run of pop-culture-themed SAOs the same maker sold that year (a Punisher badge SAO and a Windows Logo badge SAO among them). It has no microcontroller or LEDs — it is a plain PCB novelty piece with a keychain hole, sold through the maker's Tindie storefront.

The listing calls out a solder-bridge pad as a running joke: bridging it is described as recreating the film's "burn the building down" gag rather than adding any real function. The Tindie listing is now marked retired, with no price or production-quantity figures published, and no hardware or firmware files were found for it.
