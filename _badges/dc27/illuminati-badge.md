Kredence -- known for the annual "Illuminati Party" badge line -- brought a new version of their signature eye-themed independent badge to DEF CON 27 in 2019. It follows directly from their DC26 "All Seeing Eye" badge, described by Kredence's own DC27 repository as the "main badge," larger than the previous year's and built in a triangle shape rather than the earlier design's form. The eye is lit from behind, with an offset header and 3D-printed light baffles used to keep the glow centered in the eye rather than leaking out at the board's edges, a detail Hackaday's DC27 badge roundup called out specifically.

Beyond that description and a single photo of the finished piece, little is publicly documented. Kredence's GitHub repository for the badge contains only that image, with no schematic, firmware, bill of materials, or Gerbers, and Hackaday's writeup notes that photos of it lit up weren't available at the time, suggesting the badge "just wasn't quite ready in time for DC27." Pricing, production quantity, and how it was distributed were not found in any source checked.
