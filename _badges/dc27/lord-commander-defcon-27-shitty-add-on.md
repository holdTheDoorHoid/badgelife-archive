Jeremy Ward designed the Lord Commander SAO as his second shitty add-on for DEF CON 27 (2019), following an earlier Mooncake SAO. The board depicts Lord Commander, a character from the Conan O'Brien-produced animated series *Final Space*, as a 2-layer, 100 x 94 mm PCB in purple solder mask with white silkscreen.

The design files — schematic and Gerbers — are published on GitHub under a CC BY-SA 3.0 license, and the board is also listed as a shared project on PCBWay, where the manufacturer donates a share of build costs back to the designer. Nothing in the repository or the PCBWay listing documents active components, a bill of materials, or firmware, so it reads as a decorative/passive add-on rather than one with lights or a microcontroller; no pricing, quantity, or distribution details were found.

## Make your own

Gerber files and the KiCad-style schematic/layer files are available in the [GitHub repo](https://github.com/cybr1d-cybr1d/Lord-Commander-SAO); the same files can be ordered directly through the [PCBWay shared project page](https://www.pcbway.com/project/shareproject/Lord_Commander_DEFCON_27_Shitty_Add_On.html).
