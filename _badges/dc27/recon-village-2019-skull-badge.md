Recon Village, the OSINT and reconnaissance community at DEF CON, made The Skull Badge for DEF CON 27 in Las Vegas in August 2019. It carries the village's "Steampunk Recon" theme that year: a skull-shaped PCB rendered with mechanical, gear-and-rivet artwork in a metallic blue-gray finish, with compass and coordinate markings worked into the design and a single glowing LED standing in for one eye. A separate "CREW" variant marked out Recon Village staff from the general-attendee badge.

Beyond that description on Recon Village's own retrospective page, little technical detail about the badge has surfaced publicly. No source found gives a microcontroller, battery type, LED part number, price, or production quantity, and no schematic, firmware, or PCB files have been published for it — a gap a third-party badge catalogue (badge.gallery) also notes when covering the same piece.
