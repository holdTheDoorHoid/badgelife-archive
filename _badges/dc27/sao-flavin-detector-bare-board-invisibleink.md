The Flavin Detector is a bare-board Shitty Add-On (SAO) made by Harbinger LTD (Andrew Nicholson) for DEF CON 27 in 2019, sold through the maker's awkwardai storefront on Tindie. Rather than blinking lights for show, it puts a bank of 390nm UV LEDs to a practical (if crude) forensic use: fluorescing substances like saliva, blood, semen, bacteria, and urine that don't show up under an ordinary blacklight. The listing described it as able to spot these from a couple of feet away in the dark, with an optional switch to control the UV emission.

It shipped unassembled, as bare boards with a UV3TZ/UV5TZ LED set, a small switch, and 0805 resistors for the buyer to solder themselves, along with an SAO header and a vial of invisible ink. Two editions were offered: a blue "Whoopsie Edition" with just the UV LEDs, and a purple Regular Edition that added green Kingbright side-view LEDs. The Tindie listing is now marked retired, and no dedicated Hackaday.io write-up specific to this SAO was located, only the maker's broader page cataloguing their earlier Harbinger SAO line from DEF CON 26.
