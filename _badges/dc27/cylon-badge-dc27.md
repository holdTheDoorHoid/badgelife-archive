The Cylon Badge is an unofficial electronic badge made by TeamBazooka for DEF CON 27 (2019). It reproduces the scanning red-eye effect of the Cylon Centurions from Battlestar Galactica, driving 48 LEDs in a back-and-forth sweep from an STM32 microcontroller. It was documented in Hackaday's pictorial roundup of DEF CON 27's unofficial badges.

Only three of these badges were ever made, making it one of the rarest entries in that year's unofficial badge scene. No maker storefront, source repository, or additional photos beyond the single Hackaday image have been located; distribution details (price, how the three units were given out or sold) are unknown.
