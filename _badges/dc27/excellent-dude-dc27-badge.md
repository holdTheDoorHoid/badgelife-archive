The Excellent Dude DC27 badge is a hand-made wooden badge built by Hackaday.io user jdwaller and worn at DEF CON 27 in 2019. The maker describes it as a simple, low-tech project meant to put a year's worth of newly learned woodworking and basic soldering skills to use, along with on-site modifications made during the conference itself.

No electronics specifications, pricing, or production numbers are given on the project page, and no design files have been published. The maker's Hackaday.io gallery for the project includes a photo of the finished badge, one of which has been saved to this entry.
