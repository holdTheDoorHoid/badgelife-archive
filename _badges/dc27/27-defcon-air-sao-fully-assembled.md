The DC26/27 Defcon Air SAO is a Shitty Add-On made by Harbinger LTD (Tindie seller "awkwardai") for DEF CON 27, sized to plug into the SAO header of the previous year's DEF CON 26 badge — hence the "26/27" in its name. The artwork combines two references the maker cites directly: the 20th anniversary of *The Matrix*, and the film *Con Air*, playing on the pun "Def Con Air" with imagery of Nicolas Cage's plane smashing through the "Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas" sign.

It was sold assembled for $15 on Tindie alongside other Harbinger LTD SAOs from the same DEF CON 27 lineup, documented as a group on the maker's Hackaday.io project "Defcon 27 Shitty Add-On's." As of this research pass the Tindie listing shows the seller on a sales break, so current availability is unclear. No technical specifications (MCU, LEDs, SAO connector version) or design files were published on either page found during this research.
