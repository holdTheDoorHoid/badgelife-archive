Kredence built the Intrepid Monarch as a playing-card-style PCB badge for DEF CON 27 (2019), part of the annual "Illuminati Party" badge line the maker is best known for. The card itself, printed in black, white, and copper, carries the "Intrepid Monarch" title banner, an owl illustration, the Illuminati Party crest, and a short maxim ("Exploit the Rules / Decipher the Unknown / Command Your Own Affairs / Search Out Vulnerabilities"). No electronic components are visible in the available photo, and the maker's GitHub repository for it holds only that photo and a one-line description, with no schematic, firmware, or Gerbers.

Hackaday's DEF CON 27 badge roundup describes the related "monarch and sovereign badges" from the same maker as "a nice middle-ground between electronic and non-electronic badges," which fits what can be seen here, though the coverage does not name this card specifically. Pricing, production quantity, and availability for this particular card could not be confirmed; a DEF CON-era post from the maker's account mentions a "Monarch Badge" selling for $40, but it isn't clear whether that referred to this exact card or the line as a whole.
