TwinkleTwinkie's "I'm 40% UFO!" is a Shitty Add-On made for DEF CON 27 (2019), shaped like the flying-saucer form Bender briefly takes in a Futurama episode. Two LEDs light the character's eyes and two more light the mouth, mostly in white; a limited run reportedly used yellow LEDs instead. The board carries a matte finish over its plating to cut glare, and the back is printed with Bender quotes rendered in Futurama's fictional Alienese script.

The SAO uses the v1.69bis (6-pin) header standard and runs at 3.3 V drawn from the host badge, so it needed no battery or MCU of its own. It was sold through TwinkleTwinkie's Tindie store as an assembled unit; that listing is now marked sold out, and the maker's storefront has since gone on hiatus, so current availability and original pricing/quantity could not be confirmed.
