The Unicorn SAO is a glitter-finished, unicorn-shaped Shitty Add-On that TwinkleTwinkie made for DEF CON 27 (2019), inspired by the maker's daughter. It grew out of experiments with side-view LEDs: five 1204 side-view LEDs in red, orange, yellow, blue, and pink shine into a hot-glue diffuser sculpted into the unicorn's mane, blending the colors together evenly rather than showing them as distinct points of light. It runs on 3.3V, drawing power from the host badge, and uses the SAO v1.69bis (6-pin) connector standard while remaining compatible with the original 4-pin SAO layout.

TwinkleTwinkie sold the Unicorn SAO fully assembled through their Tindie store alongside other DC27-era SAOs and badges. As of this check the listing is no longer available for purchase, and the storefront notes the seller is taking a break; no production quantity was published. No hardware design files, firmware, or a repository for the Unicorn SAO were found, so it does not appear to have been released as an open-source project.
