The DC27 SAO Gold/White Combo was a two-piece shitty-add-on sold by sqearlsalazar (also the maker of the DC26 Fry Add-On and later Baby Bender/Baphomet/HailSatan pieces) for DEF CON 27 in 2019. The set paired a gold badge with a plain white SAO, sold together through the maker's Tindie storefront under the tagline "I apologize for nothing!"

The gold badge is the more elaborate of the two: it carries two slow-blinking RGB LEDs standing in for eyes and two fast-blinking RGB LEDs for a mouth, giving it a simple blinking face. It can run off a coin-cell battery or draw power from a DEF CON 26 or 27 main badge. The combo shipped with both a male and female v1.69 2x3 SAO connector left for the buyer to solder on themselves, along with a pin and clutch for wearing it separately from a badge.

The listing has since been marked "Product Retired" on Tindie, with no price or quantity information remaining on the page. No hardware or firmware files were found to be published for this piece.
