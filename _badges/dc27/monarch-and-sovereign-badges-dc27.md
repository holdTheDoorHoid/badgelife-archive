The Monarch and Sovereign badges are a companion pair of unofficial badges Kredence brought to DEF CON 27 in 2019, alongside their better-known Illuminati and Popcorn Bucket badges from the same event. Hackaday's photo roundup of DC27's unofficial badge scene features both the front and back of the pair, describing them as sitting in "a nice middle-ground between electronic and non-electronic badges" — suggesting a mostly passive PCB-art design rather than a fully powered, blinky badge, though no maker page confirms the construction in detail.

No storefront, repository, or maker write-up for this specific pair turned up in research, so technical specifications, price, and production numbers are unconfirmed. A search-engine snippet of a tweet from Kredence's account mentions selling "the Monarch Badge" for $40 alongside a $10 popcorn SAO, which may describe this item, but the tweet itself could not be loaded to confirm the date or exact badge referenced, so that figure was not carried into the record.
