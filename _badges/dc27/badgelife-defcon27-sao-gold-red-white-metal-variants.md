A family of DEF CON 27 add-on badges from Tindie seller sqearlsalazar, sold both as single-color pieces (Gold, and others) and as multi-piece bundles: a Red/Metal Combo, a Gold/White Combo, and a "combo (all 3)" bundle pairing the "Shiny Metal," "Glorious Golden," and "Human Badge White" variants. Each piece is a small RGB-LED add-on ("SAO") that plugs into a compatible DEF CON 26 or 27 badge via a 2x3 pin header (v1.69 style, sold with one male and one female connector that the buyer solders on), or runs standalone off an included CR2032 coin cell. The two onboard RGB LEDs "light up the eyes" with a fast color-changing blink; there is no display, radio, or other interactivity described.

All of the maker's listings for this line, including the gold single piece, the red/metal combo, and the three-piece combo, are now marked "no longer available for sale" on Tindie, with the maker's shop notes mentioning a break through early March 2020. No pricing, production quantities, MCU, or open-source design files were ever published for these pieces as far as could be found; the storefront descriptions are the only documentation.

A closely related but distinct listing, the Gold/White Combo, already has its own archive entry (`dc27-badgelife-def-con-27-sao-gold-white-combo`); this entry is kept separate since its title and links point at the gold, red/metal, and all-three-variant listings rather than that specific combo.
