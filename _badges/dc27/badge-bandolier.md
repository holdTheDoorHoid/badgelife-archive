The Badge Bandolier is a leather harness built by the maker known as LqqkOut for DEF CON 27 (2019), aimed at the "#badgelife" crowd who collect and wear many independent electronic badges over the course of the conference. Rather than a badge itself, it is a wearable rig for carrying several badges at once: a mesh panel lets badges be zip-tied on so they can't be knocked loose, while grommets route power cables from a set of large lithium batteries through adjustable voltage regulators, letting the wearer light up multiple badges simultaneously from one power source on their body.

It was featured in Hackaday's coverage of hardware shown at their DEF CON 27 breakfast meetup alongside other non-badge hacker hardware of that year. No pricing, production quantity, or ongoing availability information has surfaced; the maker's own Twitter/X profile, linked from the original community sheet, could not be retrieved to check for further detail.
