Scotty Allen, better known for his Strange Parts YouTube channel and shop covering Shenzhen electronics manufacturing, brought an oversized novelty PCB shaped like Grumpy Cat to DEF CON 27 in August 2019, worn around his neck. It appears to have been a small, informal batch of decorative circuit boards rather than a commercially released or fully electronic badge -- no sources describe a microcontroller, LEDs, or other active components on it.

At the con, Allen met the designer of the well-known oversized "bigfuckingbadge" MIDI badge (Facebook handle @boucherbranden -- likely, but not confirmed to be, hexum064, the bigfuckingbadge's designer on Hackaday.io), and the two traded their large novelty PCBs with each other -- an exchange Allen later called one of the highlights of his weekend and posted about as "the meeting of the Grumpy Cat PCBs at #defcon."

No project page, repository, or store listing was found for the Grumpy Cat badge itself, and it does not appear in contemporaneous roundups of DEF CON 27's electronic badges, consistent with it being a one-off art piece rather than a distributed hardware badge.
