This Hackaday.io page ("Defcon 27 Shitty Add-On's," project #166344) is Awkward Intelligence's project journal for their entire lineup of SAOs made for DEF CON 27 in 2019 — it is a round-up, not a record of one badge. The maker describes pushing past simple blinky designs that year, standardizing on a Kingbright-compatible 1206 side-view LED footprint, 0805 passives, and 2x3 SAO headers, and settling on 0805/1206 parts sourced ahead of time so builds could proceed in parallel.

The writeup names several distinct pieces from that run: "Dumpster Fire" (a flame-flicker effect achieved with hot-glue light channeling on a matte-green/white-ink board with exposed gold immersion finish), "All out of Gum" (a They Live tribute using reverse gull-wing LEDs), "Def Con Air" (a Matrix-anniversary/Con Air pun sized to mate with the prior year's badge), and a "Shitty Semen Detector" UV-light gag SAO — plus two unnamed giveaway pieces limited to under 50 units each that were never sold. Each of those is a separate collectible with its own story and deserves its own archive entry rather than being folded into this page.

Because this record describes the collection rather than a single item, the standard fields above (functions, colors, tech, pricing, images) are left blank; filling them in would require attributing details to a specific one of the several designs the maker describes only in prose.
