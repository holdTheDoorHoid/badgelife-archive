The Bob Ross Happy Trees SAO is a joke entry in AND!XOR's lineup of DEF CON 27 add-ons: a 6-pin (v1.69bis) SAO board themed around the painter Bob Ross that, per the maker's own README, "does nothing" electrically. Its sole purpose is to sit in front of another SAO and frame it, with a hole punched through the board so it can be zip-tied to a badge for extra hold. The maker recommends ordering it with a light-colored soldermask and black silkscreen so the artwork reads clearly.

AND!XOR shared the Gerber files on OSH Park (under user zapp1337, a member of the AND!XOR crew) for anyone who wanted to fab their own copy; the OSH Park listing shows a small two-layer board (about 61 x 40 mm) uploaded in April 2019, ahead of DEF CON 27. No schematic or bill of materials was published alongside the Gerbers, which fits its status as a passive, non-electrical decoration rather than a functional add-on.

No pricing, production quantity, or distribution details for physical units were found in the sources checked; the only concrete numbers available are the OSH Park fab listing's board dimensions and per-board manufacturing cost, not a retail price for finished units.
