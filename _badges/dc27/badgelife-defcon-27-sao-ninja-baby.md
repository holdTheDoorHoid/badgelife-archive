The Ninja Baby SAO is a gold, robot-shaped shitty add-on (SAO) made by sqearlsalazar for DEF CON 27 (2019). It lights up with two slow-blinking RGB LEDs for eyes and two fast-blinking RGB LEDs for a mouth, giving it a simple blinking "face." It can run off its own included coin cell battery or draw power from a compatible DEF CON 26 or 27 main badge.

The SAO uses the 1.69bis-style 6-pin (2x3) connector, and the listing notes that a male and a female connector are included but must be soldered on by the buyer to mount it. It also ships with a pin and clutch so it can be worn on its own, like a pin, independent of a host badge.

The Tindie listing for this item is retired ("no longer available for sale"), with no price or quantity-made disclosed. No design files, firmware, or open-source hardware repository were found for it.
