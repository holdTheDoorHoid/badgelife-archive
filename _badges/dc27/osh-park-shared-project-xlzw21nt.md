The "Prince OSHCat DEF CON 27 SAO" is a cat-shaped SAO (Shitty/Simple Add-On) designed by TwinkleTwinkie, shared publicly as an OSH Park project rather than sold through a storefront. The shared project's PCB was uploaded on June 30, 2019, sizing it to DEF CON 27, and the design is a 2-layer board measuring roughly 1.97 by 2.72 inches. OSH Park's shared-project listing includes a bill of materials link and downloadable design files, letting anyone order their own copy of the bare board.

Beyond the board outline and dimensions, no maker write-up, repo, or press coverage describing the SAO's electronics (MCU, LEDs, power) or how it was originally distributed at DEF CON 27 could be located, so those details are left blank pending further research.
