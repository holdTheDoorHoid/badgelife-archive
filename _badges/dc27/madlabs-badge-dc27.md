The MadLabs Badge is an unofficial electronic badge from DEF CON 27 (2019), made by a
badgelife maker or team going by MadLabs. It appears alongside dozens of other
independent badges in Hackaday's pictorial roundup of DC27's unofficial badge scene,
which notes that a few hundred were produced but offers no further detail about its
design, functions, or how it was distributed.

No maker storefront, source repository, or social presence for MadLabs turned up in
subsequent research, so beyond the two photographs in the Hackaday article (front and
rear of the badge), nothing further about its hardware, price, or availability could
be confirmed.
