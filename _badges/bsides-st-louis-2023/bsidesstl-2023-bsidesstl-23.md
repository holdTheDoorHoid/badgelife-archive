BadgePirates, a group known for making conference badges "for fun and no profit," produced this badge for BSides St. Louis in 2023. It is listed under the title "BSidesSTL 23" both in the group's own GitHub organization and in their public badge catalog, which confirms it as a genuine BadgePirates project for the event rather than a rumor or a mislabeled sweep result.

The GitHub repository (archived as of September 2024) contains KiCad PCB layout files and Inkscape artwork sized at 150mm by 150mm, along with BadgePirates' standard drawing-sheet templates, but no schematic, bill of materials, or firmware. The PCB layout itself shows no populated component footprints aside from a logo silkscreen, so it is not possible to say from the available files whether the badge carries any electronics (MCU, LEDs, display) or is a purely decorative PCB piece. No storefront listing, price, quantity, or photo of the finished badge could be found.
