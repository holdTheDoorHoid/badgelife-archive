The HITBSecConf2019 Amsterdam badge was a special-edition electronic badge issued to attendees of the 10th-anniversary Hack In The Box Security Conference, held 6-10 May 2019 at the DoubleTree by Hilton / Beurs van Berlage in Amsterdam. Rather than a static ID card, it was built to be reprogrammed and hacked, with hidden functionality attendees could unlock over the course of the event.

HITB ran a dedicated Badge Village at the conference where attendees were taught how to get into the badge's firmware, reprogram it, and unlock secret features and challenges. Beyond that framing, neither the official HITB conference archive nor secondary listings disclose the badge's hardware details (microcontroller, LEDs, display, or connectivity), its price or production quantity, or a named hardware designer distinct from Hack In The Box as the organizing entity. No photos of the badge or design files could be located; the record is limited to what the event's own pages describe.
