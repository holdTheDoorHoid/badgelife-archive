The Black Badge Raffle is a long-running, unofficial DEF CON tradition run by a maker known as "sonicos" (@DefconRaffle), who built an ESP32-based electronic badge for the raffle at DEF CON 30 (2022) and an SAO version the following year. For DEF CON 32 (2024), the project took the form of a trading card game: the Black Badge Raffle TCG, or BBRTCG. Cards riff affectionately on con culture and in-jokes — one from the saved set jokes about "Random QR Codes" around DEF CON being "generally a Bad Idea," another recreates a pink Black Badge Raffle admission ticket as a game artifact.

Packs of BBRTCG cards circulated at DEF CON 32 in August 2024 and quickly became a fixture of the convention's contest scene. DEF CON's own DC32 contest results write-up for the "Wall of Bribes" reports that after organizers were given a few packs, "most bribes focused to the Black Badge Raffle TCG" for the rest of the weekend — attendees traded in oddities (in one case a PCI Ethernet card claimed to be haunted) specifically to get BBRTCG cards. The maker's site was already serving card images days before the con opened, and the surviving card scans are printed with a "DC32a" edition mark and a 2024 copyright, tying them to this specific year.

The series continued: a Black Badge Raffle SAO had been the DC31 (2023) offering, and by DC33 (2025) the trading cards had grown into a crowdfunded run of over 4,000 packs (roughly 50,000 individual cards) funded via Indiegogo. Exact pricing, print quantities, and the precise distribution mechanic (raffle prize vs. direct giveaway vs. purchase) for the DC32 cards specifically were not found in public sources.

## History

- **DC30 (2022):** Black Badge Raffle Badge — an ESP32-based electronic badge, open-sourced on GitHub by sonicos.
- **DC31 (2023):** An SAO version of the raffle badge, sold for around $5.
- **DC32 (2024):** The Black Badge Raffle Trading Card Game (BBRTCG) — this entry.
- **DC33 (2025):** A larger, crowdfunded run of BBRTCG cards (Indiegogo campaign "Black Badge Raffle - DC33 Trading Cards").
