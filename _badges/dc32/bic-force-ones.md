The BIC-FORCE ONES is a novelty badge from Blacks in Cyber (BiC), the group behind DEF CON's Blacks in Cybersecurity Village. Rather than a wearable electronic badge in the usual conference-badge sense, it is a custom PCB shaped and decorated like a Nike Air Force 1 sneaker, fitted with multicolor LEDs and a built-in battery so it lights up whether it is worn, set on a desk, or displayed in a car.

The maker's listing frames the badge explicitly as a tribute to Black American sneaker culture: it name-checks the Air Force 1's 1982 debut and 1990s re-release, and its enduring status as a cultural staple, tying the badge's design directly to that history rather than to a game or CTF mechanic. The listed dimensions are 3.25 x 1.75 x 0.25 inches, and it sold for $65.

The listing itself is titled "BIC Village Badge 2023," which suggests the design may have first appeared at DEF CON 31 before still being available when the DEF CON 32 community sheet was compiled in mid-2024. No technical details beyond "multi-colored LED lights" and "a battery" are given, and the storefront no longer lists the product as of this research pass, so quantity made and current availability are unknown.
