Ninjaican appears on the DEF CON 32 community badge sheet as a maker name with no
other information: no title, links, price, or notes were filled in on the source
row. Ninjaican previously made the [Sailor Moon Badge](sailor-moon-badge) for DEF
CON 31, working within the alt_bier badge family (the Sailor Moon badge's project
page is hosted at sailormoon.altbier.us, and describes Ninjaican as "the daughter
from one of the badgemakers").

Checking alt_bier's own portfolio site and their documented DEF CON 32 project (the
G0dzilla VS Badge) turned up no badge or SAO credited to Ninjaican for 2024 - that
year's alt_bier badge is credited solely to alt_bier. No independent storefront,
Hackaday.io project, or social presence for Ninjaican could be located. It is
possible Ninjaican did not release a separate item in 2024, or that it was never
documented online; this entry is left as unknown pending better information.
