The DEF CON Shoot 32 Badge is not an electronic badge at all: it is a 3D-printed rubber band gun that doubled as the entry credential for the DEF CON Shoot, a side event held alongside DEF CON 32 (2024). Made by seeess, who has produced a Shoot badge in multiple years, the design modifies an existing Thingiverse rubber band gun (thing:5950314), reworking the wheel, trigger, frames, barrel, and bolts for better fit, durability, and print quality.

The badge is worn on a lanyard whose retractable-spool concept was designed by a collaborator credited as Hamsa, letting the wearer aim and fire the gun without taking the lanyard off. It loads 6-7 rubber bands (size 12, 1-3/4" x 1/16") onto a rotating wheel and fires them via a trigger and pin mechanism; the maker's README includes loading tips such as always resting bands on the pin above the trigger to keep them from catching.

All design files and detailed build/modification notes are published on GitHub, making it fully open source, though no price or production-quantity figures were given since it was distributed as a free entry badge to Shoot participants rather than sold.
