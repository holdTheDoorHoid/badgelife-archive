Technick, a longtime independent DEF CON badge maker known for his "Jack" playing-card-themed series (New Jack at DC27, Monopoly Jack at DC29, King Jack at DC30, Pirate Jack at DC31, later AI Jack at DC33), also sold a separate DEF CON 32 badge under the deliberately mysterious placeholder name "F CAESARS." The Tindie listing leaned into the mystery, describing the badge as "nameless" and promising an "official reveal" on the maker's X (Twitter) account, with the seller noting his badges are one-time runs: "I never remake them. When they're gone, it's gone."

The badge sold for $25 (or $20 each for orders of three or more) and shipped from the United States. It was sold out by September 6, 2024. No public source ever disclosed the badge's microcontroller, LED count, display, connectivity, or exact production quantity, so those fields are left blank here rather than guessed.
