Listed on the DEF CON 32 (2024) community badge sheet under the maker name
"Verbaal and H4r0ld (House of Hacker Horrors)" with no further detail in
the row itself. This pair — DEF CON Goons and Blue Team Village/Lonely
Hackers Club regulars who run a recurring "EFF BadgeLife" fundraiser line
under the name "H4r0ld & V3rbaal's House Of Hacker Horrors" — are
independently documented elsewhere in this archive selling a badger-shaped
PCB badge referencing EFF's "Privacy Badger" browser extension, priced at
$70 with proceeds to the Electronic Frontier Foundation. A DEF CON 34
listing photo for the makers' later "Electric Fist" badge explicitly
labels that badger design as their "DC32 design," which is why this entry
has been filled in on that basis rather than left blank.

Whether this badger badge actually debuted at DEF CON 32 in 2024, or was
instead a rerun of a design the same makers first sold at DEF CON 31 in
2023 (as a separate archive entry, dc31-eff-privacy-badger-badge, claims),
could not be settled this pass — the two sheet-derived entries disagree,
and no independent DC32-specific storefront listing or photo could be
located to break the tie. No technical details (microcontroller, LEDs,
display) or a firm quantity were found for either year's sale.
