The Voyager Golden Record SAO is a free add-on given out at DEF CON 32, styled after the cover of the actual gold-anodized aluminum record NASA attached to the Voyager 1 and Voyager 2 probes in 1977. According to the community sheet it was fabricated in gold ENIG finish, echoing the original disc, and breaks out two GPIOs for whoever wants to hack on it further.

The sheet credits it to Abhinav of Hackerware.io alongside collaborators Max and Hankashyyyk. Hackerware.io (Abhinav Panda) is a prolific DEF CON 32 badge maker with several other credited pieces that year — the AI Village Badge, Arcanum Badge, Bug Bounty Village Badge, Constellation Badge, and Flare Badge — but none of the maker's own published pages, portfolios, or the wider web turned up an independent listing, photo, or write-up of this specific SAO, so most technical and appearance details could not be confirmed beyond what the sheet recorded.
