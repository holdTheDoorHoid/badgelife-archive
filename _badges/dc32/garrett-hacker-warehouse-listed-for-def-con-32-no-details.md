The community sheet for DEF CON 32 (2024) lists "Garrett / Hacker Warehouse" as a badge-life vendor at the con but gives no title or details for what they brought. Hacker Warehouse is a longstanding DEF CON vendor-floor fixture selling hacking tools and badgelife pieces. Their current storefront's Badgelife category carries two items, the Hacker Mindset Badge and the Cicada Invada DIY Kit, but both appear to date to 2023 rather than being made specifically for DEF CON 32, and no source found ties either one (or anything else) to the 2024 con by name.

Because the maker and product for this specific year could not be confirmed from any source, this entry is left as unknown rather than assuming it refers to the already-catalogued Hacker Mindset Badge (see the separate DEF CON 31 entry for that item).
