The Recon Soldier is Recon Village's electronic badge for DEF CON 32 (2024), part of a yearly badge series the village has run since 2017. It is a 3D, multi-layer PCB assembly built around a soldier figure wearing a military-style helmet, with red and blue LEDs lighting the character's eyes. The design carries the village's "Tactical Recon" theme for that year, continuing a tradition of village-specific badges distinct from the main DEF CON badge.

Beyond the maker's own description and feature list, no further technical details (microcontroller, LED type/count, power source, price, or production quantity) were published or found. It is treated here as released, since Recon Village's badges are distributed to attendees at the village each year, but the specific distribution method (free, kit, or sale) for this one is not documented.
