The community-maintained DEF CON 32 badgelife spreadsheet lists a maker identified only as "Brock (Infosec Exchange)," with no badge or SAO title, price, link, or notes recorded next to the name -- the row is blank apart from the maker credit itself, both in the archive's import and in the original Google Sheet it came from.

The same person or handle appears to have made a prior item: "SAO Marquee," a $35 SAO sold at DEF CON 31 in 2023 and promoted via the Twitter/X account @INIT_6_ (see the `dc31-sao-marquee` entry). Whether they brought a follow-up piece to DEF CON 32, and what it was, could not be confirmed -- no product page, storefront listing, Hackaday.io project, or social post naming a DC32 item from this maker turned up in search, and the X profile itself could not be fetched. Until a source surfaces naming what (if anything) they actually released for DC32, this entry is left without a retitle.
