The Quacked-Out Duck SAO is a duck-shaped add-on badge that the Cyber Swamplords — the Central Florida hacker collective behind HighRollerCon — handed out by the thousands at HighRollerCon 2024, held on the LINQ High Roller observation wheel in Las Vegas on August 10, 2024, timed to run alongside DEF CON 32. The duck comes in six named mystery variants — Blackout, Fallout, Bleedout, Whiteout, Hideout, and Tripout — each a differently colored PCB cutout in the shape of a rounded, big-eyed duck, with small SAO edge-connector pins visible along its bottom edge. Recipients did not choose which variant they got; the joke, echoed in the "go to High Roller Con" speech bubble on the promotional art, was the same "mystery" gimmick that later became common for badgelife SAO drops.

One duck SAO (one of the six variants, chosen at random) was also folded into HackerBox #0104 "Engage," a June 2024 subscription electronics box from HackerBoxes, alongside other DEF CON-season builds like the Alien Robot Badge Kit and several UV-printed SAOs. No maker documentation was found describing onboard electronics, a microcontroller, or LEDs for the duck itself, so it appears to be a passive, decorative SAO rather than one with active circuitry — though this could not be confirmed either way from available sources.

As of this research pass, HighRollerCon's own website no longer carries any trace of the 2024 duck SAO giveaway; it currently only solicits sponsorship inquiries for a 2027 event, so this entry relies on a third-party HackerBoxes/Instructables account of the original invite rather than the makers' current page.
