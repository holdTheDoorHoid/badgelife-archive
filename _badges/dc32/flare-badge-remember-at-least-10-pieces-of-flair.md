This badge was a free giveaway at DEF CON 32 (2024), made by Abhinav Panda of Hackerware.io for the FLARE team. It carries a full-colour illustration of a hacker raccoon based on FLARE's mascot, and its title plays on the "10 pieces of flair" line from the movie *Office Space*. It was distributed first-come-first-served, with drop locations announced through the FLARE-related channels on the RTV (village) Discord server rather than sold or reserved in advance.

No dedicated project page, photo, or hardware spec sheet for this specific badge could be located; Hackerware.io's own portfolio site does not list it among its featured works, and general web searches for the title turned up nothing beyond the original community badge sheet. It is confirmed, however, that this is the first of a recurring FLARE-branded badge series from the same maker: a "FLARE RACCOON PURPL3HAT" badge followed at DEF CON 33 (2025), and further FLARE blinky and CTF badges appeared at DEF CON 34 (2026). Whether "FLARE" here refers to Mandiant's FLARE reverse-engineering team (which runs the annual Flare-On CTF) or the cybersecurity company flare.io could not be determined from available sources.
