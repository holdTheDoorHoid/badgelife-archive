This badge was a free giveaway at DEF CON 32 (2024), listed on the community badge sheet under Abhinav Panda of Hackerware.io. It carries a full-colour illustration of a hacker raccoon inspired by Flare's mascot, and its title appears to nod to the "pieces of flair" gag from the movie *Office Space*. It was distributed first-come-first-served, with drop locations announced in the #General and flare channels on the RTV Discord server rather than sold or reserved in advance.

No dedicated project page, photo, or hardware spec sheet for this specific badge could be located; Hackerware.io's own portfolio site does not list it among its featured works, and general web searches for the title turned up nothing beyond the original community badge sheet. Later community sheets show this is the first of a recurring Flare-branded line from the same maker: a "FLARE RACCOON PURPL3HAT" badge followed at DEF CON 33 (2025), and "Flare Blinky" and "Flare CTF" badges are listed for DEF CON 34 (2026). Whether "Flare" here refers to Mandiant/Google's FLARE reverse-engineering team (which runs the annual Flare-On CTF) or the security company Flare Systems (flare.io, the destination of the DC33 sequel's sheet link) could not be determined from sources for this badge.
