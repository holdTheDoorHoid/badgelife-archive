BlackGirlsHack is a cybersecurity training nonprofit focused on Black women and girls in infosec, known for its annual SquadCon conference and community programs. The community badge sheet listed them as present at DEF CON 32 (2024) but recorded no details about what, if anything, they distributed.

Research turned up no evidence of a BlackGirlsHack-branded electronic badge or SAO, at DC32 or any other year. Their only documented involvement at DEF CON 32 was as a supporting donor of contest prizes (CompTIA and TryHackMe exam vouchers, a Cyberjutsu training course, and swag such as lockpicks and Lego sets) for the "Octopus Game" contest, run jointly with the Women's Society of Cyberjutsu. Their own online store sells apparel, stickers, vouchers, and books, but no hardware. It is possible the original sheet entry conflated conference sponsorship or table presence with badgelife participation; without a surfaced badge or SAO, this entry is left as status unknown rather than guessed at.
