Wireless Village appeared on DEF CON 32's community badge sheet with no accompanying details -- title, maker, and features all blank beyond the village's name. Wireless Village is also known as RF Hackers Sanctuary, the group that has run the Radio Frequency Village (formerly WiFi Village, then Wireless Village) at DEF CON since the early 2010s; DC32's own village listings confirm they were present that year running RF-focused talks and a Radio Frequency Capture the Flag.

No source located during this pass describes a specific badge or SAO the village made or gave away at DEF CON 32. The group's own website did not surface any badge content, and general write-ups about the village cover its history and activities rather than any hardware giveaway. It remains possible the sheet entry refers to a physical item that simply hasn't been documented online, or that "Wireless Village" was listed for its CTF/village presence rather than a badge at all.
