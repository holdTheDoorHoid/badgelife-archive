The Edgerunner License is a novelty prop badge from DC Punks (Kay Kidoutai and NeonPlaidPants), sold on Tindie as "your license for mischief in the cyberpunk dystopia." It is built from laser-cut, chrome-mirrored acrylic layered over a blue PCB, styled to look like an ID card or permit rather than a conventional electronic badge.

Its one electronic trick is NFC-triggered: holding the card near an NFC reader lights it up red, playing on the "license" conceit as if the card were being scanned and approved. The Tindie listing does not specify the microcontroller or LED part used to drive that effect, and no separate maker page, Hackaday.io project, or open-source repository was found describing the internals.

It was sold for $22.22 with pickup available at DEF CON 32 in 2024, and remains listed as in-stock on Tindie as of this check, with over a hundred units sold to date.
