Ben's Badges — a two-person outfit, Ben and Hugo — made a wearable LED badge for DEF CON 32 in 2024, sold as a preorder through their Squarespace storefront (bensbadges.com) for pickup at the con. The badge listing (as captured on the community sheet) described 1,984 LEDs for scrolling text or patterns, plus a built-in microphone and accelerometer for reactive lighting effects, at $120.

As of this check in September 2026, the maker's site confirms the badge was made, sold, and distributed at DEF CON 32 ("the badge went down even better than we hoped it would"), but the store section no longer lists it or shows any product photos or specs, so the LED count, microphone/accelerometer, chip, and price could not be independently verified from a primary source — they are carried over from the original sheet import. The site says Ben & Hugo are considering a second run: a dual-purpose version that could be worn or mounted on a wall to display data from a connected machine (weather, bandwidth, firewall status), and invites interested buyers to email ahead of production.
