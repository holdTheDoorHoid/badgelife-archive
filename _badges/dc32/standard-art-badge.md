GothCon is the crew behind the long-running goth-themed dance party held during DEF CON, and each year they sell fundraiser badges to help cover the cost of the (free, DEF-CON-unaffiliated) event. The community sheet for DEF CON 32 (2024) lists two separate GothCon badge rows for that year: an LED "Blinky Badge" and this "Standard Art Badge," both priced at $40. The Blinky Badge is separately confirmed by GothCon's own social media as a randomized "Spirit Board" themed LED badge; the Standard Art Badge most likely represents a simpler, non-illuminated counterpart sold alongside it for buyers who wanted the artwork without the electronics, but no maker page, press coverage, or social post naming this specific badge could be found to confirm its design, materials, or exact distribution.

GothCon's badges are sold through their Shopify storefront and in person at the party during the convention, with proceeds going toward the event rather than to DEF CON itself.
