The Society of Shenanigans Badge was a DEF CON 32 (2024) badge made by CyDefe Labs LLC and funded through a dedicated Indiegogo crowdfunding campaign rather than sold outright. The community sheet lists a $50 price and notes that the campaign offered seven "co-creator" slots at $75 each, alongside a teaser that the makers would scatter "gyms" around the convention for battles — suggesting some kind of Pokemon-gym-style scavenger or challenge game tied to the badge, though the mechanics were never confirmed from a maker source.

Little else about the badge could be pinned down. The Indiegogo campaign page (confirmed live via the entry's t.co link, which redirects to `indiegogo.com/projects/society-of-shenanigans-defcon-32-badge`) sits behind a Cloudflare bot-detection wall that blocked every attempt to read its content, so the badge's appearance, electronics, and final funding outcome remain unknown. CyDefe Labs LLC does not appear to have a live web presence under the obvious names: their apparent domain is an unclaimed Squarespace placeholder, and the `@cydefe` handle referenced in the sheet no longer resolves to an active X/Twitter account.
