The community badge sheet for DEF CON 32 (2024) lists "Space Bits 'R Us" as a maker with no further details — no title, description, price, or link. No independent record of a 2024 item from this maker could be found.

The name "Space Bits R Us" is documented from the prior year: at DEF CON 31 (2023) it was the team name adopted by a group of competitors in the Hack-A-Sat competition, who made themselves an exclusive team badge (not sold to the public) alongside a separate "Space Pony" SAO that the team did sell at the Aerospace Village (see the dc31-space-pony-sao entry). Whether this same group returned for DEF CON 32 with a new badge, and what it might have been, is not established by any source found during this research pass.
