The DCPunks Countdown to Y2K Badge is an independent electronic badge sold by DC Punks - the two-person crew behind the recurring "DC Punks" merch table, run by Kay Kidoutai and NeonPlaidPants - at DEF CON 32 in 2024. It carries the group's signature pink PCB, with the "Countdown to Y2K" lettering picked out in blue, and a built-in countdown clock that flashes when the display rolls over to Y2K, playing on the Y2K anxiety theme that also ran through that year's official DEF CON badge and branding.

The badge sold for $33.33 through the group's Tindie storefront, with buyers able to arrange pickup at the con instead of shipping. Each unit shipped with a 9V battery and a lanyard, but the listing did not disclose the microcontroller, LED part, or any display technology used, so those fields are left blank rather than guessed.

No hardware or firmware files were found to be published for this badge, and current stock status is unknown as of research date; the Tindie listing was live but did not state whether units remained.
