The EZ Puzzle Blinky Badge was made by T9ck for DEF CON 32 (2024). Per the community
badge sheet, it sold for $40 with the first five given away free, in a run of 75, and
was distributed informally rather than through a storefront — the sheet's contact note
points people to "this year's popular bar (circle or otherwise) OR offsite" to find the
maker, suggesting in-person, word-of-mouth distribution typical of small badgelife runs
rather than a pre-announced drop.

Its stated function is to simulate the feeling of hitting a slot-machine jackpot,
implying some combination of lights (and possibly sound) tied to a win/lose or
random-outcome mechanic, though no chip, LED count, or display details could be
confirmed from any independent source.

No maker page, GitHub repository, Hackaday.io project, storefront listing, or press
coverage for T9ck or this badge turned up in research. It appears to be one of the
many small, undocumented badges that circulated at DC32 without an online footprint;
the sheet entry may be the only surviving record.
