BadgePirates (Badge Pirates LLC) is a small, longstanding badgelife maker group that has built
conference badges and SAOs since 2016, including entries for SecKC/DEF CON 27, DEF CON 26, and
more recently CactusCon 13/14 and BSidesKC. They were listed on the community badge sheet as
present at DEF CON 32 (2024), but the sheet row carried no further detail about what they brought.

A check of BadgePirates' own website, blog, Tindie storefront, and GitHub organization turned up
no badge or SAO specifically documented as a DEF CON 32 / 2024 release. Their public output around
that time includes a general-purpose "SAO Totem-Sword_v2" holder (2024, sold on Tindie) that is not
tied to any specific event; the badges they do list for that period are BSides KC 2024 and the
CactusCon 13 and 14 badges, and their own DEF CON list stops at DEF CON 29. It is possible BadgePirates brought an SAO or badge to DEF CON 32 that was never written up
publicly, but nothing could be confirmed from available sources.
