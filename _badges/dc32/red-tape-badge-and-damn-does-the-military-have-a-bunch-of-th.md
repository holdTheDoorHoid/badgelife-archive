VetCON is a veteran-focused community space that has run its own badge at DEF CON since at least DC31, distributed through the ThreatHunter.ai storefront (run by Milton Security Group) rather than sold at the party itself. The 2024 badge — nicknamed "Red Tape Badge" on the community tracking sheet for its artwork — pairs a front face with a color LCD screen, four buttons, and a USB-C port under bold "VETCON 2024" lettering, with a back panel depicting the U.S. Capitol building bound in red tape, a raised hand, and a line of soldier silhouettes over a desert-camo PCB finish, a pointed jab at military and veterans'-affairs bureaucracy.

The badge was preorder-only and sold for $90, with pickup restricted to the VetCON Community Space in person during DEF CON 32 (August 8-11, 2024) — it was not shipped, and unclaimed preorders were forfeited. As with VetCON's other merchandise, 100% of net profit was earmarked for VetCON's own events and the Semper Fi & America's Fund charity. No separate maker credit beyond the VetCON name was found, and no hardware or firmware files have surfaced publicly; whether the board is open source is unknown.
