Antihacker Hacker Club is a San Antonio, Texas hardware/soldering group (Tindie
and X handle `0xAHHC`, tagline "We drink and we solder things") that appears on
the DEF CON 32 community badge sheet as an attending badgelife maker, but the
sheet row carried no further detail about what they brought that year.

The club is known for one prior badgelife release: "The Birds are not Real," a
broSAO (brooch + SAO) for DEF CON 31, sold on Tindie with a CR1220-powered
blinking LED bird. No source turned up during this pass ties a specific,
separate item to DEF CON 32 for this maker — their Tindie storefront still
lists only the DC31-era broSAO in the Wayback Machine's 2024 and later
snapshots, and neither a web search nor the DC32 badgelife forum thread
surfaced a DC32-specific product. It is possible they simply attended and
distributed the same DC31 SAO again, or brought something that was never
documented online, but that cannot be confirmed from available sources.
