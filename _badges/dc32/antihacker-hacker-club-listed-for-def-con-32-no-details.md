Antihacker Hacker Club is a San Antonio, Texas group (Tindie seller "Anti Hacker
Hacker Club", handle `0xahhc`) that appears on the DEF CON 32 community badge
sheet as a badgelife maker, but the sheet row carried no further detail about
what they brought that year.

The club is known for one prior badgelife release: "The Birds are not Real," a
broSAO (brooch + SAO) listed on the DEF CON 31 community sheet and sold on
Tindie with a CR1220-powered blinking red LED bird. No source turned up during
this pass ties a specific, separate item to DEF CON 32 for this maker, and the
DC32 badgelife forum thread does not mention them. It is possible they simply
attended and distributed the same DC31 SAO again, or brought something that was
never documented online, but that cannot be confirmed from available sources.
