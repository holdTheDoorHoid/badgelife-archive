The Rick Roll SAO is a small add-on board from DC Punks (Kay Kidoutai and NeonPlaidPants) built to do exactly one thing: play Rick Astley's "Never Gonna Give You Up" at whoever it's plugged into or handed to. The maker's Tindie listing describes it as showing the music video in "one bit color" with looping 12kbps audio, plus a mute switch for the inevitable moment someone wants it to stop.

It was sold on Tindie for $33.33 with free shipping, with pickup also offered in person at DEF CON 32, alongside other DC Punks releases that year like the Edgerunner License and the big pink y2k countdown badge. No hardware details (MCU, LED type, SAO header version) or open-source files turned up in the sources checked for this pass; the listing focuses on the joke, not the specs.
