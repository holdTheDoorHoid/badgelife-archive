AND!XOR's "Who really knows... just buy the damn thing" listing was not a distinct badge but a philanthropist pre-purchase option for the group's DEF CON 32 (2024) badge, sold on their Shopify store for $250 in Black, Purple, and Clear color variants. Buying it guaranteed the purchaser one of that year's badges without knowing its design in advance — the product page states plainly that supporters were funding "additional badges," transport and upgrades for the group's 5n4ck3y hacking project, and general production costs, in exchange for a badge shipped directly rather than won or found at the con.

The listing set expectations for buyers: no in-person pickup in Las Vegas, shipping by early August 2024, no guaranteed delivery before the con for international orders, and hacker challenges tied to the badge that would not be posted to AND!XOR's CTFd server until the Friday of DEF CON. It credited Urbane Security, Macrofab, and CTFd as that year's sponsors. All three color options were marked sold out on the Shopify page, matching the community sheet's note that the run had fully sold.

Because the badge's actual electronics were unrevealed at the time of purchase, no chip, LED, or display specifications could be confirmed for this entry; those details would belong to AND!XOR's underlying DC32 badge itself rather than this purchase listing.
