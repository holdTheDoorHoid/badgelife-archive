The DEF CON 32 (2024) community badge sheet listed JollyBadge as bringing a badge that year, but the maker had not released its name at the time the sheet was compiled. The sheet notes a $125 pre-order price, with pickup at the Cyber Swamplords vendor table at the con for anyone unwilling to wait for post-con shipping.

JollyBadge, run under the handle @GetJollyBadge and the site getjollybadge.com, previously made the DC30 "Jolly Roger" badge (see the separate dc30-jollybadge entry). As of this research pass, the maker's current site and GitHub only document a "JollyBadge V2" (SAMD21-based, open firmware at github.com/stevejabs/jollybadge-v2) tied to DC33 (2025) in the site's own copy, and no archived snapshot of the site or its Twitter/X account from mid-to-late 2024 could be found. Whatever badge JollyBadge actually released at DEF CON 32 remains unidentified.
