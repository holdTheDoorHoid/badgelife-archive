Alee's "TPB Badge" was listed on the DEF CON 32 (2024) community badge sheet as a $100 set described only as "a badge and three (3) SAO's" — no further detail, links, or contact information was given on the sheet, and no source found during this research names the set directly or explains what "TPB" refers to.

The same maker later sold badges on Tindie under the handle alee97422 (linked from this archive's DC33 "cRab" and DC34 "Breadbadge" entries). Around the time of DEF CON 32, that store carried several small individually-sold SAOs — logo boards for Arch Linux, Ubuntu, and Docker, plus a rocket SAO, a PCB keychain, and a cowboy SAO — but none of the archived Tindie listings from that period is named "TPB" or described as a three-SAO bundle, so it is not possible to say from the sources checked here whether any of those pieces were part of this set.

Without a photo, a maker post, or a matching product listing, the badge's appearance, hardware, and how many were made remain unknown.
