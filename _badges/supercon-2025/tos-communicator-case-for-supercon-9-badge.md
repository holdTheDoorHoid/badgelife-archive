Thomas Flummer, a longtime badge and SAO designer, published a free 3D-printable case that turns the official Supercon.9 Communicator badge (the 2025 Hackaday Supercon badge) into a replica of the flip-open communicator prop from the original 1960s *Star Trek* series. The design was built by referencing the design files Hackaday published for the Communicator badge itself, so the case's cutouts line up with the badge's PCB, keyboard, and battery.

The case uses a "Fuzzy Skin" slicer setting to give the body a leather-like texture, and is finished with chrome trim, a metal hinge pin joining the flip-up antenna lid to the main body, and brass inserts for the fasteners. The lid opens to expose the badge's screen and controls, matching the iconic gesture from the show even though the actual badge's internal layout differs from the original prop.

## Make your own

The print files are shared for free on Makerworld. Print the parts (the Fuzzy Skin setting is recommended for the body), add the metal hinge pin and brass inserts, and assemble around your own Supercon.9 Communicator badge.
