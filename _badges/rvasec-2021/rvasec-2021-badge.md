HackRVA, the volunteer hackerspace that has built RVAsec's conference badge nearly every year since the con's early days, made the badge for RVAsec 2021. The conference itself is confirmed to have returned to in-person format on November 4-5, 2021 at the Omni Richmond Hotel in Richmond, VA (tickets held over from the cancelled/scaled-back 2020 event were rolled forward), and RVAsec's own 2021 recap post credits Hack.RVA with "an amazing badge."

Beyond that, no source recovered in this pass gives technical specifics for the 2021 board: no confirmed MCU, LED count, display, price, production quantity, or design-file links, and no photo of the badge itself turned up. HackRVA's own build wiki, which otherwise documents badge hardware year by year, breaks off after 2020 with a note that a 2021 badge was "not looking good," and never posts a follow-up describing what (if anything) was actually built and distributed once the conference went ahead. It is possible 2021's badge was a simpler, lower-documentation effort compared to the RP2040-era badges HackRVA released starting in 2022.
