"Peace Was Never an Option Badge" is a SAINTCON 2024 minibadge made by a hobbyist going by "Mister X," styled after the antagonist goose from the video game *Untitled Goose Game*. The PCB art shows the goose in profile holding a yellow LED shaped like a key in its beak, with the badge's title lettered across the right half of the board. It carries no microcontroller; it is a beginner-level soldering project with two SMD LEDs (D1, D2) and two matching resistors (R1, R2) on the back, plus four 2-position headers along the edges so it can interlock with other SAINTCON minibadges.

The maker rated it "Rare" and listed it under the "Personal" category on the SAINTCON community minibadge database (minibadge.wiki), rather than selling it: their own acquisition note says they traded it for other attendees' badges, or simply gave it away free to anyone who didn't have a badge to trade. No production quantity, price, repository, or design files were stated or found.

The board's back silkscreen also carries the handle "@thatonemisterx," likely the maker's own social handle, though it was not independently confirmed as linked to this project.
