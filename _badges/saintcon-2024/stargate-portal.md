The STARGATE Portal is a SAINTCON 2024 minibadge by maker SHIFTY, styled after the ring-shaped Stargate portal from the classic Stargate film and TV franchise. It is listed in the "Personal" category on the community-run minibadge.wiki database, which tracks SAINTCON's yearly minibadge scene.
