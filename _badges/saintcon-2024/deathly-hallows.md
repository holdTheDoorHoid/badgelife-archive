The Deathly Hallows minibadge was made by kittysedai for SAINTCON 2024, part of a large personal series of minibadges they produced that year (also including Clever Girl, D20, Hacker Era, Hold Monster, Winter Is Coming, UNIX System, DISA, and doTERRA). It depicts the Harry Potter symbol formed by the Elder Wand, the Cloak of Invisibility, and the Resurrection Stone, with the maker's description adding a line about Severus Snape's enduring love for Lily.

Like the rest of the maker's 2024 minibadges, it is a beginner-difficulty, self-assembly board: the recipient hand-solders through-hole LEDs and a resistor using the single-pad method, then adds pin headers. It was not sold — kittysedai's listed method of acquisition was direct trade at the convention — and the community sheet marks it "Super Rare" under the "Personal" category, consistent with a small, one-off trade badge rather than a mass-produced release.

No maker storefront, GitHub repository, or social profile could be located, so hardware details beyond the soldering instructions (LED count/type, board colors, exact quantity made) are not documented anywhere and are left blank here rather than guessed.
