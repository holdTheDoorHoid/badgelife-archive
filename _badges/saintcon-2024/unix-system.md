The UNIX System minibadge is a SAINTCON 2024 minibadge made by kittysedai, riffing on Jurassic Park's famous line "It's a UNIX system, I know this!" It's a personal, one-off project rather than part of an established series, and the maker rates it "Super Rare" on the community minibadge sheet.

The badge is a small solderable kit: a beginner-level build with LEDs and a resistor attached using the single-pad hand-soldering method, plus pin headers. Exact LED count, colors, and dimensions aren't documented anywhere found. It wasn't sold — the maker's listing simply says "Trade with me," meaning the only way to get one was an in-person swap at the con.

No maker storefront, repository, or design files were found, so build details beyond the soldering instructions above are unknown.
