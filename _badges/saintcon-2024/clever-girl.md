"Clever Girl" is a SAINTCON 2024 minibadge by maker kittysedai, themed around the original *Jurassic Park* film — the title quotes game warden Robert Muldoon's last line before a Velociraptor attacks him. It is a simple hand-solderable board: a red LED and an orange LED (top and bottom respectively) driven through a resistor, soldered using the single-pad method, with a beginner soldering difficulty rating.

The maker listed it under the "Personal" category with a self-assessed "Super Rare" rarity, and made it available only by trading directly with them at the con rather than through any storefront. No chip, connectivity, or power source beyond the simple LED circuit is described, and no design files, repository, or independent maker page could be found — the only record located is the maker's own submission to the minibadge.wiki community database.
