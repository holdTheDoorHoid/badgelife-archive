The Keep-Community-2024 minibadge was made for The Keep, a hacking-skills community run by 801Labs at SAINTCON 2024. The Keep was split into two areas: the Exploit Armory, where attendees learned offensive-security tools and techniques, and the Dungeon, a competitive environment where players used those tools against vulnerable Windows and Linux boxes for leaderboard points. The community gave out a minibadge to visitors of its booth, though SAINTCON's own event page left the collection details ("come visit our booth for more details") without saying what the badge looked like, its price, or how many were made.

Design files for the badge -- a KiCad schematic, PCB, and project file, plus an Adobe Illustrator concept sketch -- live in the `utahsaint-org/MiniBadges2024` GitHub repository under `Keep-Community-2024`, alongside dozens of other 2024 SAINTCON minibadges. No README accompanies the folder, and no maker name is credited there or on the community's own page, so this entry credits 801Labs as the community behind it rather than a named individual designer. No photo of the finished board could be located.
