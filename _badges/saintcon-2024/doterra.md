The doTERRA minibadge is a SAINTCON 2024 minibadge by maker kittysedai, themed around the doTERRA essential-oils brand ("gift of the earth" in Latin). The community listing describes it as a beginner-level soldering kit: a single through-hole LED soldered onto an SMD pad (D1) with its legs bent to curve toward the badge's center, plus one resistor soldered using the single-pad hand-soldering method, and pin headers.

The maker categorized it as a "Personal" badge and rated it "Super Rare," distributed only by trading directly with them at the con rather than through any public storefront. No hardware files, repository, or price were found; the only source located is the minibadge.wiki community badge database, which does not list PCB colors, shape, or a maker profile page.
