The Reaper's Bones Badge is a SAINTCON 2024 minibadge by a maker known as Mister X, part of a personal minibadge set themed around the pirate factions of the video game Sea of Thieves. This particular badge represents the Reaper's Bones, an in-game faction associated with piracy and combat against other players. It carries no series identifier beyond the maker's own description of a "Sea of Thieves minibadge set."

Electronically it is simple: two LEDs and two matching resistors, hand-soldered using the single-pad technique, connected through four 2-position headers oriented so the Reaper's Bones artwork faces outward. The maker rated the soldering difficulty as Beginner. No microcontroller, display, or wireless connectivity is present.

The badge was not sold. According to the maker's own listing, it was given away free to trade at SAINTCON 2024, or handed out at no cost to anyone without a badge of their own to trade. It is marked "Super Rare" and categorized as "Personal" on the community minibadge.wiki database, the only source found for this item; no independent Hackaday, storefront, or repository page for the maker or the badge turned up.
