Every SAINTCON badge ships with a minibadge expansion board: a small add-on PCB with header slots where attendees plug in the "minibadges" (klip-ons) they collect or trade at the con. The bare board leaves rows of exposed header pins and solder points on its back, which tend to snag on shirts and lanyards. This listing is a free, 3D-printable snap-on cover designed to sit over those pins and protect clothing from them.

The 2024 version is a redesign of covers made for earlier SAINTCON years, reshaped specifically because the 2024 minibadge expansion board added a USB-C port that the older cover geometry didn't clear. STL and STEP files are provided on Printables so anyone can print their own copy for free; no assembled units, kits, or paid listings are involved.

The maker is not confirmed directly from the Printables listing (the page could not be fetched past a Cloudflare bot-check), but the pattern strongly points to compukidmike, the Printables user credited with the 2022 predecessor of this same cover and the designer behind the official SAINTCON minibadge expansion board hardware itself.
