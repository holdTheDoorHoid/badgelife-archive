The Merchant Alliance Badge is a SAINTCON 2024 minibadge by maker "Mister X," one piece of a themed set based on the video game *Sea of Thieves*. It represents the game's Merchant Alliance trading faction, alongside companion minibadges for the Gold Hoarders, Order of Souls, and Reaper's Bones factions, plus a capstone "Athena's Fortune" badge awarded to anyone who collected three of the four faction badges.

Electronically it is simple: two through-hole LEDs and two matching resistors, hand-soldered using the single-pad method, connected through four 2-position headers so it can be chained to other minibadges with the artwork facing outward. The maker rated it Beginner difficulty to solder.

The maker gave the badge away for free at SAINTCON, or traded it for other badges; no price was ever attached to it. minibadge.wiki, a community-run catalog of SAINTCON minibadges, lists it as "Super Rare," though quantity made is not reliably recorded.
