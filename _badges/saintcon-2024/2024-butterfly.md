The 2024 Butterfly is a personal minibadge made by RuShan (maker of several other SAINTCON 2024 minibadges and accessories) as an entry in what the maker describes as a yearly butterfly-themed minibadge tradition. This installment adds a 3D-printed element to the usual PCB minibadge format.

It was not sold or distributed through the SAINTCON store; per the maker's listing, it could only be obtained by trading with RuShan directly or as a bonus for supporting their other projects, and it is marked "Super Rare" on the community minibadge wiki. No chip, LED, or SAO details are documented for this badge, and no dedicated build guide, repository, or Gerber files were found beyond the general soldering-instructions link to the maker's YouTube channel.
