The Order of Souls Badge is a SAINTCON 2024 minibadge made by a badgelife maker known as Mister X, representing the Order of Souls — one of the trading company factions from the video game *Sea of Thieves*. It is one of six pieces in a self-made "Sea of Thieves" minibadge set that also includes badges for the Gold Hoarders, Merchant Alliance, and Reaper's Bones factions, an "Athena's Fortune" badge for players who complete the other four, and an official "Sea of Thieves Minibadge."

The badge is a simple, beginner-difficulty build: two through-hole LEDs and two matching resistors are hand-soldered to the back using the single-pad method, along with four 2-position headers, with the Order of Souls artwork facing outward. Mister X listed it as "Super Rare" and distributed it by trading at the convention, offering it free to anyone who didn't have a minibadge of their own to trade for it.

No repository, BOM, or fabrication files for the badge were found; the only record located is the maker's own submission to the minibadge.wiki community database, which does not name a PCB house, MCU, or specific LED part.
