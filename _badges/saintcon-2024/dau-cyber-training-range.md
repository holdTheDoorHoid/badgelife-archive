The DAU Cyber Training Range minibadge is a shield-shaped, non-electronic trade badge that maker RuShan made for SAINTCON 2024, honoring the DAU Cyber Enterprise Team — the instructors who run the Defense Acquisition University's cyber training range. The red-and-gold badge carries a circuit-trace emblem of a torch held in a fist and the Latin motto "OFFENDICULO ENIM TUTELA," a nod to the badge's defense/security theme.

It's listed on the community-run MiniBadge Wiki as "Super Rare," with the maker reporting under 50 total in circulation. Rather than being sold, it was given out to people who attended RuShan's talk at SAINTCON, or to members of the DoD and "4th Estate" (the Department of Defense's non-service defense agencies). No hardware files, storefront, or fabricator are documented; RuShan's YouTube channel (@Herushan1) is linked from the wiki as a general soldering-instructions resource rather than a project-specific page.
