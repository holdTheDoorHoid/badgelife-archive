DISA is a SAINTCON 2024 minibadge by kittysedai that riffs on the Defense Information Systems Agency, the US Department of Defense agency responsible for connecting and protecting the "warfighter" in cyberspace — a natural in-joke for a security-conference badge collection. It is a simple beginner-friendly soldering project: two through-hole LEDs (D1 and D2) mounted on SMD pads with their short legs oriented inward and bent to curve toward the center of the board, plus a single resistor soldered using the single-pad hand-soldering method, and pin headers.

The badge was not sold; per the MiniBadge Wiki community listing it was distributed by trading directly with the maker, and the wiki marks it "Super Rare." No maker storefront, GitHub repository, Hackaday project page, or social media post was found describing it further, so details like PCB color, exact shape, board house, and whether design files were ever published remain unknown.
