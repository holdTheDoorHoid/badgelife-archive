The D20 is a hand-soldered minibadge made by kittysedai for SAINTCON 2024, shaped after the twenty-sided die used in tabletop role-playing games like Dungeons & Dragons and Dungeon Crawl Classics. It's one of several personal minibadges kittysedai brought to that SAINTCON (others in the same series include "Clever Girl," "Deathly Hallows," and "Winter Is Coming"), each built around a pop-culture or hobby reference.

The badge carries through-hole LEDs and a resistor, both meant to be soldered by the recipient using the single-pad hand-soldering method, then finished with pin headers. Soldering difficulty is listed as beginner. It was not sold; the maker's SAINTCON minibadge community listing says simply "trade with me," and its rarity is marked "Super Rare" on that listing, consistent with a small, personally-made run handed out through in-person trades rather than any storefront or crowdfunding campaign.
