The 40oz Stanley Mug is a SAINTCON 2024 minibadge shaped like the popular insulated tumbler, described by its makers as keeping "your drinks ice-cold for hours or piping hot through the day." It was a two-maker collaboration: atticus produced it in black and blue, while kittysedai produced white and purple versions, giving collectors four colorways to track down.

Like other SAINTCON minibadges, it was not sold but obtained by trading directly with either maker at the conference. The community-run minibadge.wiki lists it under the "Personal" category with a "Super Rare" rarity rating, but no maker-run page, repository, or storefront was found describing quantities made or further details.
