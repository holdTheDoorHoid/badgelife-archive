Winter Is Coming is a SAINTCON 2024 minibadge by maker kittysedai, referencing the Game of Thrones / House Stark motto. It was made in both white and black PCB versions. The maker categorized it as a "Personal" badge (a one-off/small-run design rather than a village or vendor badge) with a "Super Rare" rarity rating, and describes it as available only by trading directly with them at the con.

It is a simple beginner-level soldering project: the maker's instructions call for hand-soldering LEDs and a resistor using the single-pad method, then adding pin headers to the board. No chip, LED type/count, or power source is documented by the maker, and no hardware or firmware files are published, so those fields are left empty.
