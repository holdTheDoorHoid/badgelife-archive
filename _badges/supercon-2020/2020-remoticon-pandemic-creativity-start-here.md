This page is the Hackaday.io hub for the 2020 Remoticon, the virtual event Hackaday ran in place of an in-person Supercon during the COVID-19 pandemic, held November 6-8, 2020. It is a "start here" landing page rather than a badge or SAO listing: it points visitors to 22 workshops and 8 demos on topics like 3D-printer firmware reverse engineering, soldering, circuit design, and firmware analysis, and it credits a team of about 14 people led by "Lutetium" (including Aleksandar Bradic and Mike Szczys), with sponsorship from ARM, Microchip, Digi-Key, Twilio, and Supplyframe.

No physical badge, SAO, or other hardware is described or sold through this page - there is no chip, LED, price, or availability information because none exists for it. The page does note that a community member, Thomas Flummer, put together an unofficial fan-made badge design and shared it on GitHub independent of the official event, which could be worth its own archive entry if someone tracks down that repository, but that was outside the scope of this pass.
