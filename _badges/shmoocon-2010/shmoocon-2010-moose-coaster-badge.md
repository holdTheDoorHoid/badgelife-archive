ShmooCon 2010's badge was not electronic: it was a round foam/rubber drink coaster printed with a moose head, the text "2010 Shmoocon," and a set of numbers. The numbers were part of that year's badge puzzle, designed and run by G. Mark Hardy (Darth Null), a ShmooCon tradition of turning the badge into a solvable challenge.

Each attendee's coaster carried one of several data sets — coordinates and bearing/range figures — that combined with numbers printed on safety brochures, speaker badges, the conference program, and schedule posters (eleven data sets in all) to identify a chain of global airports by code. Working the full chain in order spelled out a hidden phrase, playing on "Mickey Mouse" via the moose theme. Winners of the puzzle received conference tickets, stuffed moose-head trophies, and airline vouchers to DEF CON.

The badges were given free to attendees as their conference credential rather than sold, and no production quantity or price was published.
