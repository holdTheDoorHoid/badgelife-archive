Cyber City Circuits, an Augusta, GA PCB design and assembly shop, put together an SAO (Simple Add-On) badge kit for attendees of BSides Augusta 2021. The company posted a short tutorial video on their YouTube channel walking through the kit, with the description simply thanking attendees for coming to the 2021 event.

Beyond confirming the kit's existence and its maker, no further build details surfaced: the video's description carries no information about the chip, LED count, price, or how many were made, and no matching hardware or firmware repository could be found among Cyber City Circuits' public GitHub projects. Cyber City Circuits also made the BSides Augusta 2019 badge and has since assembled badges for other events, including several Kernelcon badges, so this SAO kit fits their pattern of small hands-on electronics kits for regional security conferences.
