BadgePirates made this attendee badge for BSides St. Louis 2019: a rectangular PCB cut into the shape of the city's downtown skyline, with the Gateway Arch picked out in gold soldermask against black silkscreen and a white board. It carries no electronics — the badge is a wearable PCB keepsake rather than a powered device, distributed to conference attendees.

The project repository's artwork folder shows the badge was produced in several role-specific variants sharing the same skyline design — Participant, Speaker, Sponsor, Organizer, and Volunteer — distinguished by their printed labels, a common BadgePirates practice for identifying attendee roles at a glance. No build guide, price, or production quantity was published alongside the design files.
