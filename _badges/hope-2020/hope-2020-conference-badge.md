The HOPE 2020 conference — held fully online, July 25 to August 2, 2020, after the COVID-19 pandemic forced HOPE (Hackers on Planet Earth) off its usual in-person venue — came with a promise from organizer 2600: registered ticket holders would receive an exclusive conference t-shirt and badge, described as something that would "never be made available to anyone else." That is the entirety of what 2600 said publicly about the item.

No source found describes what the badge actually was. HOPE's numbered in-person conferences have a history of custom electronic "badge kits" (HOPE XV in 2018, for example, had its own electronic badge with a wiki and user manual), but HOPE 2020 had no physical venue, and nothing turned up connecting an electronic/PCB badge, a maker, a GitHub repo, or a fabrication run to this specific item. The 2600 store's HOPE 2020 merchandise collection lists only talk downloads and USB flash drives of the conference recordings — no badge product appears there, consistent with it never having been sold or made public.

Given the thinness of the record, this entry stays largely empty rather than guessing at a form factor, chip, or price that no source actually states.
