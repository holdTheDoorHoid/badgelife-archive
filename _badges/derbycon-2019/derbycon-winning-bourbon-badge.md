The DerbyCon Winning Bourbon Badge is a 3D-printed, bourbon-bottle-shaped commemorative piece made by technick for DerbyCon 9.0 "Finish Line," the conference's final edition in September 2019. It has a wood base with black detailing and no electronics - a keepsake rather than an electronic badge or SAO.

Each badge is stamped with the word "Winner," playing on the idea that simply making it to the last DerbyCon made you one. Technick, who had previously given out small runs of printed badges at DEF CON, made this piece as a send-off and toast to DerbyCon's conclusion. It sold for $15 on Tindie, pickup-only at the event, with orders cut off at midnight Mountain Time on September 3, 2019; the listing has been marked out of stock since.
