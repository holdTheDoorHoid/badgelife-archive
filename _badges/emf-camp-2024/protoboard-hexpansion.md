The Protoboard Hexpansion is a bare prototyping board by Jake Walker for the Tildagon badge, EMF Camp's hexagonal conference badge platform. Rather than a functional gadget, it exposes the Tildagon's hexpansion expansion connector as a grid of 140 unconnected pads (10 wide by 14 deep), with a 3.3V power rail along the top and a ground rail along the bottom, plus a header breaking out every available signal down one side.

The idea is to let other badge hackers try out hexpansion circuit ideas by hand-soldering wires and bridging pads, rather than committing to a custom PCB run with an ENIG finish before knowing whether an idea works. It sold on Tindie for $4.50 and has since gone out of stock (listed as sold out since June 28, 2026).

No hardware design files, firmware, or fabrication files were found published alongside the listing, so it is treated as closed/unknown on the open-source question.
