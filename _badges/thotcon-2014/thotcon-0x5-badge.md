The THOTCON 0x5 Badge is a limited-production conference badge made by Hackaday.io user rudy for THOTCON 0x5, held in Chicago in 2014. Its design borrows the look of a classic video game controller, built around Arduino-compatible AVR hardware and a 102x64 graphic LCD screen.

A USB port was the badge's headline feature: it let attendees plug into video game consoles set up in the Hacker Village at THOTCON 0x5, tying the controller-styled hardware to an actual gaming activity at the con. The maker's project page describes the badge as "fully open," but no schematics, firmware, or Gerber files are attached to the listing, so the hardware and firmware sources could not be confirmed or linked here.
