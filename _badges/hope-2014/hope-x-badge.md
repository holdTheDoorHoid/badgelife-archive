The HOPE X attendee badge was the credential issued to registered attendees of the tenth Hackers On Planet Earth conference, held July 18-20, 2014 at the Hotel Pennsylvania in New York City. It takes the form of an embroidered cloth patch cut into a police badge/shield outline, with "HOPE" stitched across the top in alternating white, yellow, pink, and blue outlined letters, a large "X" below in the same palette, and a gray banner reading "2600" - referencing 2600 Magazine, HOPE's parent publication and organizer - stitched across the bottom tab. Two grommet holes at the top let it be strung on a lanyard or cord rather than clipped like a typical convention badge.

Unlike the electronic SAO-style badges that later HOPE conferences (and much of the badgelife scene) became known for, this is a purely physical, non-electronic credential - there is no PCB, MCU, or lighting involved. No maker/embroiderer credit, price, or production quantity for the patch has surfaced in public sources; it is documented here from a single CC0-licensed photograph on Wikimedia Commons.
