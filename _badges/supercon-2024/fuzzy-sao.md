Fuzzy SAO is a hand-sewn felt hedgehog made by Laura (Hackaday.io handle ellemef) as an entry in the Hackaday Supercon 8 (2024) SAO Contest. Rather than a conventional PCB, the body is felt with embroidered details, built around a small protoboard "spine" that carries a 6-pin SAO connector and jumper wiring. Five sewable LED sequins are stitched in with conductive thread and glow through polyfil stuffed inside the felt for even diffusion, giving the creature a soft internal light rather than exposed LEDs.

The maker described it as intentionally simple and shared an SVG template of the felt cutout pieces on the project page, along with build-in-progress photos. She mentioned hoping to sew a few more to bring to Supercon 2024 to share, though no evidence was found of it being sold, kitted, or produced beyond that. It was not named among the contest's four production winners or its honorable mentions.

## Make your own

The project page includes an SVG file with the felt pattern pieces (a hedgehog silhouette) for cutting the fabric shapes. Construction from the photos: cut felt per the SVG, build a small protoboard spine holding a 6-pin SAO header, stitch LED sequins into the felt using conductive thread routed to the protoboard, stuff with polyfil for diffusion, and sew the body closed around the spine.
