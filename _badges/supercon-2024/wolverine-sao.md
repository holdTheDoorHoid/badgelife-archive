Simen E. Sørensen's Wolverine SAO is a piece of PCB art shaped like the Marvel character's head, torso, and claws, made notable by how aggressively the board is milled: razor-thin claws and steep inner-angle cuts that Sørensen expected PCBWay to reject outright. He said he sat on the finished design for about six months before finally sending it out, certain a redesign would be needed — and was surprised when the fab house ran it as drawn.

He posted a video of the finished part on Mastodon on October 7, 2024, tagged with #hackaday, #supercon, #sao, and #pcbart, followed the next day by a close-up photo calling out the steep inner angle between the head and blade and a small routed hole between the hand and the face/connector area, wondering aloud how small the milling bit must have been to cut it.
