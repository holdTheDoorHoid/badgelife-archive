No source found during this pass confirms a distinct physical badge or SAO made specifically
for TiaraCon 2017 ("Alliances!"), the women-focused DEF CON sub-conference that ran July 27–28,
2017 alongside DEF CON 25. The event's own GoFundMe fundraiser page describes talks, resume
review, and a job fair as what attendee donations supported, with no mention of any badge, pin,
or electronic item.

A TiaraCon badge does exist and is documented — an ATtiny85-based charlieplexed LED badge with
pony PCB art by securelyfitz (Joe FitzPatrick) — but that project is explicitly labeled for
TiaraCon **2016**, not 2017, and is already its own separate archive entry
(`tiaracon-2016-tiaracon-2016-badge`). No 2017 successor to that badge could be found in the time
budgeted for this pass. This entry is left as `rumored` pending a source that actually documents
a 2017-specific item.
