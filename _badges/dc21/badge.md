The DEF CON 21 badge (2013) was a non-electronic PCB puzzle badge designed by Ryan Clarke, known as LostboY or LosT, DEF CON's longtime badge designer. Rather than an electronic device, it was fabricated as a printed circuit board styled after a playing card, using copper, soldermask, and silkscreen layers to hide a cryptography and continuity puzzle: hidden inner-layer traces revealed relationships via multimeter testing, and suit symbols, binary values, and mathematical orderings (pi, e, Gray code, LFSR) combined with floor graphics and cross-badge comparisons to build a solvable challenge across the more than 40 role and deck variants (human, press, vendor, speaker, goon, and others).

A premium "Uber" badge variant went further, incorporating a hand-assembled mechanical watch as part of a one-time-pad puzzle, distributed to a smaller set of badge holders. LosT introduced the badge and its design process in his "Welcome and Making of the Badges" talk at DEF CON 21, held August 1-4, 2013 at the Rio Hotel & Casino in Las Vegas.

No pricing, production-quantity, or design-file information was found for this badge; it was distributed as part of conference registration rather than sold.
