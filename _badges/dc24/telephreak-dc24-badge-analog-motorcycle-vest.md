Telephreak, the crew behind the annual Telephreak/2600net DEF CON party, made an analog (non-electronic) badge for DEF CON 24 in 2016. Per Engadget's coverage of that year's badge scene, one of the designers, going by the handle ch0l0man, said the team began working on the badge during the drive home from DEF CON 23 -- meaning it was in development for close to a year before DEF CON 24 in August 2016.

Coverage found in this pass describes it only as an "analog badge," without detailing its form. The archive's earlier sweep recorded it as a motorcycle vest bearing the wearer's area code and the Telephreak name, but no source located here confirms that specific description, and no photo of the item was found. This entry is distinct from Telephreak's later electronic badges (DC26 "Eleven," DC27 "Telephreak 12," DC34 "Telephreak 19"), which are filed separately.
