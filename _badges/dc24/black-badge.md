The DEF CON 24 Black Badge (2016) is the top prize of that year's DEF CON puzzle competition: a win grants the holder free admission to DEF CON for life. Badge lead 1o57 (Ryan Clarke) departed from the year's "Rise of the Machines" robot-skull design used for the general attendee badges and instead built the Black Badge around a black skull with an articulating mechanical eye that pops out of it, a practical-effects mechanism designed in collaboration with Hollywood special-effects artist Rick Galinson.

Unlike the regular DC24 badge — an Intel Quark D2000-powered electronic badge with buttons, LEDs, and printed cryptographic puzzles — no source describes the Black Badge as carrying its own electronics; it reads as a one-off mechanical/practical-effects trophy piece rather than a PCB. It was not sold or distributed to the general public; it went to whoever solved the year's badge challenge.
