"No, Just No SAO" is a Simple Add-On sold by maker znjp (Zachary Peterson, a Computer Science professor at Cal Poly, San Luis Obispo) through the Uberflux storefront. It is shaped like a small folding construction barricade and uses a classic 555 timer astable circuit to blink a single amber warning LED continuously. The maker describes it as a tribute to the "seemingly perpetual state of construction on the Cal Poly campus," styled as a sarcastic all-purpose "no" to bad ideas, feature requests, or Mondays.

The SAO measures 56mm x 31mm, sold for $20, and shipped via USPS (with a discount offered for combining shipments with the maker's other badges). Uberflux tagged it as part of the "Hackaday Supercon 10" drop, which corresponds to the 2026 Hackaday Superconference. As of the 2026-09-07 check, the listing showed 13 units remaining against 7 sold, indicating a small run (at least 20 made) that was still available to purchase.

The maker also sells companion pieces through the same Uberflux storefront, including a "NOPE SAO" ($25) and a "Social Battery SAO" ($30), suggesting a small line of snarky, novelty SAOs rather than a one-off. No hardware or firmware files were found to be published for this piece, and it does not appear to use a microcontroller beyond the 555 timer driving the LED.
