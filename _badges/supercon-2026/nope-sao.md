The NOPE SAO is a shitty add-on by the maker znjp, made for Hackaday Supercon 10 (2026) and sold through the maker's own Uberflux storefront. Visually it mashes up Shepard Fairey's "Hope" Obama poster with the "woman yelling at a cat" meme, letting the wearer flip through a set of eye animations — including an "exasperated blink" — to express indignation or superiority at whoever they're glaring at. The board measures 72mm x 48mm.

Twenty units were made; as of the September 2026 check, 16 remained available at $25 each directly from the maker, who offers to combine shipping for buyers ordering more than one of their badges. No hardware files, chip details, or LED specifics were published on the listing, and no independent coverage (Hackaday, press, forums) of the badge was found beyond the maker's own storefront and Instagram (@_znjp_), which hosts a demo clip of the animation.
