The Social Battery SAO is a plug-in add-on badge by maker znjp, sold assembled through the Uberflux badge marketplace for $30. It reads "Let your Social Battery do the talking": the board carries seven expressive LED faces spanning a mood range from fully charged to critically depleted, and two buttons on the back let the wearer change which face is shown, turning the badge into a wearable stand-in for the classic "social battery" meme.

The Uberflux listing names its drop as Hackaday Supercon 10 (2026). It is one of three SAOs znjp currently sells on Uberflux, alongside a "Just No SAO" ($20) and a "NOPE SAO" ($25); no hardware or firmware files are linked for any of them.
