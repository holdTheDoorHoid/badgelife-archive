An open-source SAO adapter board for the [WHY2025](https://why2025.org/) badge, designed in KiCad by GitHub user a-little-wifi and shared publicly during the camp (August 2025). The repository contains the full schematic and PCB layout files, but the README was left as a placeholder ("Readme and pictures coming later, enjoy the camp!") and was never expanded with photos or a description of the board's exact function, chip, LEDs, or display, so it isn't clear from available sources whether it adds extra SAO ports to the badge or adapts some other connector to the SAO standard.

There is no evidence this board was sold or distributed as a physical product — it appears to have been a design shared with the community to fabricate themselves, alongside the many independent add-ons that circulate at WHY-series camps.

## Make your own

KiCad source files (schematic, PCB, and project files) are available in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/a-little-wifi/WHY2025-SAOadapter). No bill of materials or fabrication notes were published alongside them as of this check.
