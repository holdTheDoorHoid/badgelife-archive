The Monopoly Jack Badge is technick's DEF CON 29 (2021) entry in an ongoing series of 3D-printed novelty badges featuring a recurring character named Jack. For this edition, Jack wears a top hat, mustache, and monocle in a nod to the Monopoly Man, styled by the maker as a comment on the crypto and stock-market ("HODL") obsessions that took hold during the COVID-19 quarantine period. Unlike many badgelife pieces, it carries no electronics, LEDs, or microcontroller — it is a purely decorative, printed piece.

technick sold the badge directly through Tindie starting in July 2021, offering buyers a wide choice of filament colors and specialty materials (glow-in-the-dark, wood-fill, conductive copper-fill, and thermochromic color-changing plastics), plus an optional custom nameplate. It sold for $35 and was marked sold out by mid-August 2021. It is one entry in technick's longer-running Jack series, which continued in later years with New Jack (DC27), King Jack (DC30), Pirate Jack (DC31), and AI Jack (DC33).
