leHACK 2023's official access badge was designed by Energumene, a graphic-design studio, entirely in Adobe Illustrator. It has no electronics: leHACK's organizers deliberately avoid sponsor-branded, feature-loaded conference badges in favor of what Energumene describes as an exercise in minimalism, relying on visual or print tricks rather than blinky hardware to make the badge memorable.

No pricing, production quantity, or distribution details are published on the source page, and no design files or repositories are linked. The badge appears to have been issued to attendees as their conference credential rather than sold separately.
