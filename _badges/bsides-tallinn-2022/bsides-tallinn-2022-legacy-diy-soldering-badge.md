BSides Tallinn 2022 (held September 22, 2022 at Vaba Lava in Tallinn, Estonia) gave every registered participant a badge built around a "legacy" theme. Rather than a finished, pre-assembled board, the badge doubled as a DIY soldering project: attendees assembled it themselves, with the freedom to design their own custom configuration during assembly. Hands-on setup for the badge ran in the Student track / Village, staffed by the local k-space.ee hackerspace team, from check-in through the day. The design is credited to Konstantin Shibin.

No public source describes the badge's electronics: there is no confirmed microcontroller, LED count or type, display, power source, or SAO support, and no schematic, PCB files, bill of materials, or firmware have been published. No photo of the badge itself could be located either on the official BSides Tallinn 2022 site or in the badge.gallery compendium record for it, so the entry remains image-free rather than substitute a generic or unrelated photo.
