BSides Orlando runs an annual fundraiser where attendees can buy into one of two supporter ticket tiers: the "Order of the Golden Grin" (OGG) and the "Order of the Toothy Grin" (OTG/OTGG). For 2023, both tiers came with an exclusive give-away the organizers nicknamed the "Termigator SAO hat" — styled after the con's grinning "Termigator" mascot (a green, gator-faced, sunglasses-wearing creature) wearing a small purple top hat printed with the tier initials. BSides Orlando announced it on Instagram and Facebook in late September 2023, and a follow-up Facebook post later noted that Toothy Grin tickets had sold out while Golden Grin tickets remained available, each purchase still including the SAO.

No maker storefront, hardware repository, or teardown was found for this piece beyond the organizers' own social posts, so it isn't clear whether it is a functional electronic SAO (with a header connecting to a host badge) or a decorative novelty item using the "SAO" label loosely, as some cons do for keepsake accessories. The photograph shows a two-piece look — a grinning face charm and a separate hat piece — with no LEDs or connector visible.

The community sheet that seeded this entry had it filed under the 2025 event with no year confirmed; research traced the specific "Termigator SAO hat" campaign to 2023 via a dated Instagram post and corrected the event accordingly. A different, unrelated OTG/OGG perk (a challenge coin and mission patch) was found for BSides Orlando 2025 — see the research report for that separate item.
