Built for the Badge Village at HITBSecConf2024 in Bangkok (August 26-30, 2024), this is a Raspberry Pi Pico-based fault-injection tool rather than a wearable attendee badge. It was created by ShiQi Yu (wireless and hardware) and Wei Lu (binary and hardware), two researchers from China Telecom Cyber Security Technology Co., Ltd. and the Waterdrop Laboratory, and was exhibited to let attendees explore glitching-style attacks against embedded targets such as Arduino boards.

The official conference page describes the device's purpose in detail but gives no information on distribution, price, or quantity, and no maker repository, storefront listing, or third-party coverage of the device turned up in a search. It is documented here as a village exhibit/demonstration tool rather than a badge given out to attendees, pending any further source that clarifies how (or whether) it was distributed.
