The Nullcon Goa 2014 Hardware Badge was a teaching-oriented conference badge designed by Amey Gat and Umesh, built around an ATmega8 microcontroller chosen specifically for being cheap, small, and easy to work with. It included LED indicators, an audio output, and switches for interaction, aimed at introducing embedded-systems and microcontroller basics to attendees in India's hardware-hacking scene who might not have prior experience.

The badge and its design process were the subject of a dedicated conference talk, "Nullcon Hardware Badge 101 (Desi Jugaad)," presented by its makers at Nullcon Goa 2014 (Feb 14, 2014). The talk covered how the badge idea originated, how the design evolved, and the manufacturing problems encountered along the way -- framed under the "Desi Jugaad" (local, resourceful improvisation) ethos. A recording of the talk is available on YouTube.

No maker-published project page, photo of the physical badge, price, production quantity, or open-source design files have surfaced. The community catalog badge.gallery, which the discovery sweep drew from, independently notes it has no badge photo on file for the same reason.
