HackRVA, Richmond's member-run makerspace, has built the attendee badge for the RVAsec security conference every year since the event's early days. The 2017 edition (RVAsec 6) was sponsored by Capital One, which RVAsec's own site announced at the time, directing attendees to the Capital One table in the CTF room.

No public source currently documents the 2017 badge's hardware: the microcontroller, LEDs, display, connectivity, battery, or physical design are not published anywhere found, and HackRVA's own wiki page for the build links only to a private Google Sheet, a private Google Doc, and a GitLab instance that could not be read without an account. No photo could be confidently attributed to the 2017 badge specifically, since HackRVA's general badge page mixes undated images from several years.

Later RVAsec badges in the archive (2018 onward, and the earlier 2012-2016 boards) may carry more detail from HackRVA's own build logs; this entry should be revisited if HackRVA's GitLab or a conference recap ever surfaces the 2017 schematics or a dated photo.
