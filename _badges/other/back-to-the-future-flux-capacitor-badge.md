Pedro Minatel, a Brazilian electronics hobbyist (later a developer advocate at Espressif) and member of the Laboratório Hacker de Campinas hackerspace, built this wearable recreation of the flux capacitor from *Back to the Future* as a personal project, posting it to Hackaday.io on May 5, 2018. He described it simply as combining "two passion. Electronics and Back to the Future!" and shared a demo video along with photos of the lit-up badge.

The badge runs on a single 18650 lithium cell mounted on the back, per a reply Minatel gave in the project's comments. When Hackaday editor Mike Szczys and other commenters asked for build details and where to get one, Minatel replied "I'll publish the details soon!!!" — but no further logs, component list, schematics, or firmware were ever added to the project page. It was not built for or sold at any convention; it remains a one-off maker project documented only through the original Hackaday.io post.
