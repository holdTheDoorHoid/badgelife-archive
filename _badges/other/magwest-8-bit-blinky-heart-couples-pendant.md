The MAGWest 8-Bit Blinky Heart Couples Pendant is a two-piece wearable made by electrical engineer and game programmer Tina Belmont (SoftEgg) as premium swag for badge holders at MAGWest 2018, the west-coast sister event of the music-and-games festival MAGfest. Each half of the pendant is an 8-bit pixel-art heart built from six layers of laser-cut acrylic diffusion over an ATtiny85-driven PCB with WS2812 addressable LEDs, powered by a CR2032 coin cell and controlled through a power toggle switch and three 3D-printed two-color buttons. The gimmick is in the pairing: when the two heart halves are brought together, the LED animation on both changes to signal the connection, making it a literal "couples" badge.

Belmont's write-up notes that the design was originally meant to license a heart-with-NES-controller motif from artist Jeremy Brown, but when that license didn't come through in time for manufacturing, she designed an original 8-bit pixel heart that would still fit the already-finalized board layout. Hundreds of units were reportedly manufactured and hand-assembled. No storefront, price, or open-source hardware/firmware release was found; it appears to have been a limited giveaway tied to the 2018 event rather than a product sold afterward.
