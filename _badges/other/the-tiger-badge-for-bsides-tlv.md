The Tiger Badge for BSides TLV was a soldering-village kit Hackerware.io (Abhinav SP) designed for BSides TLV in 2022, built around that year's "Welcome to the Jungle" conference theme. The badge used a big-cat motif split by role: a white tiger for attendees, a black panther for sponsors, and a red/yellow cat for crew and speakers. Each badge came pre-soldered with a few components already in place — including a through-hole CR2032 battery holder soldered SMD-style after the team ran short of SMD holders during production — leaving attendees a small, approachable soldering activity: fitting a capacitor, resistor, tactile switch, and a red LED for the glowing eyes.

Once assembled, pressing the tactile switch lights the LED eyes and plays a recorded tiger roar through a small speaker driven by an onboard audio-memory IC, an idea Hackerware had tried once before on an earlier badge. Hackerware also ran a hardware village at the event.

No storefront listing, price, production quantity, or published design files were found; the badge appears to have been distributed only at BSides TLV 2022 rather than sold.
