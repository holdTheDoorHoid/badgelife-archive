The Witch Hat is a small, inexpensive 3D-printed accessory made by tilde.industries (Didcot, Oxfordshire, UK) to dress up their Joule Thief Cat in a Halloween theme. The Joule Thief Cat itself is a beginner-friendly, through-hole soldering kit built around a joule thief circuit: it lights up a cat figure's LED eyes using the last dregs of charge in an otherwise "dead" AA or AAA battery. The kit is sold widely on general electronics/maker storefronts (Tindie, Lectronz, Etsy, Pimoroni) rather than through any conference badge channel.

The hat itself has no electronics or independent function — it simply clips onto the assembled cat kit as a seasonal decoration, priced at $6 with limited stock noted on the Tindie listing. Nothing in the maker's own pages ties either the hat or the underlying kit to a hacker convention, badge sweep, or SAO ecosystem, so this entry is best treated as general maker merchandise rather than a badge/SAO archive item.
