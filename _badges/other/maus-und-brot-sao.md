Maus und Brot is a simple SAO (add-on board) by davedarko, referencing two German-language TV/media figures: Die Maus (from the long-running children's program "Die Sendung mit der Maus," whose "Open Days of the Mouse" event apparently supplied the PCB inspiration) and Bernd das Brot, a deadpan animated loaf of bread who had recently appeared as a guest on John Oliver's American late-night show. According to the maker's own writeup, seeing PCBs made for the Mouse event, combined with Bernd das Brot's US television cameo, prompted davedarko - while visiting the United States and within reach of OSH Park's manufacturing - to design something simple purely to see how the artwork would render on OSH Park's Afterdark PCB service (a black solder-mask, gold-immersion finish option).

No chip, LEDs, price, quantity, or distribution details are given anywhere in the source material, and no photo of the finished board could be located, so this entry is likely closer to a PCB-art test than a functional electronic add-on. The project folder carries a "2510" date prefix (October 2025) but names no specific convention it was built for.
