This GitHub repository, maintained by SAINTCON attendee CompuDocUt, is not itself a single badge but a personal collection of assembly-instruction PDFs for four different minibadges made for the SAINTCON conference's long-running minibadge tradition: a "BESD Train Mini Badge," a "CompuDoc Mini Badge," and two editions of a "Grateful Dead Mini Badge" (2022 and 2023). The repo's README gives no further description beyond "Badges for Saintcon," and each PDF is a scanned soldering/assembly guide rather than a spec sheet, so no chip, LED, price, or quantity details were pulled from them.

Because this entry bundles four distinct minibadges under one title, it is being marked as not a single archive item. Each of the four badges is reported separately below as a candidate for its own entry, with CompuDocUt as maker and SAINTCON (2022 for three of them, 2023 for the Grateful Dead reissue) as the likely event.
