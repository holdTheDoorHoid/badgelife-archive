The Mini Fat Pika RGB SAO is a Pikachu-themed "Shitty Add-On" from maker TwinkleTwinkie, sold on their Tindie store for $20. It is a shrunk-down remake of an earlier 2018 "Fat Pika" SAO, about 30% smaller at 46x50mm, and was updated to the SAOv2 (6-pin) connector for a more mechanically stable fit while remaining backward compatible with the original 4-pin SAO standard. The board runs on 3.3V drawn from the host badge or a breadboard/totem, and its only described behavior is a set of RGB LEDs that flash in a fast, random pattern.

No conference or badge-series affiliation is mentioned on the listing, and as of this check the product page was marked "On A Break," meaning the maker was not accepting orders for it. No hardware files, firmware, or LED part numbers are published for this item.
