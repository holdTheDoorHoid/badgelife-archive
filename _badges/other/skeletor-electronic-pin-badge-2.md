The SKELETOR Electronic Pin Badge is a wearable electronic pin from the Italian maker TheGizmolab (also credited as GizmoLab, handle hyuma78), depicting Skeletor, the villain from the 1980s cartoon Masters of the Universe. Two red LEDs mounted on the back of the PCB light up in several blinking modes, and the badge is worn using a black pin backing. It measures about 5 cm by 4.3 cm and runs on a single CR-1225 coin cell, which is not included.

Unlike many entries in this archive, the badge was not made for a specific hacker convention; it was sold directly through the maker's own lectronz.com storefront at $40.68 as a general 80s pop-culture piece. As of this research pass it is listed as out of stock, with an option to sign up for restock notifications. No hardware, firmware, or design files were found published for it, and the specific MCU used is not disclosed on the product page.

The same maker previously produced the DC33 Power Glove Badge, also a pop-culture-themed electronic pin, suggesting this Skeletor badge may be part of a broader informal line of retro-media-themed pins from TheGizmolab.
