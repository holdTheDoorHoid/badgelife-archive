The DC406 SAO is a simple, passive add-on made for the DC406 DEF CON group in Belgrade, Montana. Designed by |)3vice Makers |{its as only their second PCB ever produced, the 1.75"x1.75" board depicts a sunrise over mountains; a single LED lights up behind the artwork to look like the rising sun when the board is powered through a standard 2x2 (v1, 4-pin) DefCon SAO header. The board also includes a small crypto puzzle for anyone who wants to dig into it.

It was sold through the maker's Tindie storefront at $20 for small quantities (up to 19) or $33 apiece for orders of 20 or more, marketed as "the only #badgelife SAO with a sunrise." The Tindie listing has since been retired as sold out, and no hardware or firmware files have been published for it.
