The Lucky Oshcat is a PCB SAO (Shitty Add-On) shaped like a maneki-neko, the beckoning "lucky cat" figure, cut in purple soldermask with the cat's paw, bell, and coin details worked into the outline. The back of the board is silkscreened "LUCKY OSHCAT / MADE BY TWINKLETWINK", crediting Twitter/X user twinkletwink as its designer.

The fabrication files were shared publicly on OSH Park by user fustini on August 15, 2018, as a 2-layer, 2.01 x 2.41 inch board priced at $24.20 through OSH Park's shared-project ordering system — meaning anyone could order a bare copy of the board, though no BOM, schematic, or firmware was published alongside it. The listing links to a tweet from twinkletwink (which could not be read directly), suggesting that account is where the design was first shown off. No specific convention or badge event is named in the surviving listing, so it is catalogued here as an unaffiliated/independent SAO rather than tied to a particular con.

No electronics beyond a pair of small header-style footprints are visible in the shared board art, and nothing in the available sources confirms an MCU, LEDs, or display, so those fields are left blank rather than guessed.
