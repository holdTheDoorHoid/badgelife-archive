The Shy Guy SAO is a Shitty Add-On shaped like Shy Guy, the masked enemy character from Nintendo's Mario franchise, made by a hobbyist or small maker going by "Potato Nightmare." No dedicated project page, storefront listing, repository, or social account for Potato Nightmare turned up in the sources checked, so nothing is known here about the board's chip, LEDs, price, production quantity, or whether it is still available.
