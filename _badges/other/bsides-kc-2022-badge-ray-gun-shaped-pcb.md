BadgePirates, a badge-making team based in Kansas City, posted this ray-gun-shaped PCB in the badge portfolio on their own site, badgepirates.com, with "BSides KC 2022" silkscreened across the barrel in cream/yellow ink on green solder mask. The design has a bulbous grip, a trigger, and a barrel built from concentric rings tapering to a small emitter ball at the muzzle -- a classic sci-fi ray-gun outline rendered as a badge-shaped board.

BadgePirates' own GitHub organization hosts a separate, well-documented repository for their actual BSides KC 2022 conference badge (`BadgePiratesLLC/BSidesKC_2022`), which is a rectangular, ESP32-S2-based "Safe" badge with a combination-dial graphic, issued in color-coded variants for organizers, sponsors, speakers, volunteers, village staff, and general participants. That repository's CAD and prototype folders do not contain a ray-gun-shaped board, so this item does not appear to be part of the same production run described there. It may be an unreleased concept, a one-off novelty piece, or the image may simply be misfiled under a 2022 folder on BadgePirates' site. No further details (chip, LEDs, pricing, or availability) for the ray-gun board specifically could be confirmed from available sources.
