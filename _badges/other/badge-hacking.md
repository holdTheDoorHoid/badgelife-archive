This entry is not itself a badge or SAO. It is a Hackaday.io collaborative project page documenting the effort by Mike Szczys and more than two dozen contributors to solve the puzzles embedded in the official DEF CON 28 "Safe Mode" badge, DEF CON's first fully virtual badge (August 2020). That badge took the form of an audio cassette rather than an electronic PCB.

The puzzles, designed by longtime DEF CON puzzlemaster 1o57, were spread across WAV audio recorded on both sides of the cassette, high-resolution PDF liner notes containing encrypted text and visual ciphers, and a lanyard printed with numbered symbols. Contributors worked through a substitution cipher that decoded to "be sure to drink your ovaltine," a Vigenere cipher keyed on "NOMATTERWHEREYOUGOTHEREYOUARE," and color-coded blocks that spelled out "DEF CON SAFE MODE" and "BOO COVID."

Because this page is a puzzle-solving writeup rather than a hardware project, it has no maker, chip, LEDs, or availability information to record, and it is being marked as not an item for this archive.
