The Big Deal With It Foot is a Shitty Add-On (SAO) by TwinkleTwinkie, shaped like a foot wearing the "deal with it" meme sunglasses. TwinkleTwinkie designed it live on stream while demonstrating how to make an SAO, then decided to actually produce it despite not having planned to. It was published on Hackaday.io on September 10, 2018, and grouped into the site's community "Shitty Add-Ons (2018)" list rather than tied to a specific conference.

The project page lists zero components, consistent with a simple passive PCB rather than an SAO with an onboard microcontroller or LEDs. TwinkleTwinkie released the full KiCad project and Gerber files as a single downloadable archive, so anyone can fabricate their own copy, but no information about a production run, price, or how (or whether) finished units were distributed beyond the stream has surfaced.

## Make your own

Download `Big_Deal_With_It_Foot_hackaday.zip` from the project's Hackaday files section, which contains the KiCad source and Gerber files, and send the Gerbers to a PCB fabricator of your choice.
