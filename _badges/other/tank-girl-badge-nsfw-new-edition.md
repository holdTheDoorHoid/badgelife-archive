The Tank Girl Badge NSFW NEW EDITION is a wearable electronic badge made by TheGizmolab (also credited as GizmoLab / hyuma78), a badge maker based in Fossombrone, Italy. It depicts the comic-book character Tank Girl in a mature ("NSFW") pose and was released in June 2024 as an update to an earlier version of the badge, adding SAO-header compatibility and revised artwork.

The board runs on an ATtiny13A-SSUR microcontroller and is powered by a CR1225 coin cell (or via a host badge's SAO connector). A pushbutton on the back cycles through programmable LED lighting effects with a short press; holding it for three or more seconds puts the badge to sleep. It is sold directly through the maker's Lectronz storefront for $52.30, shipped without a battery due to shipping restrictions on lithium cells, rather than being distributed at a specific convention.

No hardware or firmware files were found published for this badge, and the listing does not specify LED count/type or exact quantity produced. TheGizmolab is also the maker behind the Power Glove Badge sold for DEF CON 33, indicating an ongoing presence in the badgelife community beyond this single item.
