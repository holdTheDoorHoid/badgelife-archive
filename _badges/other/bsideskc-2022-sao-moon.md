This entry is a moon-shaped Simple Add-On (SAO) attributed to BadgePirates, the team behind the BSidesKC badge line, found as a set of bare KiCad PCB design files ("BsidesKC SAO - Moon.kicad_pcb") in a "CAD - Prototypes" subfolder of their BSidesKC_2022 GitHub repository. Beyond the file's existence and its title, no other documentation of it survives online.

BadgePirates' own blog writeup of the 2022 badge (an ESP32-S2-based "safe" themed main badge with 20 reverse-gullwing LEDs, produced in six role-based colorways) describes a Participant SAO made from the event T-shirt graphic and special SAOs given to Solder Village volunteers and to sponsors Arista-TGS, Corelight, Red Canary, and Tines — but does not mention a "Moon" SAO by name. It is possible this design was one of the sponsor or village SAOs under an internal working title, a separate prototype that was never produced, or simply undocumented outside the repo. Without a README, image, or announcement, its purpose, electronics (if any), and distribution remain unconfirmed.

## Make your own

KiCad source files for the PCB (and an associated footprint library) are in the repo linked above, alongside the rest of BadgePirates' BSidesKC 2022 badge project. No firmware or bill-of-materials specific to this SAO was found alongside them.
