The Skeletor Electronic Pin Badge is a small wearable PCB novelty shaped like the Masters of the Universe villain's skull, made by hyuma78 of thegizmolab and sold through the maker's own Uberflux storefront (listing GZL-1) for $30. It is built around an ATtiny13A microcontroller driving red LEDs in the eyes, with a tactile push button on the back cycling through five lighting modes; holding the button drops the badge into a low-power sleep mode to preserve battery life. Power comes from a single CR1225 coin cell, which ships separately due to postal restrictions on lithium batteries, and the badge arrives fully assembled with a pre-installed pin-back clip.

Nothing in the listing ties the badge to a specific convention or year — it reads as a general 80s-nostalgia / He-Man collectible rather than con swag, which is why it remains filed under the "other" event bucket. As of the last check, 19 units had been made, with 10 sold and 9 remaining. The same maker (GizmoLab) is also behind the DEF CON 33 Power Glove Badge in this archive, but no source connects the two items or places this Skeletor pin at any particular event.
