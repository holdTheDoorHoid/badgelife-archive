The Alpaca Electronic Badge is a small wearable electronic badge shaped like an alpaca, built by the team at Zalmotek (with Alexandra Covor credited alongside) and posted to Hackaday.io in June 2019. Rather than using a microcontroller, the badge relies on a self-blinking, color-changing RGB LED (an OPTOSUPPLY OST1MA3131A) to animate the alpaca's rainbow-colored tail, powered by a CR2032 coin cell and controlled with a simple slide switch.

The badge is a hand-assembled project: sewing hooks used to attach it to clothing were fashioned by hand from the LED's own leads. It was offered for sale through Tindie, though the specific listing page and pricing could not be confirmed from the available Hackaday.io project page. No hardware files, firmware, or repository were listed, and the project does not appear to be tied to any particular hacker conference.
