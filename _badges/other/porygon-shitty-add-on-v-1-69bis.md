The Porygon Shitty Add-On (v.1.69bis) is a Simple/Shitty Add-On (SAO) shaped like the Pokemon Porygon, created by Benchoff — a Hackaday editor and one of the people behind the Shitty Add-On standard itself. The "v.1.69bis" in its name is a nod to the SAO v1.69bis connector revision (a 2x3, 6-pin 0.1" header that added two GPIO pins to the original power/I2C pins and enabled addressable RGB LEDs like WS2812 and APA102), which Benchoff co-authored, rather than a version number for the badge design.

Beyond the maker's own two-line description — "pad printing and reverse mounted LEDs" — the Hackaday.io project page carries no schematic, bill of materials, chip identification, pricing, or distribution details, and lists zero uploaded files or components. It is dated March 19, 2019, but no source ties it to a specific conference, so the event field is left as "other" rather than guessed.
