HeartyPatch is an open-source, single-lead ECG and heart-rate-variability wearable developed by ProtoCentral, led by Ashwin K Whitchurch, and built around an ESP32 (WiFi/BLE) paired with Maxim's MAX30003 ECG analog front-end. It connects to a smartphone or cloud service over Bluetooth Low Energy and WiFi, computes R-R intervals for HRV analysis (stress, arrhythmia, and mood indicators), and pulses an onboard RGB LED in time with the wearer's heartbeat. Hardware, firmware, and companion apps are published as open source.

The project was entered in multiple categories of the 2017 Hackaday Prize and later launched as a crowdfunded product on Crowd Supply (September 2017), with units shipping from January 2018. It is a general-purpose health/wellness device rather than a conference badge or SAO, so it is not a fit for this archive as its own entry; it is included here only because a discovery sweep flagged it as related to a separately-named "HeartyBadge," which — if it turns out to be a distinct hacker-con badge — would merit its own entry.
