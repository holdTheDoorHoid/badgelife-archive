The SAO Holder - Mac version is a desktop display stand made by BadgePirates, shaped like a classic all-in-one Macintosh, designed to hold and show off up to six Simple Add-On (SAO) badges at once. It runs off a CR2450 coin cell with a power switch to save battery, and ships with three SAO connectors already soldered plus parts for three more that the buyer solders themselves.

The current (Rev B) version is molded in white to match the original Mac's case color; an earlier black revision is now sold as a limited-edition variant. It is a general accessory rather than a badge made for any specific convention, sold on Tindie for $10.99, and listed with only a couple of units left in stock at last check.
