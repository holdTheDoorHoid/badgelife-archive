The Badge Killer Shitty Add-On is a joke concept by Hackaday user Gerben (grbn), submitted to the June 2019 MacroFab Design Contest themed "Useless Machine" and sponsored by Mouser Electronics. Rather than functioning as a normal SAO, it was proposed to plug into a badge's SAO header and deliver a jolt of high voltage to it, in the spirit of a USB Killer device — the project's own tagline is "Turn #badgelife into #bricklife."

Community discussion on the page floated implementation approaches, such as a boost converter stepping 3.3V up through an H-bridge into a diode-capacitor voltage multiplier, or a multi-stage charge pump, but the Hackaday project page lists no files, no components, and no build log entries. The creator noted uncertainty about actually completing it, having no prior experience with high-voltage circuit design, so it appears to have remained a concept rather than a built or distributed device.
