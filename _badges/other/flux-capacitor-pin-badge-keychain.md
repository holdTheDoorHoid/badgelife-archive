Curious Design Labs, a maker based in Istanbul, Turkey, sells this small "Back to the Future" themed Flux Capacitor as either a wearable pin badge or a keychain. The board measures 36mm x 29mm and just 3.4mm thick, built around an ATtiny1616 microcontroller driving 25 small LEDs plus one brighter center LED to recreate the movie prop's animated "fluxxing" light effect. A push button lets the wearer power it on, choose from 8 animation patterns, and adjust brightness and speed; a low-power sleep mode holds current draw to about 0.5 microamps between uses so a single CR2016 or LIR2016 coin cell (sold separately due to shipping restrictions on lithium cells) can run it for 80-128 hours depending on settings.

The item is sold as a general novelty/prop replica through the maker's Tindie store rather than as merchandise for a specific hacker conference, and the listing does not mention any convention. It sold for $16.50 (discounted to $15.00 each at five or more units) and has been listed as out of stock since April 19, 2024, with over 400 orders and a 5-star rating across the listing's reviews at the time it was checked.
