The DEFCON 14 Homage Indie Badge is a tribute badge by the maker "|)3vice Makers |{its," recreating the look of the original 2006 DEF CON 14 "Human" badge for newcomers to badgelife. It ships as a simple through-hole soldering kit — just four types of components and six parts to solder — built around an ATtiny microcontroller that drives multi-color, slow-cycling LEDs in the badge's "eyes." It runs off a single CR2032 coin cell that the maker says lasts for days, and the whole board is finished in black to match the original's aesthetic.

The maker was explicit that this is not a counterfeit of the real DC14 badge, but a simplified, beginner-friendly tribute made to share their appreciation for the badge that first drew them into hardware hacking. It was listed for sale on Tindie and documented on Hackaday.io (project created September 15, 2018), placing its release around the DEF CON 26 badgelife season, though no source confirms it was actually sold at that specific convention. The Tindie listing is now marked as no longer available.
