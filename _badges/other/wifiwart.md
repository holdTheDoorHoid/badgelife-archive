WiFiWart is an open-source hardware project by o7-machinehum (Rootkit Labs), not a conference badge or SAO. It is a Linux single-board computer built around a 1.2GHz Cortex-A7 MPU with two WiFi radios, designed to look like an ordinary USB wall charger, intended as a covert WiFi penetration-testing tool. The electrical, mechanical, and software design files are all published on GitHub under the MIT license, and the build was documented across a five-part blog series on interruptlabs.ca (now mirrored at rootkitlabs.com) running from April to October 2021.

The maker's final post makes clear this stayed a prototype: only one working unit ("Proto 1") was completed, and a planned crowdfunding campaign to bring it to more people never happened because of the 2021 chip shortage. There is no evidence it was ever sold, given away, or associated with any hacker conference, so it does not fit the archive's badge/SAO scope and is flagged here as `not_an_item` rather than filled in as a badge.
