"Simple Add-ons (SAO)" is a Hackaday.io page by the badgelife maker TwinkleTwinkie that documents a connector standard, not a specific badge or add-on hardware. It defines a 2x3, 0.1" pitch header carrying 3.3V power, ground, I2C (SDA/SCL), and two GPIO lines, and is presented as a more accessible rebrand of the earlier "Shitty Add-on" (SAOv169bis) pinout that many badgelife SAOs already use.

The page mainly offers reference material rather than a product: a KiCad footprint library covering both "Badge" and "SAO" silkscreen conventions, and pointers to where compatible male and female headers can be sourced (generic suppliers for the male pins, TwinkleTwinkie's Tindie store or AliExpress for the polarized female connector). Because it describes a specification rather than a single manufactured item, it has no maker's event, year, price, or unit count to record.
