[ShittyWall](https://github.com/bbenchoff/ShittyWall) is a project by Brian Benchoff (bbenchoff) for mounting and displaying a collection of "shitty add-ons" (SAOs) on a wall, rather than a badge or SAO itself. The GitHub repository describes it simply as "A wall of shitty add-ons."

The repo contains no README or write-up. Its contents are purely mechanical/CAD files: a "PCB Panel" folder with a DWG drawing, a DXF outline, and a measurements PDF (likely the panel that the SAOs mount to), and a "PowerSupplyMechanical" folder with STL files for a 3D-printed base and top, apparently an enclosure for the power supply that feeds the mounted boards.

No event, year, price, quantity, or photos are given anywhere in the repository, and no further coverage of the project was found. This entry is recorded as not_an_item since it documents a display fixture rather than a specific badge or SAO.
