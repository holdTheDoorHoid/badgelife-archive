This is a Hackaday.io project by maker Kojoe consisting of uBASIC game scripts written for the 2018 Hackaday Superconference badge, rather than a badge or SAO of its own. Two playable scripts were released: "Catapult and Castle," a castle-crushing game, and "Drag Racer," a two-player gear-shifting race game; a third, four-player concept was left as a work-in-progress idea. Both released scripts were shared as plain-text uBASIC source files (DragRacer.txt and CatapultCastle.txt) for owners of the physical badge to load and run themselves.

Kojoe developed the games without having a physical Supercon 2018 badge on hand to test against, and the project page notes it was put on hold pending feedback from people who could actually try the scripts on real hardware. There is no indication the games were bundled with the badge, sold, or otherwise distributed beyond the Hackaday.io project page itself.
