The DEF CON 28 "Safe Mode" badge, issued for the pandemic-forced 2020 virtual conference, was a real, playable audio cassette tape rather than an electronic board. It shipped in a jewel case with shrink-wrap, the cassette itself molded in purple-tinted clear plastic with silk-screened logos and the number "28" on both sides. The package also included a lanyard printed with cryptic, possibly runic character strings and folded liner notes crediting The Dark Tangent and carrying puzzlemaster communications.

There were no electronics inside. Side A opened with decodable tones and side B with number-station-style sounds, both interspersed with music; a Ghost in the Shell quote appeared in the B-side liner notes. The audio, lanyard characters, and printed materials together formed a single collaborative puzzle that attendees solved on the DEF CON forums. Badge design is credited to veteran DEF CON badge creator LostboY, with the puzzle content authored by 1o57 -- both names for the same person, Ryan Clarke. DEF CON sold the badge through its official eBay storefront during the August 2020 virtual event, and copies still turn up on the secondary market; the full cassette audio is mirrored on the Internet Archive.

This entry duplicates an existing, previously researched archive entry for the same badge (`dc28-def-con-safe-mode-official-badge`), which credits LostboY/1o57 similarly but had no confirmed photograph; the two images here (a Hackaday hands-on piece) fill that gap.

## Notes merged from the duplicate entry "DEF CON Safe Mode Official Badge"

The DEF CON 28 "Safe Mode" badge, issued for the pandemic-forced 2020 virtual conference, broke from the electronic badgelife tradition entirely: it was a real analog audio cassette tape in a jewel case, designed by veteran DEF CON badge and Mystery Challenge creator LostboY (Ryan Clarke). The package included the cassette, a lanyard, and printed liner notes styled as a conference program.

There were no electronics inside. Instead, the badge carried a hidden, collaborative puzzle authored by 1o57 (also Ryan Clarke, DEF CON's puzzlemaster) spread across the tape's two audio sides, the printed liner notes, and lanyard character art -- description from a contemporary source characterizes the clues as track-list, color, character, tone, and number-station-style hints that attendees worked out together on the DEF CON forums. DEF CON sold the badge through its official eBay storefront during the virtual event, and copies still turn up on the secondary market.

No maker-published price or production quantity was found. No photo of the physical badge could be sourced and verified during this pass: DEF CON's own badge page only carries the Safe Mode event logo/ad art (not the item), and badge.gallery's writeup on this badge states it deliberately does not publish photos due to image-rights provenance.
