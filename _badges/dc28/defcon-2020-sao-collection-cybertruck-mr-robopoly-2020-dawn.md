This is a bundle of three separately-designed Shitty Add-Ons (SAOs) sold together by Harbinger LTD (trading as AwkwardAI on Tindie) for DEF CON 28 in 2020: a Cybertruck SAO, a "Mr. Robopoly 2020" SAO, and a "Dawn of the Karen" SAO. All three are fully assembled, use steady-on (non-animated) LEDs with hot glue used as a light diffuser, and connect to a host badge's SAO header rather than carrying their own microcontroller.

Mr. Robopoly is the best-documented of the three: it is a hacker-themed take on the Monopoly mascot, originally created for DEF CON 26 and a crowd favorite at DEF CON 27, and updated for 2020 to the SAO v1.69bis (6-pin) header with side-mounted red LEDs replacing the earlier reverse-mount yellow ones. It was also sold on its own, as a kit or fully assembled, for $10. The Cybertruck and Dawn of the Karen designs are less documented in sources found during this pass; the seller noted the bundle represented their last remaining Cybertruck stock and was nearly out of Dawn of the Karen units, while priced the whole three-SAO set at $45.

No hardware files, schematics, or firmware were found published for any of the three designs, and the Tindie listing is no longer accepting orders (the seller noted they were on a break as of this check).
