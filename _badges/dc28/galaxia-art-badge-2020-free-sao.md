The Galaxia Art Badge 2020 is an unofficial indie badge made by Harbinger LTD (the maker known as AwkwardAI) for DEF CON 28 in 2020. Rather than a general-purpose electronic badge, it's an art piece: a 555 timer and 4017 decade counter drive a chain of LEDs to perpetually animate a scene of an astronaut and a hacked satellite set against an intergalactic invasion, in the style of a novelty animated clock. A trimpot lets the wearer adjust the animation's speed. The badge runs on two CR2032 batteries and carries a SAO v1.69bis port so other add-ons can be plugged in.

Harbinger LTD sold the badge on Tindie for $30, bundling it with a "Mr. Robopoly" SAO add-on kit (a Mr. Robot/Monopoly Man mashup design that had been a running favorite in their earlier DEF CON badge lines), plus a lanyard, instructions, and stickers. The bundled SAO shipped as a bare board requiring hand assembly with hot glue. As of research, the Tindie listing noted the store was on a break and not accepting orders, so current availability is unclear.

Harbinger LTD / AwkwardAI has produced a series of indie badges and SAOs for multiple DEF CON years (including DC26 and DC27); this badge fits into that ongoing line, though no dedicated project log page or open-source hardware/firmware files specific to the 2020 Galaxia badge were found.
